01 · Overview

What is a Marketing Request?

A marketing request (called an Activity request in the system) is how you propose and get approval for a field marketing activity — a doctor visit, a hospital group meeting, a deal, a conference, and more.

📢
In simple words
You want to run an activity with a doctor, clinic, or hospital. You create a request in the system with the customer, the products, the cost, and a forecast of sales. Managers and departments approve it one by one. Then finance executes the payment and you track results.
Find it in your portal sidebar under MARKETING REQUESTS ↓

↑ The sidebar shows: Customer Validation · Marketing Requests · Approval Inbox · ROI Tracking

The 4 sections you will use:
✅ Customer Validation
Validate a customer and their pharmacies before you can use them in any request.
📋 Marketing Requests
Create your activity request, add products, set cost, and submit for approval.
📥 Approval Inbox
Where approvers see requests waiting for their decision. You also see it if you are a manager.
📊 ROI Tracking
After your request is approved and active, track whether real sales met your forecast.
02 · First Step

Customer Validation

This must be done before creating any activity request. The system only lets you select customers that have been validated. If your customer is not validated yet, start here.

⚠️
Important — do this before anything else
The CUSTOMER field in your request will be greyed out and disabled until you validate at least one customer. You cannot submit a request without a validated customer.

↑ Customer Validation page — stats at top: SYSTEM VALIDATED · SFE VALIDATED · PENDING · REJECTED

1
Click "Customer Validation" in the sidebar
You will land on the Customer Validation page. Click the Validate Customer button (orange). The form "Customer and pharmacy evidence" will open below it.
2
Fill in the Customer information
Complete all the fields shown in the CUSTOMER VALIDATION section:

↑ Customer fields — all GPS fields are filled automatically when you capture location

CUSTOMER TYPESelect from: Clinic, Doctor, Hospital, Account, or Other
CUSTOMER NAMEType the full name of the doctor, clinic, or hospital
AREA (GPS)Filled automatically from your GPS capture
GOVERNORATE (GPS)Filled automatically from your GPS capture
PHONEMobile number (optional)
ADDRESS (GPS)Full address — filled automatically from GPS capture
3
Capture the Customer GPS + Live Sign Photo
This is required. Look for the "Customer GPS + live sign photo" box. It will show LOCATION REQUIRED and LIVE PHOTO REQUIRED until you complete both.
📍 GPS button
Tap to capture your exact location. Area, Governorate, and Address fields fill automatically.
📷 Photo button
Take a live photo with a timestamp. This proves you were physically at the location.
✓
When done correctly
The two orange tags disappear and the box turns green, showing your coordinates and photo filename. The GPS and location fields are now filled automatically.
4
Fill in the Pharmacy Validation
Scroll down to the PHARMACY VALIDATION section. At least one pharmacy is required. Fill in Pharmacy 1 (more can be added with "+ Add Pharmacy").

↑ Pharmacy section — name, GPS capture, photo, and Vendor Codes with distributor dropdown

PHARMACY NAMEFull name of the pharmacy
AREA / GOVERNORATE / ADDRESS (GPS)All filled from GPS capture
Pharmacy GPS + live sign photoSame as customer — capture location + take live photo at the pharmacy
VENDOR CODESSelect your distributor (e.g. chimipharm) from the dropdown, then type the pharmacy's code with that distributor. Click "+ Add" for more distributors.
💡
What is a Vendor Code?
It is the pharmacy's unique code in your distributor's system (like chimipharm or IQVIA). The system checks this code to confirm the pharmacy is real and trackable. Without it, the validation cannot pass automatically.
5
Click "Save Validation"
When the bottom bar shows "1 pharmacy row ready" (or more), the Save Validation button becomes active. Click it to submit your evidence.
🤖
What happens next — System Validation
The system automatically checks: Is the GPS + photo complete? Does the vendor code match the reference database? If yes to both → the customer status becomes SYSTEM VALIDATED and you can immediately use them in a request. If something doesn't match, an SFE Manager can manually approve it → status becomes SFE VALIDATED.
6
Check the "Validated" tab to confirm
Click the Validated tab (next to Validate Customer). Your customer should now appear there with a SYSTEM VALIDATED or SFE VALIDATED status. You are now ready to create your activity request.
03 · Create a Request

Creating an Activity Request

Click Marketing Requests in the sidebar. The page shows the draft creation form and all your existing requests below it.

↑ Marketing Requests workspace — stats at top (DRAFTS · APPROVED · CUSTOMERS · PRODUCTS) and the form below

📌
The form is always visible
Unlike most systems, you don't need to click a separate button to open the form. The CREATE DRAFT — Activity request form is always shown on the Marketing Requests page. Just fill it in and click Save Draft.
1
Select Customer and Request Type
At the top of the CREATE DRAFT form:

↑ The CREATE DRAFT · Activity request form with all required fields

CUSTOMER Select from the dropdown. Only validated customers appear here. If the dropdown is empty or greyed out, go to Customer Validation first (Section 02).
REQUEST TYPE Choose the type of marketing activity.

↑ Request Type options: Special request · Hospital Group Meeting · Deal · RTD · Conference · Other

2
Set the dates and cost
Three fields on the same row:
REQUEST DATEThe date you're making this request. Defaults to today.
PAY DATEThe date you expect payment / the activity to be executed. Must be in the future.
ROI DURATION (MONTHS)How many months after execution to track actual sales performance. Default is 3.
ESTIMATED COST (EGP)The total budget you need for this activity in Egyptian Pounds.
3
Add Products
Under PRODUCTS (your assigned line is shown — e.g. "Line: Pedia"), add the products you will promote in this activity.
Select productPick from the dropdown. Only products from your assigned line appear.
QtyEnter the number of units you expect to sell as a result of this activity.
UNITFactory price per unit — filled automatically from the product catalogue.
VALUECalculated automatically: Qty × Unit price.
SHAREThis product's share of total expected sales value. Calculated automatically.
➕
Add more products
Click the Add button on the right to add another product row. You can add as many products as you need.
4
Add a Note (optional)
The NOTE field ("Optional request note") lets you add context for the approvers — why this activity is important, special circumstances, etc. It is not required but helps approvers decide faster.
5
Check the ROI Preview
Below the form, the ROI Preview section calculates automatically every time you save. You must understand these numbers before submitting.

↑ ROI Preview — EST. COST · REQUIRED · EXPECTED · COVERAGE shown live

The ROI Rule

Your expected sales from the activity must be at least 10 times what you spend. This is the Required ROI.

Expected Sales≥Estimated Cost × 10
EST. COST
What you entered
REQUIRED
Cost × 10 (minimum needed)
EXPECTED
Sum of all product values
COVERAGE
Expected ÷ Required × 100%
⚠️
Coverage must reach 100% or more
If Coverage is below 100%, the system will block submission. Increase your product quantities or add more products until Expected ≥ Required.
6
Click "Save Draft"
Click the Save Draft button on the right. Your request is saved in the Draft Rows section below. You can save as many times as you need — nothing is sent to anyone yet.
💾
Drafts are safe
Saving as draft does not notify anyone. Come back and edit anytime. The ROI Preview recalculates every time you save. When you're happy with everything, the next step is to submit for approval.
7
Submit for Approval
Once your draft has a validated customer, at least one product with quantity, a cost, and the ROI Coverage is 100% or more — the Submit option becomes available from the Draft Rows section. Click it to send your request into the approval chain.
🚀
After you submit
The request moves to Pending Approval. It is automatically assigned to your first approver. You will receive a notification at each stage. The status badge on your request will update as it moves through the chain.
04 · Approval Chain

How Approval Works

Once submitted, your request travels through a chain of approvers. Each approver sees it in their Approval Inbox.

↑ Approval Inbox — where managers and reviewers see requests waiting for their decision

🧑‍💼
Step 1 — Management Chain
Starts with your direct manager and moves up the hierarchy until it reaches the National Sales Manager. Each manager in the chain decides before it moves on.
✓ Approve
↩ Return for Changes
✕ Reject
📋
Step 2 — SFE Review
The SFE Manager reviews the activity plan, product targets, and ROI. Product Managers are notified (CC only — they don't block the flow).
✓ Approve
↩ Return for Changes
✕ Reject
📧 Product Managers (CC)
📣
Step 3 — Marketing Review
The Marketing Manager reviews alignment with brand strategy and ongoing campaigns.
✓ Approve
↩ Return for Changes
✕ Reject
👔
Step 4 — CEO Final Approval
The CEO gives the final sign-off before the request goes to finance.
✓ Approve
↩ Return for Changes
✕ Reject
💳
Step 5 — Finance Execution
Finance processes the payment and marks the request as Active. ROI tracking begins from this point.
✓ Execute (becomes Active)
↩ Return for Changes
✕ Reject
↩
If your request is Returned
The approver will leave a comment explaining what to change. Open the request, read the comment, make the edits, recalculate the ROI if needed, then click Resubmit. The request resumes from the same step — you do not start over from Step 1.

06 · Status Reference

What Each Status Means

Every request has a status badge. Here is what each one means and what you should do.

Draft
Draft
You started the request but haven't submitted it yet. Only you can see it.
→ Complete the form and submit when ready.
Pending Approval
Pending Approval
In the approval chain — one of the approvers is currently reviewing it.
→ Wait. You'll receive a notification when there is a decision.
Returned
Returned
An approver sent it back to you with a note. Something needs to change.
→ Read the return comment, edit the request, then click Resubmit.
Approved
Approved
All approval steps are complete. Waiting for Finance to execute the payment.
→ No action needed from you. Finance will process it.
Active
Active
Finance has processed the payment. The activity is now running.
→ Execute the activity. ROI tracking has started.
ROI Tracking
ROI Tracking
Activity done. The system monitors real pharmacy sales vs. your forecast for the ROI window.
→ Check your ROI Tracking page for actual sales performance.
Closed
Closed
The ROI tracking window ended. Request is complete and archived.
→ View only. No further action.
Rejected
Rejected
An approver permanently rejected the request. It cannot be resubmitted.
→ Read the rejection reason. Create a new request if needed.

Before You Can Submit

  • Customer must be System Validated or SFE Validated
  • At least 1 product with a quantity
  • Estimated Cost (EGP) filled in
  • ROI Coverage must be 100% or more
  • Pay Date must be set

Key Rules to Remember

  • Required ROI = Estimated Cost × 10
  • Products are locked after submission
  • Rejected = permanent — create a new request
  • Returned = resume from same step, not from start
  • ROI Preview recalculates every time you Save Draft
