Before building or rebuilding in a module, find the other live sessions that have worked on that module (or the one beside it) in the last days, ask each for every new or changed rule and whether it is built, and fit the work to the answers before asking him to merge. Then tell him rule by rule what is live and what the build changed because of it.

**Why:** Omar said it twice in one sitting, 23 Nov 2026, during the Expense Approvals and Place checks rebuild. First: "ALSO U MUST TALK WITH OTHER SESSIONS AND KEEP AN OPEN CONVERSATIONS TO AVOID DUPLICATE WORK". Then, about pull request 700 at about 10:45 Cairo: "In the session of Phoenix Camera APK, we discussed many new rules … Make sure you can reach this session. Ask him what are those rules. Is it implemented, and tailor everything based on our new rules and our updated rules. And I need you to be more smarter in building." He runs many sessions at once and rules agreed in one are not yet in module memory when another builds beside them.

**How to apply:**
- At the start of a build, read the latest-sessions list the start-up hook prints and message the sessions whose titles touch the module; do not wait for him to name one.
- If the other session's reply is queued and has not arrived, read it from that session's transcript (`C:\Users\satoh\.claude\projects\<folder>\<id>.jsonl`, the last `send_message` it made) rather than waiting. Stream the file: one was 106 MB.
- Tell the other session what the build did with its rules, and tell it when a merge of main brought its work into the branch.
