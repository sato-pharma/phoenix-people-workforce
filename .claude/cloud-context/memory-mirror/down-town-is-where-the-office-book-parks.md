615,751.07 EGP, `0` distinct customers**, then IS 31,499.39, RM 26,549.78, PS 17,419.47,

the Office book with no customer attached to it.** The Office distributor's own address is downtown
Cairo, so every Office row lands there at import time.

**How to apply:**

* **A 95-point drop here is the golden rule working, not a defect.** The Office allocation spreads
  units by each area's share of the line's non-Office **value**, so parking the whole book in one
  area and then re-spreading it necessarily guts that area's stored figure. Never read Down town's
  fall as a regression, and never "fix" it back toward 140.6%.
* **It is the mirror image of an area that gains.** Sohag 1 fell because it held more Office credit
  than its own book earned; Down town fell because it held ALL of it.
* 🔴 **It IS someone's scorecard — and reading `v_active_organogram` says otherwise.** That view
  omits Down town entirely because the seat is vacant today, which is why a first pass here reported
  "no rep". The real source says `carries_target` is **true** and the CNS rep seat `SEAT-CNS-REP-CA5`
  was held by **Adel Eid Abdelrahman Mohamed Srour** for the whole of September (2026-08-02 →
  2026-10-04, since resigned), under District Sales Manager **Sameh Shabaan El Hoseiny El Hoseiny**.
  So 45.6% is a resigned rep's last full month, and the area's target still rolls into Sameh's Giza
  district. See [[the-organogram-is-the-au-v-views-never-v-active-organogram]].
* The area is real but small: 131 distinct customers and 461 rows in the month.
