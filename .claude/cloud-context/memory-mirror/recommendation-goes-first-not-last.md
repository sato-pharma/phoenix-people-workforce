**His words, 2026-09-07:** *"okkay but put ur recommendation first"*

## The rule

The single recommended next step goes **at the top of the reply**. Everything else — findings,
evidence, tables, what shipped — comes after it.

This **amends** the standing rule in `AGENTS.md` §4 ("How to answer"), which said to *end* every reply
with it. The count is unchanged: still exactly **one** recommendation, still never padded to a number.
Only its position moved.

## Why he asked

He reads on a phone, at night, across long sessions, and he is the only person who can act on the
recommendation. Burying it under a full audit means he has to scroll past evidence he has already
decided to trust in order to reach the one line that asks something of him. The findings are support
for the recommendation, so they belong underneath it.

## How to apply

* Open with the recommendation. Name the decision or action, and who has to make it.
* Then the substance, longest-lived first.
* Do **not** also repeat it at the bottom — one recommendation, one place. Repeating it is the failure
  mode this rule creates, because the old habit reasserts itself at the end of a long reply.
* **Amended 2026-09-15 (owner, "it keeps me in a loop"):** finish the work first. Never invent an
  action, a re-check or a "continue" to fill the line — that half stands in full.
* **Amended 2026-09-18 (owner, "always put something in the box"):** the line ALWAYS ends in
  curly-quoted words he could send as-is. The no-quotes `nothing waits on you` form is **retired** —
  it left his input box reading `<no suggestion>` turn after turn, and he ruled against it after
  watching it happen. A reply that asks nothing of him says what finished and then quotes the next
  REAL action, named with its real target. See [[fix-means-fix-no-unrequested-work]] for the
  boundary: the quoted action is the next real move, never work invented to fill the box.
  The user `CLAUDE.md`, `settings.json`, the `next-step` skill and `next-step-capture.mjs` all carry
  the current form; `AGENTS.md` §4 in the repo still carries the old one.
