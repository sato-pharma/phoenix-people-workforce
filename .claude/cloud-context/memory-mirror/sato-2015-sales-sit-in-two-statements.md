Found 13 October 2026 while building the company profile from `F:\Dr Anwar Attachments`.

- `2015-07/…حساب ساتو فارما شهر يونية 2015.pdf` is headed "مبيعات مستحضرات شركة SATO PHARMA". Its only product is Clerovate Cream 25 g, made under toll (batch B-1550591). Net sales were 59,937.13 pounds.
- `2015-10/…sep -2015 sato promotion.pdf` is headed "عمولة ساتوفارم من 2015/09/01 حتى 2015/09/30". It shows total sales of 283,028.94 pounds, a 30% commission of 84,908.68 pounds, and a net due of 80,663.25 pounds. The 283,029 is the sales figure; the 30% line is the commission.
- `2015-08/…Copy of Company detailing plan.xlsx`, "Detailing plan" sheet, has a "Target/M.Rep" column that divides each brand's monthly target by 28. That means about 28 medical reps in mid-2015. It lists 11 promoted brands.

**Why:** a profile or analysis that takes the first statement as company sales makes 2015 look about five times smaller than it was. That inflates the then-versus-now growth.

**How to apply:** for 2015 sales, quote about 0.3 million pounds a month, which adds both statements. Never quote 59,937 alone. Pounds from 2015 are not inflation-adjusted, so lead growth claims with packs and people.
