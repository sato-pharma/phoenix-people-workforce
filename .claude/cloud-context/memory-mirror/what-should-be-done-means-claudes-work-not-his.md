When he asks "what should be done" / "so what should be done" after an audit or a finding, he is asking about **Claude's work**: the code, guard, data fix or memory change Claude will make. Answer by doing that work in the same turn. Never hand him a list of steps in his own HR or payroll screens.

**Why:** on 2026-10-22, after the Shimaa Ibrahim / Ahmed Nabil audit, he asked "so what should be done" twice. The second answer listed Employee Master and payroll corrections for him to make. He replied: "Don't fucking tell me what to do in my work. I'm asking you about your fucking work."

**Once he has explained a person's case, stop naming that person.** The same day, after he had said Ahmed Nabil moved to Pedia, I proposed checking the new warning on "Ahmed Nabil's Edit dates" and he answered: "I don't care about fucking Ahmed Nabil. He resigned." Verify a feature on any record that proves it, silently, and report the feature, not the employee.

**How to apply:** lead with what you are building, then build it. Mention a correction that is genuinely his (money, approvals) in one line at most, as a fact about where it sits, never as instructions.
