The attendance fingerprint device is a Head Office concept. Field staff (Medical Representatives, District/Regional/Senior managers) have no fingerprint mapping BY DESIGN - their attendance comes from the CRM (iReports360), see [[e360-attendance-source-field-not-device]].

**Why:** on 2026-10-12, matching unknown device user 50, I listed five field hires (a Medical Rep and four District Sales Managers) as candidates and closed the reply with "map the new hires to their fingerprint device users". The owner answered "the attendance fingerprint punch only suitable for head office employee ... refer to ur memory". Those candidates were never valid.

**How to apply:** when asked to identify or map a device user id, filter candidates to non-field job titles only (Head Office: HR, finance, regulatory, R&D, talent acquisition and similar). If no Head Office hire fits the punch dates, say so rather than widening to field staff. Never put "enrol/map the new hires" in a Next step. An employee with no active mapping is NOT a gap when they are field staff (70 Medical Reps and 19 DSMs on 2026-10-12 had none, correctly). See also [[attendance-eligibility-device-mapped]].
