🔒 GOLDEN RULE (owner, 4 November 2026, Cairo, after he had said it "many times"): **the Training Manager is
NOT responsible for product or medical knowledge.** Training the field on the products, the diseases, the key
messages and the product test all belong to the **Product Manager** (job post 16 October 2026: "Field force
enablement: train the sales force on the products…").

The Training Manager owns selling skills only: field coaching and double visits, the Coaching Skills Survey,
the selling-skills induction and probation follow-up, teaching district managers to coach, training impact and
the training plan.

**Why:** I put a "Product knowledge training" competency on the Training Manager's competency page; he rejected
it as a trap he had already warned about repeatedly.

**How to apply:** in any job description, competency framework, org chart, training plan, report or Phoenix
screen, never give the Training Manager product or medical content. Avoid "key message" or "disease" wording on
Training Manager material. Route product knowledge to the Product Manager. Related: [[the-training-manager-chair-reports-to-the-ceo]].
