The owner, 13 October 2026, on the Sato Pharma company profile: "don't … make … many speaking,
make graphs, charts, comparisons, catchy, you know?"

**Why:** the profile is for branding: LinkedIn, the website and new hires. Readers scan it, they
don't read it.

**How to apply:** give each page one big visual idea, such as a chart to scale, a then-versus-now
comparison or large figures. Keep the words to a headline, a one- or two-line lead and short labels.
Inside the HR premium identity, draw charts with keylines, diamond markers and an engraved field
rendered as an image, never solid filled bars (the ink rule). See [[hr-premium-identity]].

**Tone, same evening:** "try to be actual. … we are not big, but we want to make them feel this, but don't tell it like this." The cover headline "Small start. Big company." was rejected for "Twelve years, step by step." Use factual, modest words, and let the scale of the numbers do the persuading.

**People = the full structure, not the payroll count** (same evening: "don't put the actual count,
put the total structure"). On 13 October 2026 that is 151: 136 field seats plus 15 at Head Office.
- The field seats are 3 national managers, 1 regional manager, 22 district managers and 110 reps,
  filled or open, from `au_v_seat_occupancy_all`.
- The Head Office 15 is 14 people plus 1 open post in `position_vacancies`.
- 🔴 `au_v_org_structure_kpis` also counts each line's "Others" holding bucket (`SEAT-*-OTHERS`) as
  a vacant district-manager seat. Leave those buckets out.

**Money in EGP, never in dollars** (same evening, on a page 3 bar that read "about 20 times in US
dollars": "use egp never dollar"). Every money figure in a branding piece is in Egyptian pounds.
Never convert to dollars, never add a dollar comparison, not even as a footnote to temper a growth claim.
