The work email on the Employee Master (and the organogram) is the address. Never change it, never pick a
different one because the mailbox is missing, deleted or taken — create (or restore) the Turbify mailbox at
exactly that address, and keep sending to it even while the mailbox does not exist yet.

**Why:** owner, 2026-11-23, after medical cover statements bounced for joiners whose mailboxes had been
deleted in the Turbify console: "stick to the email that is in the employee master or organogram. Don't
change it. If it is not created, but stick to it."

**How to apply:** for any bounce or missing mailbox, the fix is on the mail side (create/restore at the
Employee Master address), never an Employee Master edit. A deleted address shows "was recently deleted —
restore" in the console's Add email users form; restore keeps the same address. Related: [[never-fire-bulk-emails-ever]].
