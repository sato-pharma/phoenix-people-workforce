🔒 **THE NDA — the confidentiality undertaking — STAYS HAND-STAMPED. Owner's ruling, 2026-09-29:
"leave the nda hand-stamped".** Do not place a stamp or signature mark on it, and do not offer to.

**Why, measured rather than argued.** Its signing cells are `12mm`
(`.doc-sig__cell` default; it has no `.copy--undertaking` override, unlike
`.copy--certificate` at 36mm and `.copy--salaryLetter` at 22mm). The company seal is **34mm**. The
paper is already nearly full at 12mm, so giving the seal room turns a one-page document into a
two-page one — and the NDA prints **2 copies**, so that is two extra sheets for every new hire.

Rendered through Chromium at five heights, before and after:

| cell height | pages per copy |
| --- | --- |
| 12mm (today) | **1** |
| 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36mm | 2 |

**So there is no height that fits.** Even +4mm spills. The options put to the owner were: leave it
hand-stamped · a ~12mm seal that fits the existing cell · accept two pages · shorten the NDA's legal
text. He chose the first, which is also the right one — the NDA is signed in person on a new hire's
first day with both parties present, so a real stamp costs nothing and a digital one buys nothing.

**Its shape differs from the certificates, which is why it looks like it should work and does not.**
The middle column is headed `التوقيع والختم` — signature AND stamp in ONE box, where the certificate
gives the seal a column of its own — and there are two party rows, `الطرف الأول — الشركة` and
`الطرف الثاني — المُقِر`. So both marks would share one 12mm cell beside the employee's own signature.

**Why:** re-deriving this costs five Chromium renders and lands on the same answer, and the ruling
is the owner's rather than a technical limit to be engineered around.

**How to apply:** if asked to stamp the NDA, say it was measured and ruled on, and offer the real
alternatives rather than quietly shrinking the seal. Related: [[the-house-document-identity-standard]],
and the print-time choice that governs every other paper's marks.
