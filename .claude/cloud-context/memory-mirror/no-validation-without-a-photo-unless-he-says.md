A validation must have a photo unless the owner says otherwise in his own words for that case ("no no, must have a photo except i tell u", 20 Nov 2026 ~09:40 Cairo).

**Why:** asked to "support" Saad Salama's team on Place checks, I validated 70 of their waiting customers on the Super Admin's trusted-point route (no photo) on my own reading; he stopped it mid-run and everything was undone (pre-image and undo tables in `phoenix_crm_backup_20261120`, `saad_team_trusted_customers` / `trusted_undo_*`).

**How to apply:** "support them", "help them", "validate as many as you can" never authorises the trusted point or any other no-photo route — only taking back a refusal that has the rep's own photo, or work on the photo the rep sent. Offer the no-photo route only as a multiple-choice option he picks. Related: [[omar-approvals-restored-2026-11-20]].
