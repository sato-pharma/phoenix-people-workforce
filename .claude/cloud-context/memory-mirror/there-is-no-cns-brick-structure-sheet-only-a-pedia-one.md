  **"Pedia line bricks refernece.xlsx"**, filed 9 May 2026 under Yehya Mohamed Kamal eldeen Saad

  carries exactly one `area_code`.

  The **five** that do carry one are طهطا, جهينه, طما, شطورة and اولاد الياس (block **B137**), and
  each one's `source_area_code` **agrees** with its current area. So the distributors' sheets do

  carry "no Sohag brick has a source area" forward as a fact.

**Why it matters:** a brick is the heaviest object in the sales map. B213-1 مدينة سوهاج carries
**770,201.63 EGP of September 2026 CNS — 91% of Sohag 1 — of which 551,977.57 is Office (Marcyrl)
credit in 10 rows**, plus one EGD Hospital row of 82,931.10, and 1,582 active customer mappings.

area's rep. So "the brick looks wrong" is never self-justifying.
