Audited 2026-08-13. "The semester-1 incentive" is not one thing; it is three, and a request
about it can mean any of them:

**1. The LOCKED payout curve** — `src/lib/incentives/scheme-2026.ts` (protected, owner-approval
to edit). Semester incentive = **2 months of Basic Salary**; Basic = 70% of Base (via
`src/lib/payroll/policy.ts`, also locked). Curve on achievement %: `<90` → **0%** (hard floor);
`90` → 50%; `91–100` → +5%/pt; `101–120` → +1.5%/pt; `>120` → +3%/pt; achievement ceiling
**150%**, payout capped **220%**. Corners: 90→50, 100→100, 120→130, 150→220. Continuous and
self-consistent; `tests/incentive-scheme.test.ts` pins the corners (e.g. 150% on 90k net →
220% → 277,200 EGP). No defect in the formula.

**2. The in-repo S1 Business Review** — `src/lib/business-review-s1/`. This is the H1-2026
analysis engine (built outside the repo, see [[h1-2026-incentive-sales-analysis-engine]])
**productionized into the app as STATIC bundled JSON** — `data/{pedia,cns,anti,company}-s1.json`
+ `access-map.json`. **Read-only: no DB, no writes.** Regenerated by `ingest/*.py` +
`build_access_map_from_db.mjs`, not live. It honours the per-line grain rule, and company achievement is
never an average of line %s — but the formula recorded here as correct, `sum(sales)/sum(target)`,
**is the defect**: the numerator must be the COVERED actual. See
[[achievement-numerator-is-covered-actual-not-all-sales]]. The bundled S1 figures below were computed
that way, so treat them as what the old static page displayed, not as the semester's real grade. Headline S1 figures baked
in the bundle (2026-08-13):
- Company: 55.23M / 93.50M = **59.07%** ach (ex-vacant 63.05%); 94 reps, 129 areas (50 vacant);
  8,834 unique doctors. 59.07% is **below the 90% floor → zero payout at company level.**
- Pedia 60.76% (incl-only 63.5%, ex-vacant incl-only 85.55%), 33 reps.
- CNS 59.02% (ex-vacant 57.61%), 35 reps.
- Anti-Infective 55.84% (ex-vacant 71.09%), 26 reps.
- Shared thresholds: rep fail <20%, DSM fail <50%.
- Access: admin page (`app/(admin)/business-review-s1/`) = **CEO / SFE Manager / Super Admin only**;
  employee portal = scoped by SEAT MAP (`portal-scope.ts`), Medical Reps have no access by design,
  denies on any access-map-vs-live-session role mismatch.

**3. The LIVE portal incentive tab** — `src/lib/employee-portal/service.ts` +
`src/lib/incentives/semester.ts`. Computes each rep's achievement over a **DECLARED** semester
window (a fixed set of `YYYY-MM` keys anchored on the rep's latest data), NOT `slice(-6)` — the
old slice gave different-length semesters to different reps and could straddle the S1/S2 boundary.
Feeds that achievement into the layer-1 locked curve. `describeIncompleteWindow` marks a payout
"Provisional" while a semester month is missing or still to come.

**Why:** the owner asked to audit the semester-1 incentive; the figures in layer 2 are what reps
are reviewed against, and the outside-repo memory numbers differ slightly because placement
rulings/re-imports landed since (the bundle is the current productionized truth).

**How to apply:** clarify WHICH layer a "semester-1 incentive" request means. Never hand-edit the
locked curve or the bundled JSON's headline numbers to "fix" a figure — regenerate via the ingest
scripts. Company 59.07% paying nothing is correct, not a bug. Never re-aggregate area across lines
(see [[h1-2026-incentive-sales-analysis-engine]]).
