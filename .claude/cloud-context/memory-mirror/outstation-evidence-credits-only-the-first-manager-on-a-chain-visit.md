On 2026-10-08 the owner ruled that a Phoenix CRM chain visit (a rep submits one visit naming two managers) is ONE visit for the rep and sits in BOTH managers' records — Double Visit Analytics, coaching audit, Employee 360, private calendar, probation, Field Activity, compliance (PR #5499).

The same day he ruled the one exception: **outstation trip workload evidence stays on the FIRST manager only** ("keep the outstation evidence on the first manager only").

**Why:** money is decided on outstation evidence; the chain-visit ruling was about visit records, not allowances, and he chose not to widen it.

**How to apply:** never "fix" the outstation evidence reader to credit `double2` / the second chain manager to match the reports. A change there moves money and is his decision on its own screen, never a chat yes. Related: [[old-crm-two-row-chain-visits-stay-as-history]].
