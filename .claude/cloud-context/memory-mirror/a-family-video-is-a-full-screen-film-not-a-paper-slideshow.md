He rated a birthday video for his uncle 2/10 (2026-11-02). It was prints landing on cream paper, small type and a generated music track. Asked what to change first, he chose all three of: full-screen film, replace the music, add his own voice. He then asked for his wording to be rephrased and improved.

**Why:** He had asked for something "فوق الفشيخ". The "flat colour, restraint" design rule is about not looking AI-made. It does not mean a still, quiet page when the job is an emotional video.

**How to apply:** For a personal video, start with full-bleed photos, real camera movement, cuts on the beat and very large type. A generated track can't be checked by ear, so never treat it as the finished music. Polish his dictated wording unasked and show him the final text.

**The person is the point (2026-11-02):** He told me to "make sure sa3dody face is appearing in all photos". Pans and centre crops had carried the face out of frame, and words sat over it in strips near the bottom. Before framing anything, write down where that person's face is in every photo. Keep it in frame for the whole move and above the words, then measure its screen position at each shot's start, middle and end.

**The music (2026-11-02):** He dropped his voice from this film and told me to find a song on the web. He rejected the Arabic birthday tune ("no not a sana helwa ya gameel"), so don't assume the obvious birthday song. Use only a track the site offers as a free download, read its licence on the track page, and carry any required credit in the film. Measure the song bar by bar, then look for a straight stretch whose own lifts land on the film's moments. A stretch with no edits can't have a bad seam, which matters because I can't hear the result. Measure the cut audio every time: an output `-ss` with `afade` gave a silent file and only the loudness read showed it (trim with `atrim` first).

**The polish (2026-11-02):** "Premium" came from one colour grade matched to his skin, serif type, cuts instead of whips, and plain words instead of photos inside letters. He then took out the one screen with no photo ("أنا بحبك حب جنوني"), so keep the person on screen throughout and give a freed slot to another photo of them. Change only the seconds he corrects and splice them in by frame number, because a full render takes 15 minutes and leaves the song's sync untouched. Fix a word by his exact wording ("أحلى أسرة", "عمر جاويش").
