`src/lib/sales/office-distributor.ts` records his 2026-10-09 ruling — **"leave the Office respread at
October"** — together with the two facts it was put to him with: that September CNS was not published
(only September Anti Infective was, and that line carries no Office channel), and that September held
**zero Office rows** because its file had not been uploaded. **Both inverted the same evening, and the
comment still reads as current state:**

- **September's Office book landed at 20:10:51 Cairo** — `Marcyrl 9.xlsx`, validated, 3,920 rows,
  80,565 units, 6,971,179.95 EGP. So September now has stored Office geography, stamped on a base
  still missing epda and mec.
- **September has no active lock at all.** Anti Infective was unlocked at **19:12:19 Cairo**, so all
  three September lines are unfrozen (July and August stay locked, three lines each). The frozen-net
  objection in that comment — publishing one line freezes the period — no longer applies to September.

**Why:** the constant is `OFFICE_RESPREAD_START_PERIOD = "2026-10"`, pinned by
`tests/closing-office-respread.test.ts` as "the owner's start month", and the comment says it does not
move "without his own new word about September". A session reading that comment in isolation will
believe the two reasons still hold and will not know the ground moved.

**How to apply:** when September's Office geography comes up, state both inversions as measured facts
and let him rule again; never move the constant on a session's own reading. And never offer a "re-run"
as the remedy — see [[there-is-no-re-run-path-for-the-office-allocation]].
