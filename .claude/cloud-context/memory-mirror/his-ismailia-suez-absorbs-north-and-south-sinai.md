his closing **folds the outlying areas into their nearest reported area**, and a comparison that
does not fold them the same way reports a false gap.

Proven 2026-08-11 from the Ismailia-Suez customer list he supplied (169 pharmacies with codes).

| Phoenix area | shops | July CNS units |

So the comparison map needs **`OT1 -> OT9` and `OT10 -> OT9`** alongside the merges already known:
`UE9 -> GZ6` (Beni Suef into Fayoum), `OT2 -> OT9` (Suez into Ismailia), `OT7 -> OT6` (Aswan into
Luxor). Adding the two Sinai folds took CNS July's area error **2,211 → 1,944** and removed Ismailia
from the gap list entirely — no data moved, the comparison was simply wrong.

**Why:** the earlier "areas your sheet has no row for" bucket (Hurgada, Red Sea, New Valley, North
Sinai, South Sinai — 316 units) is mostly this same effect, not a coverage hole. Do not "fix" it by
moving customers.

reports it at all; if not, find which of his areas absorbs it and fold before comparing. Ask him for
the customer list — he supplies it readily and it settles the question in one pass. Still unresolved:
