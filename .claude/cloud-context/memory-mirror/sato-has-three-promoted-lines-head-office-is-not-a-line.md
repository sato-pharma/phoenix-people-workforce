The owner, 13 October 2026, correcting a company-profile outline that said "four lines": "we are
not four lines. We are three lines, but the others are … products non-promoted by the team, like …
the Lacto."

**Why:** `source_of_sales_products.line` holds "Head Office" as if it were a line, so counting lines
from the data gives four. The business has three: no rep team promotes the Head Office products.

**How to apply:** in profiles, decks, reports and summaries, write "three promoted lines". Name the
Head Office products as non-promoted products. Their sales still count in company totals; see
[[head-office-sales-are-absent-from-phx-monthly-achievement]] for their value.
