Asked in chat on 2026-09-15 whether a typed Addition in the Automated Payroll wizard (Part 2 Additions: incentive, bonus, overtime) should have a limit — flag above it, refuse above it, or no limit. **The owner chose: no limit.** HR types any figure; nothing flags or refuses it.

**Why:** his decision; it closes a question parked since the Automated Payroll audit campaign (see [[a-payroll-rule-change-does-not-reach-an-already-open-run]]).

**How to apply:** do not add an Additions ceiling, a "large addition" warning, or re-propose one in an audit finding. Standing additions pre-fill (#3804) is a separate feature and is unaffected.
