## Rules

_These five rules moved here verbatim from `platform` on 2026-11-13, so that module's pointer stays under its margin. `platform` keeps every glob it had, so a file under `src/lib/ai/` loads both._

- 🔴 **ANTHROPIC IS THE SECOND AI PROVIDER BEHIND THE ONE SHARED CALL, `createOpenAiResponseText` — ASK `hasAiApiKey()` WHETHER AI IS AVAILABLE, NEVER `hasOpenAiApiKey()`** (owner, 2026-10-18, the OpenAI account failed to recharge). It fails over to `src/lib/ai/anthropic-fallback.ts` ONLY on `quota_exceeded` or `auth_failed`, with what is LEFT of the caller's timeout, then skips OpenAI for 10, 20, 40, then 60 minutes until OpenAI answers once. 🔴 Out of credit is `isOpenAiOutOfCredit(code, type)`, never the code alone (`credit_balance_exhausted` under type `insufficient_quota`); `AI_PROVIDER=openai|anthropic` pins one. Plain `fetch`, no SDK; the caller's effort and `json_schema` go as `output_config` (measured 18 Oct: Haiku answered a prompt-only schema with the schema), and a 400 naming it or refusing the schema is resent once without. `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set on Railway BY HAND, NOT in `sync-railway-production-secrets`' list (the edit was refused 2026-10-18); `.railway/railway.ts` was not touched. 🔴 **A SECOND ANTHROPIC ACCOUNT, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY_2`, IS THE THIRD STEP** (owner, 2026-10-24: OpenAI → Anthropic account 1 → Anthropic account 2): `callAnthropicMessages` moves to it only on `quota_exceeded`/`auth_failed`, tries the refusing account last for 10 minutes, and records the leg as `failed_over` from `anthropic` to `anthropic_backup`; set it by hand on BOTH services that hold the first key. The three `src/lib/sales/**` AI paths fail over through `planSalesAi`/`runWithSalesAiFailover`; a new sales AI path never posts to a chat endpoint itself. Account: `docs/environment.md`, "AI". Pinned by `tests/ai-provider-failover.test.ts` and `tests/sales-ai-failover.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **ONE DOOR FOR EVERY MODULE — NO MODULE BUILDS ITS OWN OPENAI CLIENT, POSTS TO A PROVIDER ADDRESS OR READS A KEY** (owner, 2026-10-24: "make sure they use same rule"). The OpenAI SDK lives only in `src/lib/ai/openai-client.ts`; the OpenAI and Anthropic addresses and the key reads only in `src/lib/ai/` (OpenAI's also in the sales chain, `src/lib/sales/ai/provider.ts`); Groq and OpenRouter only in `groq-transcription.ts` (speech, which Claude cannot do) and the sales chain. A side door answers until OpenAI runs dry, then fails alone without ever reaching either Anthropic account. Claude reads JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP only: `anthropicImageMediaType` sniffs the bytes and a HEIC photo gets a plain refusal, never a 400. Pinned by `tests/ai-one-door.test.ts` (sweeps `src` and `app`, plants six breaches). A refused key (`auth_failed`, 401, 403) pages his Telegram once a day per account, like an empty account (`ai-quota-alert.ts`); the sales chain gives Claude only the time its budget has left (`runWithSalesAiFailover`); and no single AI key is required on production, only any one of the three (`checkAiKeyPresent` in `scripts/secret-policy.mjs`).
- 🔴 **AN AI ADDRESS IS WRITTEN OUT IN THE CODE, NEVER READ FROM A VARIABLE** (integration audit, 13 Nov 2026, ISS-041). Until then `AI_BASE_URL` replaced the address of every sales AI leg, OpenAI's and Groq's included, and two legs no environment had ever set (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`; `AI_API_KEY` + `AI_BASE_URL`) could send sales rows to any host. `resolveAiProvider` (`src/lib/sales/ai/provider.ts`) now returns `api.openai.com` or `api.groq.com` and nothing else; the names `openrouter` and `generic` stay in the types only because stored AI-call rows carry them. Never add a base-address variable back. Pinned by `tests/integration-audit-edges.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A LEG THE NEXT ACCOUNT IS ASKED TO COVER IS REPORTED AS `failed_over`, NEVER AS AN AI FAILURE** (owner, 2026-10-23). `AiCallStatus`'s third member, set by `legOutcome` (shared client) and `salesLegOutcome` (the three sales paths) only when there IS a next account AND `shouldFailOverToNextProvider` says the code moves to it — a timeout or a rate limit stays a failure with the account that gave it. It takes its own route (`/ai/<component>/<purpose>/failed-over`) and its own log event (`openai_failed_over`, `anthropic_failed_over`), still pages the out-of-credit alarm, and carries its status in `failureThrottleKey`. 🔴 **Before it, a dead OpenAI account wrote 520 `openai_request_failed` rows in a week plus 21 "account out of credit" rows against CV extraction, for 58 CV reads Claude had answered in full** — read as an outage by a session sent to fix it. Account: `docs/environment.md`, "A leg the next account is asked to cover".
- 🔴 **THE OUT-OF-CREDIT ALARM NAMES THE ACCOUNT THE CALL WAS BILLED TO — EVERY `recordAiCall` CARRIES `provider`, NEVER INFERRED FROM THE FEATURE** (code review, 2026-10-19: one feature reaches two accounts since the failover, so a Claude account out of credit paged him to top up OpenAI, and one alarm a day for all accounts swallowed the second). `ai-quota-alert.ts` sends once a Cairo day FOR EACH account (`takeAiQuotaAlertSlot`); OpenAI keeps its old idempotency key. Anthropic's spend limit is a 400 naming "usage limits" and is `quota_exceeded` (`anthropicErrorCodeFor`). Pinned by `tests/ai-failure-visibility.test.ts` and `tests/ai-provider-failover.test.ts`. Account: `docs/environment.md`, "The out-of-credit alarm names the account".
