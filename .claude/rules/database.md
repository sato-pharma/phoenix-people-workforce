### The migration ledger

- 🔴 **A MISSING LEDGER ROW IS ANSWERED BY `md5(pg_proc.prosrc)` AGAINST THE FILE'S OWN BODY — NEVER BY WHETHER THE OBJECT EXISTS**, because almost every one is `create or replace` / `if not exists`, so an OLDER generation passes a catalog probe exactly as well as the current one (all 10 production misses "existed" on 2026-10-08; two were superseded and re-applying either would have REVERTED production). 🔴 **COMPARE THE FILE'S BODY IN FOUR SHAPES BEFORE CALLING A DIGEST MISMATCH DRIFT — as-is LF, as-is CRLF, comment-stripped LF, comment-stripped CRLF** — because some apply routes normalise the text they send: production's `prosrc` for `20261012132430`'s three deduction functions carries **CRLF**, and two of them arrived with every full-line `--` comment removed, so a raw md5 read as three drifted bodies when all three were byte-faithful (4,102 vs 4,191 chars, 2,462 vs 2,508 — a delta of a few dozen characters is the tell). Accounts: `docs/migrations.md`, "The 15 October production sweep" (the four shapes, measured), and `docs/security/migration-ledger-reconciliation-2026-10-08.md` (the ten misses that established the digest rule).
