### PostgREST filter strings

- 🔴 **A VALUE SPLICED INTO A POSTGREST FILTER COMES FROM `src/lib/supabase/search-filter.ts`, OR ITS LINE IS A WRITTEN-DOWN EXEMPTION.** A bare `` `hr_id.ilike.${value}` `` lets a comma, a dot, a `)` or a `*` in the value rewrite the whole `or(...)` tree, so one id widens a read to every row — use `quotedOrClause`, `ilikeAnyFilter`, `ilikeContainsValue`, `ilikeExactValue` or `searchableTerm`, never a second local quoting helper and never a widened regex instead. 🔴 **An `in.(…)` MEMBER LIST is quoted PER MEMBER through `quotedInList`** — there a `,` separates MEMBERS, so quoting the list as one value is as wrong as leaving it bare, in both of its shapes. `tests/postgrest-search-filter.test.ts` sweeps every tracked `src`/`app` file in two passes and keys each exemption by file plus the exact trimmed line, so EDITING an exempt line loses its exemption; `.gte.` and `.is.` are out of both. Census, the three grades of defect and the break proofs: `docs/security/postgrest-filter-quoting-mr-b04.md` (MR-B04, 2026-10-23).
- 🔴 **A PLAIN `col=ilike.VALUE` QUERY PARAMETER (or `.ilike(col, value)`) IS NEVER QUOTED — the quoted helpers belong inside an `or(…)`/`and(…)` group or an `in.(…)` list only.** Outside those nothing after `ilike.` is structure, so the quotes become two literal `"` characters and the read matches nothing: #6350 quoted the CRM-username and work-email duplicate checks, and on production (23 Oct 2026) the unquoted read found 1 row, the quoted one 0, so a taken username reached the unique index as a raw 23505. Percent-encode it and stop. 🔴 **`WILDCARD_CHARS` blanks `\` as well as `%` and `*`** — a term ending in LIKE's escape character fails the whole read. Pinned by `tests/postgrest-search-filter.test.ts`.

### Shapes no planner can rescue

- 🔴 **AN `OR` OF TWO EQUALITIES IS NOT AN EQUIJOIN — IT CANNOT HASH, IT CANNOT MERGE, AND IT RUNS AS A NESTED LOOP WHATEVER THE ROW COUNTS SAY.** The contact refresh's vendor handover matched a rep by hr key OR by CRM id and cost **316 ms a run** — its most expensive statement — throwing 156,074 joined rows away to hand over nothing. 🔴 **Make the key, do not tune the join:** where the arms are mutually exclusive by construction (one needs the column, the other needs it blank), the lookup emits BOTH keys per row and the join compares one expression, `coalesce(nullif(lower(btrim(x)), ''), 'crm:' || y::text)` — same 21,481 rows, 0 either way. A prefix is safe only where the other key's alphabet excludes it. Account: `docs/crm-contact-master-refresh-speed.md`, "The 24 October pass".
- 🔴 **A SQL FUNCTION WITH ANY `SET` CLAUSE CANNOT BE INLINED BY THE PLANNER — SO `set search_path to 'public'` TURNS A ONE-EXPRESSION HELPER INTO A REAL CALL PER ROW** (`crm_contact_fold_grade`: ~110 ms of one list build across five call sites). 🔴 **Inline the expression at the call site and leave the helper alone**, naming the migration you copied its body from — stripping the `SET` clause to win the inlining raises a `function_search_path_mutable` finding that `scripts/check-advisor-baseline.ts` fails `validate` on, and its other callers still want it. Account: `docs/crm-contact-master-refresh-speed.md`.
- 🔴 **`analyze pg_temp.<table>` NAMES THE COLUMNS, OR IT BUILDS HISTOGRAMS NOTHING READS** — only what something joins, filters or sorts by matters. Timed on production on freshly built tables: `crm_refresh_phoenix_customers` all 14 columns **168 ms** against the 7 that are read **53 ms**; `crm_refresh_phoenix_truth` all 5 **80 ms** against `contact_crm_id` alone **3 ms**. 192 ms a run, ~96 runs a day, read never.
- 🔴 **A SQL READ'S PLAN IS MADE WITH ITS PARAMETERS UNKNOWN, SO A HAND-RUN `EXPLAIN` WITH LITERALS SHOWS A PLAN IT NEVER GETS — `PREPARE` THE BODY UNDER `plan_cache_mode = force_generic_plan`.** `phx_customer_directory`'s optional `(p_x is null or …)` filters multiply the estimate down to 24 rows of about 118,000, so its price lookup ran as a nested loop: 20,384,417 rows removed by join filter on staging, 24.6 s on production against the app's 15 s client, and the page read "Failed to load Customer Directory." on 1 Nov 2026. - 🔴 **`enable_nestloop` OFF FIXES ONE MEASURED PLAN, NEVER A BLANKET — AND `is not distinct from` CANNOT HASH, SO JOIN THAT ON A PAIR KEY.** The 14-function sweep of 1 Nov 2026: the switch took `phx_closing_corrections` from 2,530 to 5,652 ms and `phx_territory_area_pharmacies` from 216 to 633 ms, and did nothing for `phx_sales_channels_by_rep`. 🔴 **Plain `EXPLAIN` first, then `ANALYZE` on a short limit:** an unmaterialised `period_price` subquery re-runs `phx_factory_price` per outer row, and the widest `ANALYZE` of market awareness preceded an unclean staging restart by 2 min 23 s. 🔴 **`force_generic_plan` overstates PL/pgSQL**, which stayed on custom plans: `phx_exec_sos_trend` read 1,103 ms there and 281–332 ms over nine real calls, so time real calls in one session before fixing. Staging `work_mem` is 3,500 kB against production's 32 MB. Account: `supabase/migrations/20261101190920_phx_period_price_joins_never_nest_blind.sql`. Pinned by `tests/sales-reads-price-each-pair-once.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **A CTE NAMED ONCE IS INLINED, SO A COSTLY EXPRESSION IN IT IS COPIED INTO EVERY PLACE THAT READS ITS COLUMN — COUNT THE CALLS, THEN WRITE `AS MATERIALIZED`.** `phx_sales_channels_by_rep` read `channel` in six FILTERs: 23,423 rows made 140,538 `phx_customer_channel` calls, 32.9 s of a 34.1 s month. `set local track_functions = 'all'` then `pg_stat_xact_user_functions` in one transaction gives the count, for the leader only, so a parallel plan under-reports. - 🔴 **THROUGH THE APP'S ROUTE A FUNCTION'S OWN `SET statement_timeout` IS THE LIMIT THAT COUNTS, UP OR DOWN; OVER A DIRECT CONNECTION IT DOES NOTHING.** PostgREST 14.5, on both databases, applies the declared value before the call. - 🔴 **`CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION` WITHOUT A `SET` CLAUSE REMOVES ONE AN `ALTER` ADDED, AND THE OLD FILE DOES NOT SHOW IT.** `phx_customer_channel`'s `search_path` came from an ALTER in `20260929215727`, so copying its 20260907170000 definition would have dropped it.
