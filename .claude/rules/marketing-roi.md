## Rules

### Marketing ROI status

- 🔴 **THE ROI VERDICT IS RECOMPUTED IN THREE PLACES, AND A CHANGE TO THE RULE LANDS IN ALL THREE OR THE DASHBOARD CONTRADICTS THE TRACKER.** `resolveRoiResultStatus` (`src/lib/marketing-requests/roi-report-service.ts`) is the rule; `phx_marketing_roi_customer_dashboard`, `phx_refresh_marketing_roi_facts` (which STORES its answer in `marketing_roi_request_facts.actual_roi_status`) and `phx_marketing_roi_fast` each carry their own copy of it. 🔴 **`awaiting_data` is the arm that keeps getting lost: a window ending after `phx_marketing_roi_latest_validated_period()` is still being measured, so it is NEVER `behind` and NEVER `no_data`, and its cost is NEVER spend at risk** — on 2026-10-09 the fast RPC had no such arm and read all 23 dashboard requests as failures, 122,107 EGP "at risk", every window open. The window is `pay_month + roi_duration_months - 1`, and **`roi_duration_months` is NOT on the fact row** — join `marketing_requests`. A filter on a recomputed status must read the COMPUTED column, never the stored one. Pinned by `tests/marketing-roi-fast-awaiting-data.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE DASHBOARD READS THE FACT TABLES SINCE 2026-10-09, SO ITS NUMBERS ARE ONLY AS FRESH AS `phoenix-marketing-roi-refresh` — pg_cron jobid 4 on production, `20 * * * *`** (the owner's two decisions that evening: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SMI_USE_FACT_TABLES=true` on the production service, then the hourly schedule). Before it, `phx_refresh_marketing_roi_facts` ran only on a Source-of-Sales import, the normalizer, direct-order tracking or `/api/marketing/roi-refresh`, and the payload's `freshness` compares against the last validated BATCH and never the clock — so stale numbers read `fresh`. 🔴 **Every tick records `triggered_by = 'admin_manual'`** (derived from `p_batch_id is null`): nobody ran it by hand, and giving the cron a batch id to "fix" that label would rebuild one import's periods instead of all of them. An overlapping tick is safe — the function takes an advisory lock and returns `skipped`. `supabase/migrations/20261009200001_schedule_marketing_roi_refresh_cron.sql`, pinned by `tests/marketing-roi-refresh-schedule.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE HOURLY ROI REBUILD LISTS ITS DISTINCT VALID PERIODS BY SKIPPING ON `sos_smi_valid_period_line_region_idx` — NEVER BY `array_agg(distinct …)` OVER ALL VALID ROWS.** That aggregate read ~406,000 index entries (13,867 pages), and the next hourly tick found them out of cache: 5.4 s with 14,106 disk reads, 9.4 s mean over 58 calls, for a list the rebuild never reads (it is only written to `affected_periods`). `20261016223000` swaps that one statement for a recursive loose scan — 12 probes, 53 pages, 368 ms for the whole call on production — and every `marketing_roi_*_facts` table and `affected_periods` came out identical. A valid row with no period still yields one trailing NULL. Rollback: re-apply `20261009150000`. Pinned by `tests/marketing-roi-refresh-period-skip.test.ts`. Account: `docs/marketing-roi-refresh-speed.md`.

### ROI Tracker and Coverage Report (audit 2026-10-09)

- 🔴 **AN EMPTY STATUS LIST IS "APPROVED MONEY ONLY", NEVER "ALL"** — `resolveRoiStatusScope` (since #1682). A surface that needs pending, rejected or cancelled requests names them (`ROI_LIVE_TRACKER_STATUSES`); sending `[]` hid 27 of 52 requests from the live tracker's own approval-queue, rejected and cancelled tiles. The Coverage Report applies the same default scope as the ROI report. Pinned by `tests/marketing-roi-coverage-audit.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **"NOT YET PAID" IS DECIDED BY THE PAY DATE AGAINST TODAY IN CAIRO — NEVER BY HOW FAR THE VALIDATED SALES DATA REACHES.** `roiLifecycleStage(row, today)`; all 15 requests on that tile had been paid. Money not yet committed (awaiting approval, rejected, cancelled) stays out of the committed-spend flight path and calendar (`isUncommittedStage`). Pinned by `tests/marketing-roi-live-stages.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A REQUEST'S UNITS ARE SHOWN ONLY WHEN IT PROMOTES ONE PRODUCT CODE** — `singleProductUnits` (`roi-units.ts`); a View Sales bucket or total spanning two codes has `units: null`, and its value still adds. Never sum TB040 and TB080.
- 🔴 **ANY ROLL-UP OF ACTUAL SALES ACROSS REQUESTS DEDUPES THROUGH THE ATTRIBUTION LEDGER** — `sharedAttributionOverlap` (`roi-attribution-overlap.ts`): the Coverage breakdown, the monthly trend, the PM card's KPI band and the workbook TOTAL. Two campaigns claiming one sale are each right per row and wrong when added (45,153.88 vs the 42,433.56 headline). The ledger leaves out WITHHELD rows only, never degraded ones.
- 🔴 **A WINDOW STILL OPEN PAST THE VALIDATED MONTH IS `awaiting_data`, NEVER `behind` OR `no_data`** — the app's `resolveRoiResultStatus` and the customer dashboard agree; attributed sales in a window are at least `partial_pending`, never "no validated data". The stored facts follow it from migration `20261009150000` (`phx_refresh_marketing_roi_facts`) and the S&M Intelligence RPC from `20261009120001` (`phx_marketing_roi_fast`), both live on staging and production since 2026-10-09. 🔴 The Supabase tool DECLINED the facts apply on staging three times that day, twice after his typed word: staging took it from the staging migrator on merge, production from his SQL-editor paste — read `pg_get_functiondef` for `awaiting_data` before trusting a stored status.
- 🔒 **DIRECT-ORDER SALES REACH A CAMPAIGN'S ROI ONLY THROUGH `track_direct_sales`, AND THE ONE REQUEST THAT CARRIES IT STAYS ON** (owner, 2026-10-09, asked to audit it and answered "leave it on for this one campaign"): `MKT-REQ-20260816-8E4E42C4`, Nahed Tarek, EGP 4,000. **Never turn it off as a tidy-up.** Two independent gates keep every other campaign clean: the `direct_hits` CTE in `phx_marketing_request_sales_lines` and `phx_refresh_marketing_roi_facts` is `where track_direct_sales = true`, and the retail branch joins `marketing_roi_distributor_rollup`, which does **not** list the injected `DirectReq` code — so the 100 injected direct rows in Source of Sales cannot reach marketing ROI at all. Measured on production that day: all 52 campaigns returned `retail` only, and the stored facts equalled the live retail total to the piastre. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`.
- 🔴 **A HAND EDIT OF `marketing_requests` LEAVES NO HISTORY UNLESS IT CHANGES `status` OR `estimated_cost`** — `phx_capture_marketing_request_event` fires on every UPDATE but only writes an event for those two columns, plus INSERT and DELETE. A correction to `roi_duration_months`, `pay_date` or the ROI terms is therefore invisible in the case file afterwards. Write the `marketing_request_events` row yourself (`request_value_changed` renders as "changed the requested figures", so it carries a duration change honestly) with the field, the from and the to in `payload`. Do not read the older "a hand-run SQL decision DOES get an audit trail" note as covering a field edit: it is about a status *decision*.
- 🔒 **THE COVERAGE REPORT'S COMPANY-WIDE ROLES ARE ROI TRACKING'S `companyMarketingRoles` — THE FINANCE MANAGER AND FINANCIAL ADVISOR INCLUDED** (owner, 2026-10-09, "All 52, like ROI"). A role sweep found the Finance Manager opening a Coverage Report that read 0 of 52 requests, while ROI Tracking and its View Sales, which shows the same pharmacies and sales, gave him all 52. The only difference left between the two scopes is the Medical Rep's territory route, which runs in ROI Tracking alone. Pinned by `tests/marketing-coverage-report-access.test.ts`, which asserts the two sets are equal.
- 🔴 **`phx_marketing_roi_latest_validated_period()` IS A COMPLETENESS FLAG, NEVER A CAP ON WHAT IS ATTRIBUTED, SO NO SURFACE MAY PRINT "attributed up to <month>".** It answers the newest month every month-filing distributor has filed — 2026-08 on 9 Oct 2026, when September held **4 validated batches of the 18** August had — while `phx_marketing_request_roi_actuals_v2` counts EVERY validated sales row there is. A part-loaded month is therefore already in the money: asked that day for the campaigns behind one Product Manager's EGP 1,637.30, all of it turned out to be Ibn Sina's September file, under a face reading "attributed up to Aug 2026". Say sales are **loaded to** that month and a later one counts as its files arrive. Pinned by `tests/marketing-manager-scorecard.test.ts`.

### Manager Scorecard, and the entry rule he REFUSED (owner, 2026-10-09)

- 🔴 **NOTHING IN THIS REPORT MAY EVER BLOCK A MARKETING REQUEST** (owner, 2026-10-09: *"only make only the sheet, don't put the rule, I didn't get approval … build only the sheet … but don't put the rule, never block anyone"*). A first cut DID refuse a new request from anyone whose campaigns past half their ROI window had returned under 25% of their cost — he had asked for it earlier the same day, then withdrew it before it shipped because **the rule itself is not approved yet**. It was removed whole: the gate module, the lock in the drafts route's POST, the verdict on its GET, the blocker in `draft-blockers.ts` and the verdict column. 🔴 **A plan, summary or memory that still describes a 25%/50% rule, `evaluateMarketingRoiGate` or `roiWindowHalfwayPassed` is STALE — never rebuild it, and never add any other refusal to this report.** Only HIS own words bring it back. Pinned by `tests/marketing-manager-scorecard.test.ts`, which asserts a row carries no verdict and that a complete request still saves.
- 🔒 **THE MANAGER SCORECARD IS THE ROI REPORT GROUPED BY REQUESTER — ONE ENGINE CALL, NO SECOND OPINION, AND THE EXPORTS REPEAT THE SCREEN.** `getMarketingManagerScorecard` asks `getMarketingRoiReport` with `ROI_LIVE_TRACKER_STATUSES` **named explicitly** (an empty list is "approved money only") and `buildMarketingManagerScorecard` groups its rows: counts over EVERY status, money over the approved ones. 🔴 **The footer dedupes from the RAW sums, never from the already-deduped per-manager rows** — deducting the same shared sale twice read one manager's two campaigns as **zero**. Mounted on both shells from one GET route (`/api/marketing-requests/manager-scorecard`), whose door is the UNION of the admin ROI page gate and the portal ROI Tracker predicate — never a third role set, and the **Excel/PDF route carries that same union** (owner, 2026-10-09: *"make me able to extract reports from it either by excel or either by pdf"*). Both files are built from the SAME scorecard object the page renders, so a forwarded total can never disagree with the screen, and the filename carries the **Cairo** day.
- 🔴 **A ZERO ON THE MANAGER SCORECARD NAMES THE DISTRIBUTOR THAT HAS NOT FILED, AND THE CAPTION NEVER MOVES A FIGURE** (owner, 2026-10-09: *"show on the Scorecard which zeroes are missing distributor files"*). `missingDistributorFiles` (`manager-scorecard-awaiting.ts`) captions an approved campaign when a **parent** distributor code its pharmacies map to has not filed a **closed** window month it normally files — 2 of the 3 months before it. Three cuts are what make it a signal rather than noise: the running month is never owed (a file lands after the month ends), the future is never owed, and **branch and tender siblings are never captioned** — Ibn Sina had not filed September while Ibn Sina-Sato had, so reading the family as one hides the case, while flagging `PS-T` would caption nearly every row. Measured that day: 12 of 25 approved campaigns, EGP 65,669. Pinned by `tests/marketing-manager-scorecard.test.ts`.
