Since 10 Nov 2026 also the four far-visit safeguards: the impossible-location refusal, the stuck-rep alert, the far-visit hold the SFE decides, and the 500 m line of an account.

## Rules

- 🔒 **THE SFE READS A REP'S REFUSED READINGS ON `/admin/phoenix-crm/refused-readings` — HOUSEKEEPING, NOT A FLAG, AND NEVER A TAB OF LOCATION INTEGRITY** (owner, 2026-10-03: *"build the refused readings view for the SFE"*). The Super Admin and the SFE Manager, a 404 for anybody else; **the phone's mock-location answer is the Super Admin's alone and is never QUERIED for the SFE** (`readsMockAnswer`). Per rep, last 14 days: customers refused on and how many he later SAVED — a save BEFORE the refusal proves nothing, a refusal tied to no customer is never counted as stuck, nine retries are ONE blocked customer. **Each refusal shows the PHOTO the phone sent, to both readers, as a POINTER the page signs through the scoped `/api/phoenix-crm/photos/sign` route (`CrmPhotoThumb`, `lazy`) — the page never opens the bucket and a non-slot path reads "None kept".** Read-only; the rep is told nothing new. Only the recorder and this reader may name the table. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The refused-readings board". `a-refused-crm-save-is-traceable-only-in-the-upload-slot`
- 🔴 **A PERMISSION THE BROWSER BLOCKS IS REPORTED BY THE BROWSER, THROUGH ITS OWN ALWAYS-204 ROUTE — NEVER FOLDED ONTO A SAVE** (owner, 2026-10-13: "record a browser-blocked permission so it reaches the refused-readings board"). The other three reasons are SERVER refusals of a reading that arrived; a blocked permission produces none, so there is no request to ride — `POST /api/phoenix-crm/refused-readings` is it, `reason = 'blocked'` with `blocked_permission` naming `location` or `camera`, paired by the table's own CHECK. 🔴 **The rep learns nothing new (golden rule #1):** the reporter is called AFTER `setError`, returns void, awaits nothing, swallows everything, and every exit of the route is a bare 204. 🔴 **The rep is the SESSION's and the row is tied to NO customer** — one switch blocks the whole list, so a row pinned to whichever customer was on screen would read as a fault of that place. Why it exists: `src/components/phoenix-crm/report-blocked-permission.ts`'s header and the migration's. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refused-readings.test.ts` · `tests/phoenix-crm-boundary.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE REFUSED-READINGS BOARD READS THE REASON EXHAUSTIVELY (`parseRefusedReason`) — A TWO-WAY COERCION TOLD REPS STANDING 5 m AWAY THAT THEIR POSITION WAS TOO ROUGH** (defect, live 5-13 October 2026). The reader asked `row.reason === "no_fix" ? "no_fix" : "rough"`, so every `off_listing` row printed the rough-position sentence — the one thing that refusal is NOT, both its readings being precise and the account's own Google pin at fault. 🔴 **Never widen the table's reason CHECK without teaching that function the new value**: the board's test reads the CHECK out of the migration and asserts each allowed reason is recognised and reads as its own sentence, so a new reason fails `validate` rather than quietly printing as "too rough". `timetable_unreadable` / `timetable_missing` (`20261017193000`, owner, 2026-10-17) are the timetable scanner's tries: only those two may carry `shared_place_id`, the building, and `countTimetableRefusals` counts rep + building + Cairo day.
- 🔴 **THE SIGNBOARD SCANNER'S TRIES ARE ROWS HERE TOO** (2026-10-18): `signboard_unreadable` / `signboard_no_plate` (`20261018230000`). Only those two carry `signboard_key` (the place, the customer, or a new customer's folded name), paired by the table's CHECK, and `countSignboardRefusals` counts rep + key + Cairo day. A refusal whose row did not land is never thrown at the door — the photo is saved and held — because an uncounted try repeats for ever. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refused-readings-board.test.ts` and `checkDoorScanner`.
- 🔴 **`far_from_place` IS A VISIT REFUSED FOR WHERE IT WAS LOGGED, NOT HOW WELL THE PHONE READ — IT CARRIES `distance_meters` AND `place_id`, AND `max_accuracy_meters` HOLDS THE FAR LINE** (2026-10-20, `20261020034610`). Only that reason may carry either column (the table's CHECK); `recordFarVisitRefusal` writes it, void and never throwing, and the board prints the metres and the line, never "too rough". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-visit-far-refusal.test.ts` and the board test's nine-reason list.
- 🔴 **EVERY REFUSED TRY WITH A POSITION IS DRAWN ON A SMALL MAP BESIDE THE CUSTOMER'S SAVED PLACE, FOR BOTH READERS** (owner, 2026-11-10: "make any location of a logged visit even if failed to be saved and shown only in admin account vs the correct location"; he chose the Super Admin and the SFE Manager, a small map on each row). Marker 1 the phone, 2 the place, the distance and a Google Maps link each (`TryLocationsMap`, lazy OSM tiles). The place is the one the refusal was MEASURED to (`place_id`, even retired), else the customer's nearest ACTIVE one (`savedPlaceForTry`). No position, no map; no place, the phone alone. Read-only. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refused-readings-board.test.ts` §10.
- 🔒 **A WRONGLY SAVED PLACE IS CAUGHT FOUR WAYS: AN IMPOSSIBLE READING IS REFUSED AT VALIDATION, A STUCK REP ALERTS, HIS NEXT VISIT FROM THAT SPOT IS HELD FOR THE SFE, AND AN AM ACCOUNT'S LINE IS 500 m** (owner, 2026-11-10, all four, after Momen Fathy was refused 51 times at Dr. Doria Darraz's door: her place was saved 8.3 km off from an indoor Wi-Fi guess). A: a place reading 3 km or more from his own sharp visit saved in the last hour, at over 80 km/h, or the exact point already refused that way within 6 h, is filed `impossible_travel` (`judgeImpossibleLocation`, from 10 Nov only). B: the fifth sharp far refusal within 50 m on one customer inside SEVEN days (`STUCK_LOOKBACK_DAYS`, since 2026-11-11; one day missed the rep who gives up at three) tells the Super Admin and the SFE Manager once (`far_visit_stuck`, never email). C: the next visit from that spot saves into `phoenix_crm_far_visit_holds` and is out of both countable views and `visitCountsInKpi` until approved on `/admin/phoenix-crm/far-visit-holds`. D: `farRefusalLineFor`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-far-visit-safeguards.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-far-visit-safeguards.md`.
- 🔒 **A VALIDATED DOCTOR OR ACCOUNT ALWAYS HOLDS A PLACE, A CAPTURE'S TWO READINGS MUST AGREE, AND A VISIT LOGGED BEFORE THE PLACE IS HELD WHEN IT IS PAST ITS LINE** (owner, 2026-11-11, "Build all six fixes"). An approved add, a bring-back and an undone drop (`phoenix_crm_decide_request`, `phoenix_crm_reverse_list_request`) land on `pending` with no active place, and two deferred constraint triggers refuse any other new stamp (`phoenix_crm:validated_without_place`; pharmacies hold no place row and are exempt). `refuseReadingsApart` refuses a place, a manager's capture and a venue whose pin and photo reading are over 250 m apart (`readings_apart`, the eleventh reason); the rep reads only `READINGS_APART_REFUSED`, never the distance. `restampVisitLocations` writes a `basis = 'before_place'` hold BEFORE it measures a `no_place` visit. The held-visits page ends with Places to re-check (`placesToRecheck`), read by three nightly checks (`phoenix-crm-places.ts`). 🔴 Test customers validated before 21 Oct 2026 hold no place by design: every count filters `is_test = false`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-far-visit-safeguards.md`, "11 November 2026".
