## Rules

### Queues, stored notices and deliveries

- 🔴 **THE QUEUES COUNT EVERY ATTEMPT AND CLAIM BEFORE THEY SEND** (23 Oct 2026 audit, F-04/F-05/Pass 5). The outbox reaper spends one attempt per stale `processing` row (the last is terminal), pinned to the lock it read; the Telegram and push retry drains (and the manual Telegram retry) claim the row by a compare-and-set on `attempts` BEFORE sending, so two overlapping drains never both send; a background job left `processing` an hour is re-queued or failed before new work is claimed. 🔴 **The Recipient Assurance Guard FAILS CLOSED**: a failed directory read uses the last good copy (24 h at most), and with none the email is held back with a TRANSIENT reason and retried — never sent unguarded. Pinned by `tests/background-jobs-recovery.test.ts` and `tests/credential-email-mirror.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A STORED NOTICE IS NEVER REWRITTEN ONCE IT REACHED SOMEBODY — FOR EVERY SENDER** (owner, 23 Oct 2026: "fix the notification overwrite for every sender"). `createNotificationEvent` used to upsert `notification_events` on `event_key` BEFORE the delivery claim, so a repeat send that `claimDelivery` then refused still rewrote the title, body and sent time the bell shows (about twenty event types in 30 days — `interview_awaiting_response` 566, `closing_published` 66; the System Audit message read "365 issues", then "3"). Now `reachedNoticeId` returns the existing row untouched when any of its deliveries is sent, pending or retrying (`src/lib/notifications/stored-notice.ts`); a notice that reached nobody may still be rewritten. Never move the check after the upsert. Pinned by `tests/notification-stored-notice.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE DRAIN RUNS ONCE ACROSS EVERY INSTANCE — A JOB LEASE, NOT A MODULE VARIABLE** (REC-083, 13 Nov 2026). `runScheduledNotificationProcessing` claims the `phoenix-process-notifications` lease (`createExclusiveRunGuard`, `src/lib/platform/job-lease.ts`, ttl 290 s) before any step and releases it (a one-second re-claim) in `finally`; another live run → `{ skipped: true, reason: "overlapping-run" }` and `cron_process_overlap_skipped`. A lease that cannot be read falls back to the in-process guard (`cron_process_lease_unavailable`, logged once) — never stop the drain for the lock store. Pinned by `tests/shared-instance-state.test.ts` and, on the REAL drain loaded as two servers, `tests/notification-drain-two-instances.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE FALLBACK `event_key` CARRIES THE ACTOR, SO TWO SENDERS RAISING THE SAME NOTICE FOR ONE RECIPIENT ARE TWO ROWS AND NOT AN OVERWRITE** (2026-10-23). `notification_events` is upserted `onConflict: "event_key"`, and `eventKeyFor` hashed only `actionUrl`, `eventType`, `module`, the two recipient fields and `title` — and a templated title names no sender, because the sender lives in the MESSAGE. So the second sender's write landed on the first sender's row and the recipient kept whichever was written last. Suffixing per recipient in `createSystemNotificationForRecipients` never covered it: the collision is between SENDERS to one recipient. `recordId` and `subjectHrId` stay out — they say what the notice is about, not who raised it, and folding them in would split notices meant to collapse. Measured before shipping: 0 of the 97 `pending` rows use a fallback hash key, so nothing in flight was re-keyed. Pinned by `tests/notification-event-key-includes-the-actor.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A LOST `notification_deliveries` STATUS WRITE IS A SECOND REAL MESSAGE TO A PERSON, NOT A BOOKKEEPING GAP** (23 Oct 2026). `claimDelivery` opens the row, `upsertDelivery` records the outcome, and the retry drain re-sends any row still open — so when `upsertDelivery` swallowed its own failure with a log line, an outage left the row open and ARMED a duplicate email or Telegram. It now persists through the write outbox (`src/lib/notifications/notification-outbox.ts`); both queued writes are upserts on a unique key, so a replay is harmless. 🔴 **`createNotificationEvent` is RETRIED BUT NEVER QUEUED** — its id is what the claim anchors to, and a row landing hours later would assert a `status: 'sent'` send that never happened. Account: `docs/observability.md`, "Protecting the notification writes". Pinned by `tests/notification-outbox.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A BUTTON'S ANSWER NEVER WAITS FOR ITS NOTICES — THEY RUN INSIDE `afterResponse`, NEVER BEFORE THE REPLY** (23 Oct 2026, owner: approve, reject and 90% of the buttons were "stale, very long"). Production p50 before: vacation decision 6.1 s, excuse 8.0 s, direct-order decision 7.8 s (22 s at p95), marketing decision 10.7 s — the decision itself was done at 0.8 s and the notices were the other 87%, while Phoenix CRM's decide, already deferred, took 0.4 s. Keep the failure record (`notification_failed`) INSIDE the task, capture only `const`s, leave cron and loop notice code awaited (no reply to be late for), and never defer a notice whose result the caller returns. A failed notice is now logged (`after_response_failed`), not thrown over a saved decision. Every notice call in an interactive handler goes through `afterResponse(label, task)` (`src/lib/http/after-response.ts`). Pinned by `tests/notices-after-the-reply.test.ts`. Account: `docs/buttons-answer-before-notices.md`.
- 🔴 **WITHDRAWING BY KEY PREFIX NEEDS `notification_events_event_key_pattern_idx` (`event_key text_pattern_ops`) — THE UNIQUE `event_key` INDEX CANNOT ANSWER A `like` UNDER THE `en_US.UTF-8` COLLATION** (26 Oct 2026, load audit). Without it every `likePrefix` withdraw read the whole 37 MB table: 92 ms mean and 103,592 disk blocks, against 1.7 ms with it. Never drop it as a duplicate of the unique index. Pinned by `tests/db-load-sweep-skipscan.test.ts`; account: `docs/perf/2026-10-25-db-load-baseline.md`.
- 🔴 **A LOCAL PREVIEW NEVER CLEARS A PERSON'S PHOENIX CRM "NEW" BADGE** (2 Nov 2026). `/api/phoenix-crm/notices/seen` answers `isPreviewSession` with a 204 before anything else: a dev-auth look under a real HR id runs on the production database, and that person has seen nothing. A real session and a test account clear theirs as before. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-notices-seen-preview.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A credential email never sits readable in `notification_outbox`** (owner, 9 Nov 2026) — a `credentialEmail` row's `metrics` (the reset code) and `ctaHref` (the set-your-password link) are sealed by `src/lib/notifications/credential-seal.ts` before the row is written, opened in memory only to send, and deleted when the row is sent, skipped or out of retries (`credentialRedacted: true`); its dedupe key is keyed with `PHOENIX_AUTH_SECRET`. Never write the plain input back as the payload. **No other record keeps one either:** the gate's `notification_send_reviews` copy goes through `redactCredentialEmailBody`, provider click events through `redactCredentialLinksInJson`, the set-your-password notification has no `actionUrl`, and a held credential email is never re-sent from review (`src/lib/email/credential-redaction.ts`). Pinned by `tests/credential-outbox-seal.test.ts` and `tests/credential-redaction.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **Internal messages are gated by `internal_messages`, held by every active role** (owner, 2026-11-13, "Everyone, as today" — REC-053). Each route answers a signed-out call 401, then asserts `internal_messages` (read to list and mark read, create to search and send). A sender gets 10 messages a minute and 20 email copies a day (`INTERNAL_MESSAGE_SENDS_PER_MINUTE`, `INTERNAL_MESSAGE_EMAILS_PER_DAY`); both are counted before the insert, so a refused message leaves no row and no email. Every stored message writes one `internal_message` audit row (sender, recipient, whether email was asked for), never the subject or body. Never narrow the audience to pass a test. Pinned by `tests/internal-messages.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE HOME PAGE'S TWO NOTIFICATION CARDS: PUSH ASKS AGAIN ONLY AFTER A DROP, TELEGRAM ASKS ONCE** (owner, 13 Nov 2026, integration audit: "Ask again after a drop"; "Every home page, once"). Both sit on the six home pages (`PushOptInNudge`, then `TelegramHomeCard`) and share ONE settings read (`home-notification-settings.ts`); never give either its own fetch. **Push:** no card with a device receiving; an answer (`pushOptInNudgeDismissedAt`, written on close AND on turning push on) stands until a dropped device was last receiving at or after it (`pushWasDroppedSinceAnswer`, two minutes of slack for the two requests). 🔴 **Never read `unsubscribed_at` for the drop**: `upsertPushSubscription` stamps it on every page load of a browser without push, so the card would return on every visit, for ever; `subscribed_at` stops moving when a device stops receiving. **Telegram:** the SERVER decides in `GET /api/notifications/settings` (`homeCards.telegram`, `shouldShowTelegramHomeCard`): everybody not connected, never a test account, and never again once `telegramHomeCardAnsweredAt` is written (closing it, or connecting through it; `POST /api/notifications/telegram/home-card`, `employee_profile` read so whoever sees the card can answer it). Its button is the one connect flow (`telegram-connect.ts`). Phoenix CRM's daily card is a different card with its own rule. Pinned by `tests/push-opt-in-nudge.test.ts` and `tests/telegram-home-card.test.ts`. Account and the measured numbers: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The provider changes of the integration audit".
- 🔒 **A BROWSER THAT CANNOT TAKE PUSH IS TOLD WHAT TO DO, NEVER "CHECK SITE SETTINGS"** (owner, 14 Nov 2026, "Yes, fix the message", after his own iPhone failed). `requestOneSignalPermission` answers at once with `unsupported` from `pushUnsupportedReason` (`onesignal-client.ts`): an iPhone or iPad tab (an iPad in desktop mode reads as a Mac with touch points) → "Add to Home Screen"; a Home Screen app with no Notification API → update iOS; any other browser with none → open a real browser. The settings panel and the home-page card both show `pushUnsupportedMessage`. Pinned by `tests/push-enable-hang.test.ts`.
