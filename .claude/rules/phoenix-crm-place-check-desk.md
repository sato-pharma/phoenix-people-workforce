## Rules

- 🔒 **PLACE CHECKS OPENS ON ONE WAITING NUMBER AND TWO LANES, READY AND YOUR CALL — NOTHING THAT IS NOT A DECISION SITS ABOVE THE FIRST WAITING PLACE** (owner, 23 Nov 2026: "REBUILD FROM SCRATCH LESS THAN 9.5/10 PER ITEM IS NOT ACCPETED"; the first waiting place was about 1,700 px down, the phone page 51,062 px long). `validation-reviews-board.tsx` is a frame over `place-checks/`: the header (`desk-header.tsx`), the filters, the tab strip, one panel per tab. The scorecard, Approved by the system, Not confirmed, the buildings, the comparisons and the page's own `slots` are tabs. 🔴 The lanes are the Super Admin's alone (`lanes`): the SFE Manager's board has one list called Waiting, and a district manager's has no header, no lanes, no meter, and still opens with its scorecard. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-check-lanes.test.ts`. Account: `docs/approvals-rebuild-2026-11-place-checks.md`.
- 🔒 **ON HIS BOARD THE FOLDED FILTERS LINE ENDS THE TAB STRIP ON A DESKTOP — THE BOXED BAR IS THE PHONE'S, AND THE OPEN FIELDS' ALONE** (owner, 23 Nov 2026: "Fix desktop top"; the bar and its gap stood 82 px above the first waiting place on the live page). `PlaceCheckFilterLine` with `inStrip` (`place-checks/filter-bar.tsx`, drawn by `tab-strip.tsx`) takes only the width its words need: no `flex-1`, no `basis-60`, buttons `shrink-0`. Beside the last tabs it costs nothing, on a row of its own 44 px, never more than the bar it replaced. The tab list is `contents`, so the tabs and the line wrap as one row and the line is no tab. A board with no lanes keeps the bar whole. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-check-lanes.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **ON HIS BOARD A LANE'S "PICK ROWS" LINE STANDS IN ITS SECTION'S TITLE ROW — ONE LINE, NEVER A COPY, AND THE BOX OPENS ONLY ON A TICK OR ON WHAT THE LAST RUN SAID** (owner, 23 Nov 2026: "Add Pick rows to 707"; as a box of its own the line stood 61 px between the title and the first waiting place). `needs-panel.tsx` puts a slot in each lane section's title row (`action`, only while the list has rows) and hands it down through `BulkPickLineSlot`; `BulkDecideBar` (`place-check-bulk.tsx`) draws its own first line into it with `createPortal`, same state, same buttons. The reason, the note and the action buttons still open in the box under the title; the saved line and the not-answered rows still open it after a run clears the ticks. No slot (the default) keeps the bar whole: the SFE Manager's board, a district manager's, History, In use, the buildings tab. A slot not on the page yet draws nothing, so the line never stands in the old place first. 🔴 **No width cap on the slot**: measured on the live page, none never loses height and a cap does. `AdminSectionHeader` is the admin shell's and is not touched. Figures: `docs/approvals-rebuild-2026-11-place-checks.md`, "Honest limits". Pinned in `tests/phoenix-crm-place-check-lanes.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A LANE DECIDES NOTHING, AND READY NEEDS ALL THREE: THE ADVICE IS APPROVE, THAT KIND IS CONFIDENT, AND APPROVE CAN BE PRESSED** (`place-check-lanes.ts`, pure). A customer short of a full name is never Ready (`LANE_SHORT_NAME`); a waiting customer with no review row is his call (`LANE_NO_REVIEW_ROW`). 🔴 A place or a building with an integrity case nobody has settled is never Ready either (`LANE_OPEN_CASE`, `openCaseSubjects` over the cases the board already draws): one press on the lane never approves what the sign-wins rule itself would leave. 🔴 A building is ONE decision and is Ready only if everything open in it is: the building itself when held, and every customer waiting behind it (`laneOfBuilding`, `laneOfVenueGroup`). Your call carries the one reason it is his, and the lane's line names the commonest first (`yourCallReasons`).
- 🔴 **HIS LAST 30 DAYS OUTRANK THE ADVICE TABLE ONCE A KIND HOLDS 20 OF HIS DECISIONS — NOBODY PROMOTES A KIND BY HAND** (`adviceIsConfidentNow`, `AGREEMENT_READY_MIN_DECISIONS`, 19 in 20 by `AGREEMENT_READY_ONE_IN`). A kind he now agrees with becomes Ready; one he stopped agreeing with leaves it. `confident` on an advice is only where the kind stood on his first 2,353 decisions: a timetable naming the customer (116 of 143) and a building whose sign names it (100 of 120) say Approve and are NOT confident.
- 🔴 **EVERY DECISION KEEPS THE ADVICE THE PAGE SHOWED, FROM THE SAME RECORD THE ROW IS BUILT FROM** (`place-check-stored-advice.ts`; `keepReviewAdviceQuietly`, `keepBuildingAdviceQuietly`; columns `system_advice_at_decision` and `system_advice_code_at_decision`, migration `20261123052511`). "Check it yourself" is counted and never scored. A place the system approved itself is not his agreement, and becomes a disagreement the moment he takes it back. The meter's sentence is `agreementSentence`; a failed write of the advice never fails the decision.
- 🔴 **ONE UNIT, A DECISION — THE HEADER, THE LANES, THE REVIEW BUTTON AND NEEDS YOU SAY THE SAME NUMBER** (`placeCheckDesk`, `deskHeadline`, `deskMakeUp`; the unit is `needsYouDecisionCount`'s). A single clinic is one; a building is one however many customers wait behind it; a building in use with nobody waiting is none. It is counted BEFORE the filters. "N over 72 hours" is `deskLongWait` on `WAITING_LONG_HOURS`. Never add a second count beside it.
- 🔒 **THE PAGE HAS FOUR VERBS AND NO OTHERS — APPROVE, REJECT, APPROVE AFTER ALL, REJECT AFTER ALL — AND NO PLACE-CHECK SCREEN SAYS REFUSE** (`PLACE_CHECK_VERBS`, `PLACE_CHECK_RETIRED_VERBS`; "after all" is his word of 14 Nov 2026). 🔴 There is NO "New photo" button and NO "Take back" button: a rejection with its reason IS the ask for a new photo, and a decision is taken back with the other verb. Every bulk action is one of the four; a row's label is the verb alone, never "Approve this validation". The stamp reads "Rejected by" (`decision-stamp.ts`). "refused at the door" is the system's own refusal of a capture, a different thing, and stays.
- 🔴 **THE READY LANE'S ONE PRESS IS THE SAME ANSWER N TIMES, THROUGH THE TWO ROUTES A ROW'S OWN APPROVE POSTS TO** (`readyLaneRequests`: each building, then its waiting customers, then the clinics; `READY_BUILDING_ROUTE`, `READY_REVIEW_ROUTE`; `ready-lane-bar.tsx`). It holds the tabs, the filters and every row's buttons while it runs, like any bulk run. Never give it a route of its own.
- 🔴 **THE SIGN WINS: THE SYSTEM APPROVES A PLACE ONLY THE GOOGLE CHECK HELD WHEN ITS PHOTOGRAPHED SIGN NAMES THE CUSTOMER — AND STOPS BY ITSELF PAST 1 TAKE-BACK IN 20 OVER 30 DAYS** (owner, 23 Nov 2026: "Yes, the sign wins"; he had approved 117 of 117). `signWinsVerdict` (`sign-wins-rules.ts`, pure) leaves: a place with no photograph on file (`hasPhoto`, his 20 Nov "must have a photo except i tell u": a trusted point is his alone), a short name, an open integrity case, an SFE recommendation, a customer seated in a building, a sign or timetable hold, a test entry, anything decided, and anything captured before `SIGN_WINS_STARTED_AT` (the start of 23 Nov 2026, not the deploy instant; an older backlog is a bulk change to announce first). 🔴 The door reads NO sign for a place Google holds (`held_elsewhere`), so after its reply it makes the night's one read of that place first (`signWinsAfterGoogleHold` → `readPlaceSignboardNow`), then asks the rule; all four Google holds of the first day waited for the 03:35 pass and it approved none. It also runs hourly, 25 a run (`runSignWinsSweep`), approves through `approveHeldSignboardBySystem` as the system, marks `system_approved_rule` (written up to three times, because the switch counts marked rows only), only ever says yes, and every read fails closed. The tab "Approved by the system" lists them. Account: `docs/approvals-rebuild-2026-11-place-checks.md`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-sign-wins.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **REJECT IS TWO PRESSES: REJECT OPENS THE REASONS, AND THE REASON PRESSED IS THE REJECTION** (owner, 23 Nov 2026: "Fix them"; it was three). With no reason chosen, Reject draws the closed list as buttons (`RefusalReasonPick`, `refusal-reason-field.tsx`, hint `REFUSAL_PICK_HINT`) and the one pressed sends it with that reason; "Back, reject nothing" closes it. A reason the advice already chose still rejects in one press, and the reason box is drawn only once a reason is chosen, never beside the list. The same on the three lists (a waiting clinic, a building, a building's customer). Never bring back a Reject that needs a reason picked first and then a second Reject. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **ON THE SUPER ADMIN'S PAGE A LIST DRAWS ITS TABLE OR ITS CARDS, NEVER BOTH** (23 Nov 2026: both were always in the page, one hidden by its width class — 5,937 KB of HTML and 118 photographs for 16 waiting places). The page guesses from the request (`placeCheckLayoutGuess`, `place-check-layout.ts`: the browser's `Sec-CH-UA-Mobile` hint, else the phone word in its name) and `PlaceCheckLayoutProvider` hands it down; `usePlaceCheckLayout` corrects it from the screen's own width. 🔴 With no provider a list draws BOTH, as before: a district manager's page and every other screen are unchanged, and the width classes stay on both layouts so a wrong guess is never seen. Never read the width in a list by hand.
- 🔴 **A LANE'S HEAD IS ONE ROW, AND A BUILDING'S CUSTOMERS ARE ROWS OF THE SAME TABLE** (23 Nov 2026). `PlaceCheckSection` given `lane` draws the title, the why-these-are-yours line and the Pick rows slot in one row (`data-lane-head`); without `lane` it is the shell's own head. The first waiting place went from 546 px to 376 px at 1440, measured on the live page's own elements. A building's row (`data-building-row`) is followed by one row per seated customer (`SeatedCustomers` with `layout="table"`, the customer's own photograph and timetable side by side), never a table nested in the building's cell: one polyclinic's row was 8,410 px tall. The page's title band is drawn by the board around its top line (`hero`), so the waiting number stands beside the title.
- 🔒 **THE VIEW LIVES IN THE ADDRESS, AND NO PERSON'S NAME IS EVER WRITTEN TO IT** (`place-check-address.ts`, pure). The tab, the chip, the rows opened and `PLACE_CHECK_ADDRESS_FILTERS` (outcome, period, line, region, area, list) are in the query string, so a reload, Back and a shared link open the same view. The rep, the decider and the search box (`PLACE_CHECK_PRIVATE_FILTERS`) stay in the browser's session store: a reload keeps them, a link does not carry them. Every parameter the file does not know is left alone (`review` opens the one-by-one review); an unreadable value is the default, never an error; a list opens no further than `PLACE_CHECK_ADDRESS_MAX_ROWS`.
- 🔒 **A PANEL DRAWS NO TABLE, AND EVERY FILE OF THE BOARD IS IN THE LIST THE SOURCE PINS READ** A panel sets its lists in `PlaceCheckSection` (`place-checks/section.tsx`); each list owns its table AND its phone cards (`CrmCardsOnPhone`), which the lanes test reads list by list. 🔴 A new file under `place-checks/` goes into `tests/support/place-check-board-source.ts` in the same change, or the rule it carries is pinned by nothing (the test refuses one that is missing). No file of the board is over 400 lines: split it, never grow it. On a phone the tabs are one dropdown and a lane pages by ten (`PAGE_LANE`). 🔴 **A phone card of a clinic or a building is CLOSED until pressed** (`PhoneFold`, `place-checks/phone-fold.tsx`): who and what on the card, its photographs, evidence and controls under it, the same ones, drawn once. Paging alone left the page 50,990 px long at 375 with one polyclinic 14,000 px (measured in the preview, 23 Nov 2026). The system's word on a closed card is the Super Admin's alone, on a waiting row only. Never fold the desktop table.
