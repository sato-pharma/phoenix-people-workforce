- 🔴 **THE CV AND THE APPLICATION ARE ON THE HIRING JOURNEY AT EVERY STEP, FOR EVERYONE WHO OPENS IT** (owner, 7 Nov 2026: "the cv and application must appear and be appearing in all steps for every one") — `MrCvApplication` in `mr-journey-drawer.tsx` — 🔴 THREE PANES from 1024px (owner, 7 Nov 2026): the CV left, the application and the WhatsApp replies in the middle, the journey right as it was (`layout="panes"`); one column under the current step on a narrow screen, read from `/api/recruitment/mr-hiring/[candidateId]/cv`, which admits exactly the journey's readers (`readableMrRecord` → `mayReadMrJourney`).
