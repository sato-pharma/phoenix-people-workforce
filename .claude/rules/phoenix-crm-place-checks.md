## Rules

- 🔴 **THE PLACE CHECKS PAGE READS EVERY ROW, PAGED** — 598 of 643 validated places had never been looked at when it showed a capped slice (2026-10-08). Every read goes through `readEveryPage` / `selectByIds`; `capped` tells the board when a read hit its ceiling. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-checks-in-overflow.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **EVERY DISTRICT MANAGER HAS HIS OWN `Place checks` TAB — HIS TEAM'S BOARD, AND HE DECIDES NOTHING ON IT** (owner, 2026-10-13: "in every district manager account, build a tab in his sidebar that shows the check places only for his team"). `readsTeamPlaceChecks` is `{DM, ACTING_DM}` ALONE — deliberately narrower than `readsPlaceVsActual`, which also admits the NSM and the SFE Manager. 🔴 **THAT PAIR IS A RULING, NOT AN OVERSIGHT: he was put the choice the day it shipped and answered "keep place checks to district managers only"** (2026-10-13), so never widen it because the place-vs-actual report is wider, because an RSM or NSM asks why he cannot see his districts' boards, or because the code's own "add a tier only on his word" reads as an open question — only his word, naming the rung, changes it. All four section reads narrow IN THE QUERY through `placeChecksSeatScope`, which THROWS rather than returning `[]`: a PostgREST `.in()` on an empty array answers zero rows with no error, so a forbidden viewer would read an empty board as "nothing waiting". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-notes.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **Nothing on his screen says who decides** — no decision control, no "recommendation", no "waiting for approval", no name or role above him (his own words the same day: "don't show this on the screen"); `canDecideValidationReviews` is unchanged and still the Super Admin alone. 🔴 **He reads the SYSTEM's evidence AND THE DEVICE'S on this board** — owner, 2026-10-13: "system and device evidence the dm can see", reversing his own answer an hour earlier; `loadPlaceCheckIntegrity` (in `server/integrity-board.ts`, where a read of the evidence belongs) serves it, gated by the same `placeChecksSeatScope`. Everywhere ELSE is unchanged — `/admin/phoenix-crm/integrity` is still the Super Admin's and the SFE's, the place-vs-actual report still shows him the DISTANCE only, and the rep is still told nothing. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-notes.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A COMMENT ON A PLACE CHECK CARRIES ITS OWN VISIBILITY, AND A REP IS TOLD A ROLE AND NEVER A NAME** (owner, 2026-10-13). `phoenix_crm_place_notes.rep_visible` is NOT NULL with NO DEFAULT and the wire demands a boolean, so the two buttons — `Visible to medical rep` / `Not visible to medical rep` — are the only way to save one. A visible note reaches the rep on the customer through `placeNoteRepAuthorText` ("From the System Administrator" / "From your manager"), filtered on `rep_visible` IN THE QUERY because a field a browser receives counts as shown, and SILENTLY: no bell, no Telegram, no email, no badge (his pick, asked directly). The subject's seat is re-read from the place's own row, never taken from the request. 🔴 **Neither recommendation unique index is partial** — PostgREST's `on_conflict=` emits bare column names and Postgres infers a partial index only when the inference spec repeats the predicate, so `where place_id is not null` would fail every save; the subject CHECK makes the plain indexes correct.
- 🔒 **AN APPROVED VALIDATION CAN BE REJECTED ON ITS OWN ROW, AND THE ADMIN'S FREE NOTE IS OPTIONAL EVERYWHERE ON THIS PAGE** (owner, 2026-10-15: *"make admin able to reject validation even if approved before and admin comment is optional"*). 22 of the module's first 24 refusals carried the single character `-` in the then-mandatory note; the closed REASON the rep is told stays required. `20261015212903` widens ONE guard in `phoenix_crm_decide_validation_review` — a `reject` on an `approved` review passes, every other re-decision still raises `phoenix_crm:review_decided` — and drops `v_note is null or` from `phoenix_crm_restore_validation`, keeping its 500-character cap. 🔴 **Take an approval back THERE, never through `refuseValidatedPlace`**: that path upserts the review's `google` column to `refused_by_admin` and loses the evidence the system held it on. `refusable` is `status === "approved" && place.status === "active"`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **SEVERAL ROWS ARE TICKED AND ANSWERED TOGETHER, AND A BULK ANSWER IS THE SAME ANSWER N TIMES — NEVER A SECOND DECISION PATH** (owner, 2026-10-15: *"also make me able to multiple select and response"*). `place-check-bulk.tsx` (`useRowPicks` + `BulkDecideBar`) runs each section's OWN row request, one row at a time, with one reason and one note typed for all of them — so the rep reads exactly what a one-row refusal tells him and the audit rows are the same rows. 🔴 **What is ticked is intersected with the rows ON SCREEN at every render**, so a filter change or a row settling under you can never decide a place the admin cannot see, and the validated list ticks only the current PAGE, never the rows behind Show more. The reason pick-list follows the picked photographs (`schedule` only when every one of them is); a district manager's board (`mode === "team"`) draws none of it. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A REJECTED POLYCLINIC IS BROUGHT BACK ON ITS OWN ROW, AND WHICH SEATED PLACES RETURN IS DECIDED BY `retired_at`** (owner, 2026-10-15: *"make me able to approve validation after rejection"*). A rejected PLACE always had `restoreValidation`; a VENUE had no undo at all, and `loadSharedPlaceBoard` read `status.in.(active,held)` — so 35 retired venues on production were not even on the screen. The board now also reads retired venues inside `RESTORABLE_SHOWN_DAYS` (90), each row carrying `restorable`, and `phoenix_crm_restore_shared_place` (`20261015220318`) refuses anything but a venue still `retired` (`phoenix_crm:shared_place_not_retired`), un-retires exactly the places carrying the venue's OWN `retired_at` — one transaction, so that timestamp is all that separates them from a place retired earlier for its own reason — and confirms each customer under the three holds the approve branch keeps. 🔴 **The rep is told nothing; his notice is withdrawn by his own validation, by prefix.** Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A POLYCLINIC IS ONE GROUP ON ITS OWN TAB; A CUSTOMER NOT ON ITS TIMETABLE IS REJECTED ON THEIR OWN ROW** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"separate the polyclinics/hospitals checks than normal clinic steps … grouped with the drs in it … reject a dr not in the time table"*). `validation-reviews-board.tsx` gives them their own tab (one of five since the same evening, below); `shared-place-groups.ts` files each seated customer under their building from the four sets, and `withoutSeated` drops them from the clinics view ONLY when that building is on the board. The reject is the existing refusal on the timetable reasons (`kind="schedule"`, opening on `schedule_other_name`): the review route when they have a review, `/refuse` when the system never questioned them. 🔴 **A manager's board draws none of it.** Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place-groups.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "Polyclinics on their own tab".
- 🔒 **THE "SAME PLACE, SEVERAL REPS" TAB SETS EVERY PLACE SEVERAL REPS VALIDATED SIDE BY SIDE — THE SUPER ADMIN'S ALONE, AND A PAIR IS ASKED ONLY IN THE MIDDLE BAND, NEVER ABOUT TWO DIFFERENTLY NAMED CUSTOMERS** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"a place validated by more than a rep … if you are in doubt, ask — but not every place; what is clear and confident, put it direct"*). `place-match-groups.ts`: within about 30–60 m (set by the two phones' own accuracy) is grouped with no question, beyond 100–200 m is never shown, the photograph is the anchor, and a group is tight (120 m; his own *same* is never capped). "Most accurate" is named only when it beats the next by a fifth, else *too close to call*; an outlier needs a third rep. 🔴 A seated customer is never a subject, and the tab is never drawn in `mode="team"`. Answers live on the ordered pair in `phoenix_crm_place_match_answers`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-match-groups.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "Same place, several reps".
- 🔒 **PLACE CHECKS IS A REPORT OF FIVE TABS, ONLY THE OPEN ONE DRAWN, EVERY LIST PAGED, AND NO TEST ENTRY ANYWHERE** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"is this a report that really show me data? or its a bunch of chaos"*; the Clinics tab was 104,455 px tall). Needs you (a manager's: Being checked) · Not confirmed · Polyclinics and hospitals · Same place, several reps (company only) · History. `<Paged>` holds 20 rows or 10 buildings and is keyed by the filters; `isReal` drops `isTest` from all five sets, and both pages' stat cards count what the board draws (no test entry, no rejected building). Every building is one card: name, kind, status, who captured it and when, address; then its sign and its timetable, large and side by side; then its customers. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place-groups.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-place-check-scorecard.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "Place checks, rebuilt as a report".
- 🔒 **A SCORECARD COUNT OPENS ITS OWN PHOTOGRAPHS, AND ON BOTH LISTS THE PHOTO IS THE ROW** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"don't put for me non-readable … make me see what is the unreadable. Every count but Places and Confirmed is a button (`targetOf`, `Count`); `placeOfTarget` on the board sets that rep, clears the outcome, and opens the tab and narrowing that hold exactly those places — a customer seated in a polyclinic is pointed to his building's card, never repeated. Pinned by `checkCountsOpenTheirPhotos` in `tests/phoenix-crm-place-check-scorecard.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "A count opens its photographs".
- 🔒 **EVERY VALIDATION CARRIES THE SYSTEM'S RECOMMENDATION AND ITS REASON — ON HIS BOARD ONLY, AND IT DECIDES NOTHING** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"add system recommendations to approve or no on every vlidation"*, then *"and the reason of system recommendation"*). `place-check-advice.ts` (pure) answers approve, reject or look yourself with one sentence of evidence; `system-advice.tsx` draws it on the four row screens and the bulk bar.
