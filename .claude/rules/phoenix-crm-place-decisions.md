## Rules

- 🔴 **A SEND-BACK KEEPS EVERY VISIT'S EARLIER DISTANCE — A REJECTED PLACE, A HAND REFUSAL OR A REJECTED BUILDING MEASURES NO VISIT AGAIN** (owner, 19 Nov 2026: "Keep earlier distances"). The re-measure ran against the places the customer had left, so a doctor with a second clinic had every visit at the refused one turned "far" (4 visits, then 10, put back from a backup by hand that night). `decideValidationReview`, `refuseValidatedPlace` and `decideSharedPlace` call no restamp; their line copies follow with `{ retired: true, keepDistances: true }`. Taking the rejection back (`restoreValidation`, `restoreSharedPlace`) still measures again, and so do a MOVE and a mover's REMOVAL (`retirePlace`): those change where the customer is. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-line-twins.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-audit-reviews.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A DECIDED ROW STAYS DECIDED ON SCREEN, AND "ALREADY DECIDED" IS AN ANSWER, NEVER A RED ERROR** (owner, 2026-11-08: "places checks is stale and slow and many error"; the SFE Manager met "already decided" three times that morning). `useSavedOutcome` keeps its mark in the tab-wide `SAVED_MARKS`, so a remounted list (tab, filter, Show more) never brings a decided row's buttons back; every decision control and the PM full names settle on `isAlreadyDecided` (`place-check-done.tsx`). The full names decide through `placeCheckReread`, never a `router.refresh()` per press. The admin page starts its notes and evidence on `subjectReads`, never after all five reads. Pinned by `tests/place-review-deck.test.ts` (4c).
- 🔒 **THE SFE MANAGER READS THE COMPANY BOARD AND RECOMMENDS; THE SUPER ADMIN ALONE DECIDES** (owner, 2026-11-19: *"make Khaled Bedair only able to recommend validation or not recommend, never able to take a decision, and the recommended by him goes separately in a separate page for admin decision"*, narrowing his 2026-10-19 widening). `readsCompanyPlaceChecks` (Super Admin on `update`, SFE Manager on `approve`) opens the board; `canDecideValidationReviews` is the Super Admin alone and gates every decision service; `recommendsOnCompanyPlaceChecks` writes his two buttons as `writer_role = 'sfe_manager'` (`20261119081316`). A check he recommended on leaves the Super Admin's board for `/admin/field-crm/sfe-recommendations` by ONE rule (`sfe-recommendations.ts`). The routes stay on `approve`; the services refuse him. 🔴 **He holds read + approve and NO `update` — never hand him `update` to clear a red gate**, it opens every other `update` route to him. 🔴 **Not widened, each its own ruling:** taking a rejection back (`canRestoreValidation`, 2026-11-10), seating in a polyclinic (`canDecideSharedPlaces`), settling an integrity case, the new-hold notice (Super Admin only) and `readsTeamPlaceChecks`. 🔴 **The device's evidence IS his on THIS screen** (owner, 2026-10-19: "show the mock-location evidence to the SFE Manager too") — `loadPlaceCheckIntegrity` has no tier, because its seat scope already throws for anybody else — and it is STILL the Super Admin's alone on the integrity board and the visit report (`readsPositionCases`). Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-sfe-place-checks.test.ts`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-sfe-recommendations.test.ts` too. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "The SFE Manager decides on the company board" and its 2026-11-19 amendment.
- 🔒 **EVERY APPROVAL OR REFUSAL ON THE COMPANY BOARD NAMES ITS DECIDER AND CARRIES A TIMESTAMP — ON THE LISTS THAT KEPT THE OUTCOME BUT DROPPED THE NAME** (owner, 2026-10-20: *"in check places I want every approval or refusal by Khaled Bedair to appear to me — by Khaled Bedair and timestamp"*). The database always held `decided_by_hr_id` and `decided_at` (A-2 made 9 decisions on 19 Oct, 14:40–14:46 Cairo), and only the Decided list drew them. Now a place in use says `Approved by <name> · <time>`, a customer waiting to be captured again says `Refused by <name> · <time>` (a venue's refusal reads the venue's own decider), and a seated customer says either; one pure function, `decisionStamp`, so the three lists use the same words. 🔴 **Company mode only** — a district manager's board names nobody above him, and the rep never reaches these lists. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place-groups.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **AN APPROVED VALIDATION CAN BE REJECTED ON ITS OWN ROW, AND THE ADMIN'S FREE NOTE IS OPTIONAL EVERYWHERE ON THIS PAGE** (owner, 2026-10-15: *"make admin able to reject validation even if approved before and admin comment is optional"*). 22 of the module's first 24 refusals carried the single character `-` in the then-mandatory note; the closed REASON the rep is told stays required. `20261015212903` widens ONE guard in `phoenix_crm_decide_validation_review` — a `reject` on an `approved` review passes, every other re-decision still raises `phoenix_crm:review_decided` — and drops `v_note is null or` from `phoenix_crm_restore_validation`, keeping its 500-character cap. 🔴 **Take an approval back THERE, never through `refuseValidatedPlace`**: that path upserts the review's `google` column to `refused_by_admin` and loses the evidence the system held it on. `refusable` is `status === "approved" && place.status === "active"`, and not his approval (next rule). Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **WHAT THE SUPER ADMIN APPROVED, ONLY THE SUPER ADMIN REJECTS — THE SFE MANAGER AND EVERYONE ELSE ARE REFUSED** (owner, 2026-11-16: *"when super admin approve something sfe or whoever cannot reject it back bec its approved by admin"*). His approval = a review a Super Admin decided `approved`, a place a Super Admin created (a trusted point), or an active building a Super Admin approved. Both decision functions raise `phoenix_crm:approved_by_super_admin` (migration `20261116230741`); the screen reads the same list (`phoenix_crm_super_admin_hr_ids`) and shows one sentence where Reject was; 🔴 `refuseValidatedPlace` asks BEFORE its upsert. He is never blocked. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "What the Super Admin approved is final". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-super-admin-approval-is-final.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **ONLY THE SUPER ADMIN APPROVES A VALIDATION AFTER IT WAS REJECTED, AND NOTHING BLOCKS HIM — NEVER THE SFE MANAGER** (owner, 2026-11-10: *"make the administrator able to approve a rejected validation from the SFE in place checks but the SFE cannot; the administrator is never blocked"*; on a short name, his pick "Both"). `canRestoreValidation` (Super Admin on `update`, never a test account) gates `restoreValidation` and `restoreSharedPlace`; the routes keep `approve` as gate one. The screen asks `PlaceCheckRestoreProvider` (`place-check-restore.tsx`, default FALSE): the SFE Manager sees who refused it and no button, no tick, no bulk take-back, and no sentence promising one (14 Nov 2026: worded by `canRestore`, never `mode`; `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "No sentence on his board promises a take-back"). A short name never greys his "Approve it after all" out: he approves as it stands, or **Edit name** sends `nameParts` and `phoenix_crm_restore_validation` (`20261110214512`) renames the customer in the same transaction. The rep still writes three names before a visit saves. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-sfe-place-checks.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **SEVERAL ROWS ARE TICKED AND ANSWERED TOGETHER, AND A BULK ANSWER IS THE SAME ANSWER N TIMES — NEVER A SECOND DECISION PATH** (owner, 2026-10-15: *"also make me able to multiple select and response"*). `place-check-bulk.tsx` (`useRowPicks` + `BulkDecideBar`) runs each section's OWN row request, one row at a time, with one reason and one note typed for all of them — so the rep reads exactly what a one-row refusal tells him and the audit rows are the same rows. 🔴 **What is ticked is intersected with the rows ON SCREEN at every render**, so a filter change or a row settling under you can never decide a place the admin cannot see, and the validated list ticks only the current PAGE, never the rows behind Show more. The reason pick-list follows the picked photographs (`schedule` only when every one of them is); a district manager's board (`mode === "team"`) draws none of it. The run says what each answer did (`bulkOutcomeSummary`, 14 Nov 2026): "Approved, waits for the building", never plain. 🔴 An "already decided" row counts as settled, and a run locks the board's tabs (`useBulkRunGoing`, 17 Nov 2026). Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A REJECTED POLYCLINIC IS BROUGHT BACK ON ITS OWN ROW, AND WHICH SEATED PLACES RETURN IS DECIDED BY `retired_at`** (owner, 2026-10-15: *"make me able to approve validation after rejection"*). A rejected PLACE always had `restoreValidation`; a VENUE had no undo at all, and `loadSharedPlaceBoard` read `status.in.(active,held)` — so 35 retired venues on production were not even on the screen. The board now also reads retired venues inside `RESTORABLE_SHOWN_DAYS` (90), each row carrying `restorable`, and `phoenix_crm_restore_shared_place` (`20261015220318`) refuses anything but a venue still `retired` (`phoenix_crm:shared_place_not_retired`), un-retires exactly the places carrying the venue's OWN `retired_at` — one transaction, so that timestamp is all that separates them from a place retired earlier for its own reason — and confirms each customer under the three holds the approve branch keeps. 🔴 **The rep is told nothing; his notice is withdrawn by his own validation, by prefix.** Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **HE APPROVES A PLACE THE SYSTEM COULD NOT CONFIRM, ON ITS OWN ROW OR TICKED, AND THE SYSTEM'S OWN APPROVALS READ "The system"** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"make me able to approve"* on Not confirmed). `approveValidatedPlace` is the deciders' alone (the Super Admin and the SFE Manager), needs an active place, leaves a decided one as it is, and otherwise raises or reuses the place's ONE review row as `confirmed_by_admin` and runs the one approve. Needs you shows "The system is still reading this sign" while a held sign has reads left (`systemStillReading`). A manager's board approves nothing. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-held-signboard.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "The door reader and the held re-read".
- 🔴 **A SAVED DECISION RELEASES ITS BUTTON AT ONCE — NEVER "Approving…" UNTIL THE PAGE RE-READS** (owner, 2026-10-24: *"when i press approve its staled do approving and grey out to forever"*). The page is the heaviest read in the module, so a slow or failed `router.refresh()` left the button grey over a decision the database already held. Every decision control wraps its report as `markSaved(done("…"))`, clears its own `saving` on success, and draws `PlaceCheckSaved` while `useSavedOutcome(row)` still sees the row as it was pressed; a re-read that changes the row hands it back to the server view, undo included. Pinned by `tests/place-review-deck.test.ts` (4b).
- 🔒 **"DECIDED BY" NARROWS THE COMPANY BOARD TO ONE DECIDER, AND READS EVERY DECISION THEY EVER MADE — NOT THE 14-DAY WINDOW** (owner, 2026-10-26: *"i want to be able to view only approved by me or only by khaled bedair"*). The admin page calls `loadValidationReviews(viewer, { everyDecision: true })`; each row says `recent`, the stat cards count only those, and the board shows the older ones only while a decider is chosen — the 14-day read would have hidden 86 of his 480. `decidedByMatches` (pure) drops every row nobody decided, so waiting places and held buildings vanish under a name; "The system" is a decider of its own. A manager's board (`mode="team"`) draws no box and keeps the narrow read. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-review-filters.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A COMMENT WRITTEN WITH A DECISION REACHES THE REP AND THEIR MANAGER, ALWAYS, AND SILENTLY** (owner, 2026-11-01: *"when sfe or admin reject or approve validation and write the comment this comment appear to the rep and his maanger"*). `tellDecisionComment` (`server/place-notes.ts`) runs on all six decision paths, through `savePlaceNote` with `repVisible: true`. The rep reads "From the System Administrator", never a name; the manager's board gets the role alone (`managerBoard`). No bell, Telegram or email. A building comment sits on the card of every customer seated there, and for a building nobody is seated in, inside To validate for 30 days (`emptyBuildingNotes`), never above the chooser. The older ones were copied once (owner, 2026-11-01): 51 are visible; the system's own lines, the clean-up lines, a bare "-" and those naming a colleague stay in `decision_note`. A private note still goes through the Comment box. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-notes.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A SENT-BACK CUSTOMER IS FILED UNDER A REAL REP, NEVER "A rep"** (owner, 2026-11-14: *"fix the 'A rep' name on both boards"*). A customer sent back by a clean-up has no refusal to name anybody (42 on production that day), and every one fell into one "A rep" line on the scorecard. `sentBackRepName` (`server/validation-reviews.ts`): the refusal's rep, then the customer's own `created_by_hr_id`, then the seat's territory; "A rep" only when none is on file. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-audit-reviews.test.ts` (I).
