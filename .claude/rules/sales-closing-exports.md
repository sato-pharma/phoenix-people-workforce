## Rules

🔴 **`sales-closing` KEEPS EVERY GLOB IT HAD, so an export file loads the PAIR** — every money and golden rule (the closing source of truth, the city rule, El Qema, the Office respread, the August dating rules) stays THERE and still arrives. Nothing in this file decides a number: a figure an export prints is the closing engine's, and a surface that re-derives one is the bug.

Absent on a line with no Office channel, and absent under a ROW-LEVEL filter (distributor, inclusion, search), because `parts.office` is an aggregate that cannot be narrowed that way and subtracting the whole month's Office would print negative sales (`officeNarrowedWithMatrix`). `src/lib/sales/closing-matrix-without-office.ts`, pinned by `tests/closing-matrix-no-office.test.ts`.

- 🔒 **EVERY DOWNLOADED CLOSING WORKBOOK IS SHEET-PROTECTED ON EVERY TAB, PREMIUM AND NORMAL — NO OPTION TURNS IT OFF** (owner, 2026-10-13: "never be able to edit any cell in any tab"). Pinned by `tests/closing-workbook-lock.test.ts` + the two builder suites; account `docs/environment.md`.
- 🔒 **THE RANKINGS TAB SHOWS THE WHOLE LINE AND OWNS NO ARITHMETIC — a rank is meaningless without its filter** (`/sales/rankings`). Every per-line panel renders EVERY ranked rep; a `.slice` or a "Top 5" is refused (owner, 2026-10-03 — the bottom of a line is the half a manager acts on). The filter DECIDES the population, a multi-month window is summed then divided ONCE (averaging ratios made 1200/1100 read 205%, not 109%), figures come only from `loadAchievementRange` + `loadPublishedPeriodKeys` gated per `lineCode|period`, and "out of N" is organogram SEATS that go **null** under any title/region/name filter. Account — his words, who sees it, and the container-query fit: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The Rankings tab". Pinned by `tests/sales-rankings.test.ts` + `scripts/measure-rankings-fit.mjs`.
- **The Performance Review series reuses the headline's read through `byPeriod` — single-month UNMERGED matrices only.** `loadSeriesMatrices` reuses a month only when its own period AND line match; never put the folded multi-month `combined` in `byPeriod`, or one bar carries every selected month. Pinned by `tests/closing-performance-review-series-reuse.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE PERFORMANCE REVIEW'S PER-REP UNITS-BY-MONTH TAB IS ONE BLOCK PER PRODUCT CODE — that product's months and its growth inside the block, and NO row total** (owner, 2026-10-09: "all products per product per rep per unit per month … the Royafloxacin will be merged and under it july august september and the gross"). Rows are reps and areas, with region subtotals and the national total: those sum ONE product down a column, which is the only units sum that is real. A row total would add units of different products and the workbook builder refuses it. `productUnitsByRepByMonth`, sheet `Reps · Products by Month`, last on screen and in the workbook — the two stay in the same order. Pinned by `tests/closing-performance-review.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE PERFORMANCE REVIEW AND THE CONSOLIDATED CLOSING ARE ONE PIPELINE IN TWO ORDERS — the review narrows the FOLD, the workbook folds the NARROWED months, and every number must still agree** (confirmed on production 2026-10-09: three lines over Jul+Aug+Sep, Pedia over Jul+Sep, CNS Aug+Sep narrowed to Alexandria — 0 differences). They agree only because `filterMonthlyClosingMatrix` and `mergeMonthlyClosingMatrices` BOTH re-derive region subtotals and the national total from the surviving AREA rows, and because the split is one computation (`loadClosingStoreSplitNet` is a field of `loadClosingStoreSplit`) — a second roll-up anywhere in either path breaks it silently. 🔴 **The review's headline is the AREA grand total, never the workbook's "Grand Total (incl. Direct)" line** — the same number today because `directNational` is null in all 27 of 2026's line-months, two different rows the day a national direct bucket appears. Pinned by `tests/closing-consolidated-export.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A FILTERED PERFORMANCE REVIEW CLOSES WITH "FILTERED TOTAL" — "NATIONAL TOTAL" IS A CLAIM ABOUT COVERAGE AND ON A NARROWED VIEW IT IS FALSE** (owner, 2026-10-09). It is the figure a manager quotes off the bottom of the sheet, and it said NATIONAL on all five total rows however hard the view was filtered — three areas of fifty-eight under a row claiming to be the line. ONE helper decides the wording, `reviewTotalRowLabel` in `closing-performance-review-regions.ts` (the dependency-free module, because the workspace is a CLIENT component and `closing-performance-review.ts` drags `node:crypto` into the bundle), and `scopeLabel` GATES on the same predicate — so the masthead's "filtered · 1 of 2 areas" and the row's own words cannot drift; its parts decide the wording, never whether the sheet counts as filtered. 🔴 **A PRODUCT-only filter counts too:** every area is still on the sheet, but their figures are no longer the whole line's.
