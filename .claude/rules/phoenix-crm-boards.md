- 🔒 **A VISIT'S `captured_at` IS WHEN HE SAVED IT, NOT WHEN HE WAS AT THE DOOR — so no integrity rule may read it as a position in time** (owner, 2026-10-02, asked directly with the evidence). The one-tap visit (2026-09-29) removed the door photo whose slot used to date a visit, and he may log his day until **02:00**; so "five visits inside five minutes", "a metronome" and "the whole day inside an hour" describe how he LOGS — the morning from the car, the day at night — and an honest rep trips all three. **They stay computed, are NEVER strong, and reach the board only BESIDE a strong flag** (`PHOENIX_CRM_SAVE_TIME_BEHAVIOUR_FLAGS`; alone they sit in `signals.heldBack` and nobody sees them). The one strong behaviour rule left is `identical_durations` — the form's own stopwatch, which one tap does not change. 🔴 **Never put either back in `PHOENIX_CRM_STRONG_BEHAVIOUR_FLAGS`, and never raise the burst bound instead**: there is no number of saves in five minutes an honest rep cannot reach. The three labels say **saved**, because "more visits than a person can enter" is now false. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-behaviour-integrity.test.ts` §11.
- 🔴 **EVERY SWEEP READ IS PAGED, AND THE EVIDENCE IS FILED ON THE VISIT DAY** (audit, 2026-10-02). `.limit()` above **1,000** does nothing — PostgREST stops there with no error — so the behaviour pass judged the first ~1,000 visits of a month company-wide (Delta (1) alone makes ~3,700) and the photo corpus compared each picture with only the newest 1,000 fingerprints. Use `readEveryPage` (`server/paged-read.ts`) with a TOTAL order; a JUDGED read refuses past its ceiling, the newest-first corpus stops. The unpaged **places** read was the dangerous one: a seat with no areas makes `flat_coverage_no_sales` accuse a rep who covered his list and sold behind it. 🔴 **And the fingerprint backlog requires `photo_path is not null`** (applied to production 2026-10-02) — a photo-less one-tap visit can never clear the anti-join, so after ~400 of them the nightly sweep hashes nothing and still reports success. Day grouping and `visit_date` use **`cairoBusinessDay`**, never a hand-rolled Intl format. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-sweep-paging.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **MOCK-LOCATION DETECTION IS SILENT — a spoofed capture SAVES, and the rep is NEVER told** (owner, 2026-09-25: "close this hole whithout reps knowing"). Never add a refusal, a warning, an error or ANY field a rep can see: a message teaches the one person you caught what to defeat. Android's `mocked` flag is recorded and the server proves the rest alone (replay · travel · zero jitter · accuracy · precision), so a patched build, the web and iOS are still covered — **do NOT "improve" it into a package/APK scan**, which needs `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, dies to a rename and detects installation rather than use. Evidence lives in `phoenix_crm_location_integrity` (RLS, no policy, service role only), read ONLY by the Super Admin page `/admin/phoenix-crm/integrity`; a flag is where to look, never a verdict and never an action. Thresholds are set so an honest rep cannot trip them — **a false positive accuses a real person of fraud, which is worse than a missed catch**. `src/lib/phoenix-crm/location-integrity.ts`, pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-location-integrity.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **EVERY TOUCH IN PHOENIX CRM IS SAVED WHETHER OR NOT THE REP SAVED ANYTHING — `phoenix_crm_touch_trail`, readable only above him** (owner, 2026-10-03: "every click or every touch on the touch screen for this module to be saved in a separate audit trail … even if the rep didn't save it … like a file manager grabber. Anything touches him, automatically saved"). `src/components/phoenix-crm/touch-trail-collector.tsx` is mounted once over each half of the module by its own `layout.tsx` and records press · tap · type · change · focus · submit · screen · scroll · hide · show · offline · online, with the element, its words, the field's contents, the point on the glass and three clocks. 🔴 **Golden rule #1 holds MECHANICALLY: it returns null, every listener is `{capture, passive}`, it never `preventDefault`s and never throws, and `/api/phoenix-crm/touch-trail` answers 204 on EVERY path** — a readable body is what would tell a rep it exists. The actor is the session's (`personKey(access.hrId)`); a body may only ADD `is_test`, never clear it. 🔴 **A password-type, autocomplete-named or NAMED field's value is dropped IN THE BROWSER**, length kept — everything else he types is kept on purpose, the comment he abandoned included. **Typing is coalesced after 800 ms of quiet and scroll to 1/s — the only two things not literal**, and there is no keystroke listener at all. 🔴 **A LABEL IS THE CONTROL'S WORDS, NEVER THE CARD'S** (owner, 2026-10-03: "trim the touch trail label to the control, not the whole card") — a press finding no interactive owner is recorded against the container under the finger, whose `innerText` is every descendant's text joined, so a container labels as its FIRST LINE capped at 48 (`containerLabelOf`) while an element that is itself interactive keeps the full 160. A tighter cap is NOT the fix: it keeps the start of line one plus whatever of line two fits, reading as a sentence nobody saw. The table is deliberately UNKEYED (nine presses are nine rows) with a `bigint` identity for keyset paging. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-touch-trail.test.ts`. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The Phoenix CRM touch trail".
- 🔒 **THE TRAIL IS READ AT `/admin/field-crm/touch-trail`, BY THE SUPER ADMIN ALONE** (owner, 2026-10-04: "build the touch trail reader for the Super Admin"). `canReadTouchTrail` gates the page AND both loaders — not the SFE Manager, not the CEO, never a test persona — because the table holds every pair of hands on the module including a manager's, and what a rep typed and then DELETED. 🔴 **EVERY READ IS KEYSET-PAGED ON THE `bigint id`: PostgREST stops at 1,000 rows with no error and no marker, and a field day is 1,000–3,000 events per rep, so a plain `.select()` answers with the morning and looks exactly like the whole day.** No `range` and no `offset` — over a table still being written to they repeat AND skip. The day is read WHOLE and narrowed in memory, or the people filter could never be widened again. `ms_since_load` orders a page load and `occurred_at` never does (forty events share one arrival, and an offline-queued event arrives last). Only the MINIMUM gap to `device_at` says anything about the phone's clock. The reader cannot write at all. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-touch-trail-reader.test.ts`. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "Reading the touch trail".
- 🔒 **THE SFE READS A REP'S REFUSED READINGS ON `/admin/phoenix-crm/refused-readings` — HOUSEKEEPING, NOT A FLAG, AND NEVER A TAB OF LOCATION INTEGRITY** (owner, 2026-10-03: *"build the refused readings view for the SFE"*). The Super Admin and the SFE Manager, a 404 for anybody else; **the phone's mock-location answer is the Super Admin's alone and is never QUERIED for the SFE** (`readsMockAnswer`). Per rep, last 14 days: customers refused on and how many he later SAVED — a save BEFORE the refusal proves nothing, a refusal tied to no customer is never counted as stuck, nine retries are ONE blocked customer. Read-only; the rep is told nothing new. Only the recorder and this reader may name the table. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The refused-readings board". `a-refused-crm-save-is-traceable-only-in-the-upload-slot`
