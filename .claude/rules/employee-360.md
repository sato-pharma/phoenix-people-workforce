## Rules

- 🔴 **THE TEAM "PLANS SUBMITTED" AND "PLANS APPROVED" TILES COUNT THE REPS' PHOENIX CRM WEEKS, ONCE PER WEEK, AND NEVER `employee_plan_headers`, WHICH A MEDICAL REP CANNOT FILE** (owner, 2026-10-19). The rule lives in `countTeamPlanCompliance` (`src/lib/employee-360/team-plan-compliance.ts`). A week that was sent and is now submitted, approved or returned counts as submitted; a draft never does. Approved means the manager approved the week. A week is credited to the team rep who sent it, or else to the rep holding the seat now, and test weeks are left out. Until then the tiles read 0 for every field team. Since 2026-10-20 (owner) the team is the reps AND the district and regional managers beneath the subject (`selectTeamLifecycleMembers`), never the subject himself; a manager's week is his Phoenix CRM manager week, read through `manager-plan-readers.ts`, where `first_approved` is sent, not approved. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-team-plan-compliance.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A PRODUCT, GROUP PRODUCT OR MARKETING MANAGER SEES HIS LINE'S FIELD FORCE BELOW HIM IN EMPLOYEE 360 ACROSS DEPARTMENTS, NEVER LEVEL OR ABOVE** (owner, 2026-10-20: "every single role that is applicable to appear in a module — find it and show it"). They sit in Marketing and the field force is in Sales, so the department-safe line rule showed them nobody. `MARKETING_LINE_360_ROLES` in `src/lib/employee-360/access.ts` waives the department test for a FIELD-FORCE subject BELOW the viewer only (`MARKETING_LINE_360_FIELD_REACH`): a Product Manager reaches medical reps, a Group Product Manager or the Marketing Manager reaches reps and District Managers, so the line's Regional and National Sales Managers, and the expense claims their reports carry, never open to marketing (risk review, 2026-10-20; the owner's downward rule, 2026-09-04); a non-field person on the line still needs the same department, the National Sales Manager stays department-safe, and the shared `lineScopeIncludesEmployee` is untouched for every other module. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-access.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE EMPLOYEE 360 MANAGER FILTER KEEPS THE MANAGER AND HIS WHOLE SUBTREE, AND OFFERS EVERY MANAGER ON THE CHAIN** (owner, 2026-10-20). Cards are built once by `buildEmployee360ListItems` (`src/lib/employee-360/list-items.ts`), each carrying `managersAbove` walked by HR id over the whole directory; the client matches through `list-manager-filter.ts` over `src/lib/org/manager-filter.ts`, never `managerName === selected`. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-portal-browse.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE REGIONAL SALES MANAGER IS BACK IN EMPLOYEE 360, AND HIS REPORT DRAWS NO EMPTY SECTION** (owner, 2026-10-20: "bring him back but do not show empty data, tailor the report on him"). This reverses the 2026-07-30 per-person exclusion that the `employee-360-subtree-picker-and-exclusion` note still describes: `EMPLOYEE_360_EXCLUDED_HR_IDS` in `access.ts` is EMPTY, and never re-add him without the owner's word. The sections come from `planEmployee360Sections` (`src/lib/employee-360/report-sections.ts`): a Regional Manager gets leadership, team sales, his own visits and expenses only when each carries data, and never a rep-only section; every other family is unchanged. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-access.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **EMPLOYEE 360 CALLS EACH SOURCE SCREEN'S OWN DOMAIN FUNCTION, NEVER A COPY OF ITS RULE** (owner, 2026-10-27: "fix the employee 360 with all system updates since the module was built"). Built 2026-07-12, it kept private copies of the sales, outstation and CRM rules and drifted from all three. Since then: team sales follows the seats held at each month's end with seat-tenure credit, each manager equal to his Sales vs Target node (a Regional Manager's total leaves out a district with no manager, his pick), and shows PUBLISHED closing months only ("Not published yet" otherwise, his pick — January to June 2026 are hidden too, his second pick the same day, since only July onward is published); expenses total the payable amount, rejected claims left out; manager visits are split by kind through `managerDayFigures`; working days follow `isCallRateWorkingDay`; a failed read shows "—", never 0. A new 360 figure reuses the screen it summarises.
- 🔴 **EVERY EMPLOYEE 360 FIGURE SAYS "NO DATA" RATHER THAN PRINT A ZERO NOBODY READ, AND BOTH SIDES OF A RATIO COVER THE SAME DAYS** (number audit, 2026-10-27). A failed read sets `unavailable` and the section says it could not be read (Team Health, Expenses, the Training Manager); coaching counts completed reports only, a leaver's included; a before/after impact counts an event only when both 30-day spans sit inside what was read, up to yesterday in Cairo; a double visit whose partner has no HR id is still counted (keyed `crm:<id>`); a month-on-month compares with the calendar month before. 🔴 Cost / visit and Expenses vs sales stop at the last recorded claim month (`expenseEfficiencyOf`): never divide claims to August by visits or sales to October — the payroll import lags, and Dina Hamdy's claims stop in August while her visits and sales run on; each tile names the months it covers. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-number-audit.test.ts` and `tests/employee-360-dv-split.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A LEADER'S "DOUBLE-VISITS CONDUCTED" ADDS UP: AM + PM + PHARMACIES + AWAITING VALIDATION IS THE HEADLINE, AND THE REPS LIST PLUS "NOT MATCHED TO AN EMPLOYEE" IS THE HEADLINE** (2026-10-27). Dina Hamdy's year to date printed 712 over a split of 496 and a reps list of 705: 209 campaign double visits were in the headline and in no half, 7 Phoenix ride-alongs awaiting validation were in neither, and 7 iReports360 rows on a Tanta account with no HR id were skipped by the list. `conductedVisitBucket` (`src/lib/employee-360/deep-service.ts`) now holds out only a row that is not field work (`isFieldWorkVisit`), so a campaign counts in its half as on the rep's page; ride-alongs awaiting validation are held out of the halves and printed on the tile, as the calendar's `managerDayFigures` does; `unmatchedConductedDoubleVisits` feeds the reps list's own line. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-dv-split.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A LEADER'S VISIT IS IN AM, PM OR PHARMACIES — EXACTLY ONE, BY THE CUSTOMER'S LIST; AND A TEAM OVER 15 TERRITORIES READS ITS 15 MOST VISITED AND SAYS "THE 15 MOST VISITED OF THE TEAM'S N"** (owner, 2026-10-27: "AM ALONE PM ALONE PHARMACIES ALONE"; "SHOW THE MOST FRQUENT 15"). A pharmacy named on a doctor's visit stays in that visit's AM or PM and is never added to Pharmacies (`splitOf` over `phoenixVisitBucket`). The frequency section ranks a wide team by `countValidatedVisitsBySeat` (one `seat_id` read) through `busiestTerritories`, then reads at most `CRM_FREQUENCY_MAX_TERRITORIES` Frequency reports — never every territory (the 2 GB database box), and never an empty section. Pinned by `tests/employee-360-number-audit.test.ts`.
