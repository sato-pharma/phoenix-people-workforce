## Rules

### The letter, its designs, its email and the hand-off

- 🔒 **THE FOUR OLDER OFFER LETTER DESIGNS ARE FROZEN** — `src/lib/offers/` keeps classic, executive, sato and house exactly as they are: their blue, navy bands, tinted salary table and circular disc; no house-identity rule reaches them. The owner added a fifth, Premium, on 2026-10-13 ("for job offers create new templete with the new identity"); it is a new design beside them, never a restyle of one.

- 🔒 **WHAT THE LETTER SAYS A JOB *IS* BELONGS TO THE JOB TITLE, NEVER TO A DESIGN — `offer_job_titles.role_summary`, reaching every design as `{{roleSummary}}`** (2026-10-07: the designs carried the district-sales paragraph from `SP-HR-DSM-2026-019` and printed it for EVERY position, so a Talent Acquisition letter promised a field team and a territory's sales number). A design is a LOOK; the Letter Designer has no filter by position and must not grow one. **Never type a sentence about a particular kind of job into a design** — `tests/offer-role-summary.test.ts` greps all four for `this district needs`, `sales performance`, `district plan`, `field team`, `market expansion`. Blank = the role-neutral standard sentence, never a gap. PLAIN TEXT, escaped once (a real em dash, never `&mdash;`), and `{{territory}}` works inside it. `offers.role_summary` is a COPY taken when the title is chosen, never a live lookup — else rewording a position restates a signed letter.

- 🔒 **THE OFFER EMAIL CARRIES ONE ACCEPT BUTTON AND A REPLY-TO-DECLINE LINE — NEVER A DECLINE BUTTON — AND SUBMITTING THE JOINING FORM *IS* THE ACCEPTANCE** (owner, 2026-10-08). `/join/[token]` is public and only READS; the POST creates the employee through HR's own Add Employee chain (`joining-form-service.ts`). 🔴 **A Medical Rep or District Manager offer cannot be SENT without its AU seat** (`offerRequiresSeat`). 🔴 **No offer is SENT without a start date** (`OFFER_START_DATE_REQUIRED_MESSAGE`). 🔴 **Pay and payment mode are FIXED BY TITLE**: payroll's `resolveStandardSalaryAllowances` into `deriveSalaryFromNet`; two parts for Medical Reps and District Managers, one part for every other title (`joiningFormPaymentMode`). 🔴 **Front and back national ID photos are REQUIRED** and filed on Hiring Documents into the slot `joining_form_upload = 'national_id'` marks, never by name. 🔴 **The "Photo" is OPTIONAL** — it becomes the profile photo and is filed under `joining_form_upload = 'photo'` WITHOUT ticking it (`markSubmitted: false`): HR still collects the six prints. 🔴 **English and Arabic** — every message needs its `AR_MESSAGES` line. Account: `docs/recruitment-offer-joining-form.md`. Pinned by `tests/offer-joining-form.test.ts`.

- 🔒 **A JOB OFFER FIRES FROM `HR@satopharma.com` OR `hr@send.satopharma.com`, AND FROM NO OTHER MAILBOX** (owner, 2026-10-13, verbatim: *"all job offers email must only fire from HR@satopharma.com or hr@send.satopharma.com"*). The list is `src/lib/email/job-offer-sender.ts` and it is enforced in BOTH places an offer’s From is decided: a `JOB_OFFER_EMAIL_FROM` naming anything else is REFUSED (`hr@` on the apex is used, and the Job Offers screen names the refused value), and 🔴 **a backup provider account that would have to rewrite the From DECLINES the offer instead of re-branding it** — measured 2026-10-13, Resend account 2 would have sent `Sato Pharma HR <phoenix@send2.satopharma.com>` and Brevo `Phoenix Workforce <phoenix@send.satopharma.com>`, so an offer waits for Resend’s first account (the only one holding both Sato domains). Pinned by `tests/offer-reply-mailbox.test.ts` § 6.

- 🔒 **A CANDIDATE’S REPLY TO AN OFFER REACHES HIM — HIS MAILBOX CANNOT BE CONFIGURED OFF AN OFFER’S Reply-To** (owner, 2026-10-13: *"make sure if candidate replies the email reaches me"*). `resolveOfferSender()` APPENDS `ADMIN_STANDING_EMAIL` whenever the resolved list lacks it, so `JOB_OFFER_REPLY_TO` can add targets and reorder them, never remove him. 🔴 **Measured 2026-10-13: it was NOT reaching him on every path** — 18 of 63 offer messages went out through Brevo, which keeps only the FIRST Reply-To address, so those replies went to `hr@satopharma.com` alone (the From lock now keeps offers off Brevo). A candidate whose client ignores Reply-To and answers the From still reaches `hr@` only — closing that needs a forward on the `hr@` mailbox itself, which is his click in Turbify, not code. Pinned by `tests/offer-reply-mailbox.test.ts` § 3c.

- 🔴 **THE CORPORATE LETTER'S NAVY MASTHEAD DRAWS `sato-pharma-logo-on-navy.png`, A FILE OF ITS OWN — NEVER FOLD IT BACK ONTO THE SHARED `sato-pharma-logo.png`** (15 Oct 2026, his word: "fix the PHARMA contrast on the Corporate letter's masthead"). The shared on-dark mark was made by recolouring ONE path — SATO, to white — so the word PHARMA kept its paper blue `#0A6BA6` and measured **2.34:1** on this design's navy `#17304d`, under the 3:1 floor and visibly dim at the 30px the band draws it. The on-navy file is that same artwork with PHARMA alone lifted to `#1482C2` (3.19:1), the house palette's own lightest blue; SATO stays white and both waves keep their blues, the lower one deliberately left at 1.61:1 as the lockup's shading. `satoLogoReverseDataUri()` is the only reader and only the `sato` variant is given it, so the blast radius is the masthead and the welcome-band cap. The shared file is drawn by the CEO and Finance command centres, the joining form, the business-review pack and the contract — recolouring it moves all five. Pinned by `tests/offer-letterhead-inline-mark.test.ts` § 8.

- 🔴 **A DESIGN IN `src/lib/offers/*-template.ts` IS A GENERATOR, NOT THE LETTER — EVERY LIVE LETTER IS RENDERED FROM THE `offer_templates` ROW, WHICH IS A SEEDED COPY, SO A WORDING FIX IN CODE REACHES NOBODY UNTIL THE ROWS ARE CORRECTED TOO** (15 Oct 2026: four of the five live rows still closed with "We believe your leadership will be a strong addition to our organisation" after the code said otherwise). Correct a row by targeted text replacement inside its stored blocks, never by re-seeding, because a row may carry the owner's own edits. CSS is the other half and behaves the opposite way: the skins live in `render.ts`, so a `.welcome`, `.appt` or `@page` change is live the moment it deploys. 🔒 **The four older designs are FINGERPRINTED** — `frozenDesignTests` in `tests/offer-builtin-templates.test.ts` hashes each rendered letter, and the digest is re-pinned only with his word, quoted in the commit; a design that should NOT have moved keeping its digest is the proof the edit stayed where it was aimed.

- 🔒 **THE PREMIUM LETTER PLACES ITS OWN COMPANY SEAL — 21MM, FOOT ON THE SIGNATORY'S RULE (`.accept .offer-seal` in `premium-skin.ts`) — AND THE FOUR OLDER DESIGNS KEEP THE BASE'S 30MM CENTRED ONE** (owner, 2026-10-19, "Move the seal": the base seal printed over the word SIGNATURE). It is still drawn once, on the company's cell only, and the skin never restyles `.sigcell` or the signature ink. A **Training Manager** is a seeded Job Title (cost of living 3,500 = payroll's pair, reports to the Marketing Manager) with `OFFER_TRAINING_MANAGER_ROLE_SUMMARY`; migration `20261019165000` adds the row to production.

- 🔴 **THE OFFER LETTER, THE CANDIDATE AND THE EMPLOYEE ARE KEPT IN STEP BY FIVE DOORS — HR's Add Employee from a letter and the candidate's joining form point the letter at the new employee and mark its candidate hired; Add Employee opened from the workspace's "Mark as hired" (`fromCandidateId`, owner 2026-10-20) marks THAT candidate hired by id and closes the filled seat's open requisitions; "declined offer" on the Master, and a letter marked declined by hand, turn the candidate `rejected` (live piles only; withdrawn never)** (owner, 2026-10-14, after the overview read 2 of 26 offers joined and 0 hired in October). The candidate is found ONLY through the letter's own pointer, never by name, e-mail or phone; a move writes one history line and sends no notice. The overview's candidate tiles and funnel count the candidate POOL and the Hired tile the Employee Master — never the applications half. Account: `docs/recruitment-offer-hire-sync.md`. Pinned by `tests/offer-hire-sync.test.ts` and, for the fifth door, `tests/recruitment-hire-from-candidate.test.ts`.
