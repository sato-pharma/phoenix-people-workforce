## Rules

Moved out of `crm` on 2026-10-20 so its injected pointer stays under the 4,000-byte margin `tests/module-memory.test.ts` enforces. **`crm` keeps every glob it had**, so a session working on these files loads both modules and nothing that used to arrive stops arriving.

- 🔒 **IN THE EMPLOYEE PORTAL, KPIs IS A SIDEBAR HEADING WITH A SUMMARY AND THREE REPORTS — Frequency, Coverage, Call Rate each have a page of their own** (owner, 2026-10-14). `/employee-portals/kpis` keeps only the consolidated tabs (`PORTAL_KPI_SUMMARY_TABS`) and FORWARDS a retired `?tab=` to its report; the admin shell is unchanged and still carries all seven tabs. The Call Rate report's Total view lays AM and PM side by side beside a count of calls — **never one blended rate**. Pinned by `runKpisReportsSplitTests`.
- 🔒 **THE KPI REPORTS FILTER BY ANY COLUMN AND BY ANY DOCTOR ON THE OPEN REP'S LIST** (owner, 2026-10-14: "filter by anything in any of the KPI reports"). Two levels, one URL builder: rep RANGES (`KpiFilterState.ranges`, `r_<column>=min..max`) and doctor filters (`KpiFilterState.doctor`, the `d…` params in `kpis-doctor-filters.ts`; Brick is `dbrick`, pipe-joined). 🔴 **A rep with nothing to measure on a column NEVER matches a bound on it** (no plan is not 0%), bounds read the figure as printed (one decimal), and the doctor filters narrow the PRINTED rows only — the tiles and breakdowns stay on the rep's whole list and add up to his row. The doctor params ride only on Coverage and Frequency, and opening a different rep starts his list unfiltered. Both filter modules import nothing server-side (client bar). Pinned by `runKpiRangeFilterTests`, `runKpiReportFilterUrlTests`, `runKpiDoctorFilterTests`, `runKpiReportFilterWiringTests`.
- 🔒 **A DOCTOR'S BRICK ON THE KPIs LISTS IS THE ONE HIS VALIDATED LIVE PHOTO WAS FILED IN, AND A FAILED READ STAMPS NOTHING** (owner, 2026-10-29: "filter the coverage per brick … derived from the live photo validation"). `loadKpiDoctorBricks` (`kpis-doctor-bricks.ts`) reads `phoenix_crm_places.brick_name` for every rep's doctors on screen — set once by `resolvePinGeography`, never recomputed. `chooseKpiDoctorBricks` picks a listed customer over a dropped one, then the newest validation; then a place with a brick, then the earliest. Three answers: a brick; "Validated · no brick on record" (the photo is past its own area's 20 km reach); "Not validated yet", which also covers a validated customer with no ACTIVE place (owner, 2026-10-29). 🔴 On a failed read the board stamps neither `brick` nor `placeValidated`, so no doctor is called not validated. Coverage and Frequency add By brick (seen, and the frequency verdicts), each brick a link to its doctors. Pinned by `runKpiDoctorBrickChooserTests`, `runKpiDoctorFilterTests`, `runKpiReportFilterWiringTests`.
- 🔒 **EVERY REP ON THE KPIs PAGES SHOWS HIS HIRE DATE, AND THE BOARD EXPORTS AS A HOUSE-IDENTITY XLSX** (owner, 2026-10-25). `KpiRepName` prints "Hired 12 Mar 2025" from `KpiRepRow.hireDate`, which the join stamps from the directory's `employees.hire_date`; nobody else's date ever fills a blank. The Export XLSX link sends the board's own month, period, filters, tab, open rep and doctor filters through `buildKpisQuery` to `/api/crm/kpis-export`, which re-runs `getKpisBoard` behind the same gate, so the file never holds more reps than the screen. `crm/kpis-board-workbook.ts` writes the hire date as a real date cell, keeps AM and PM as separate columns, and prints the screen's marker words (`KPI_EXPORT_MARKERS`) where the screen shows no figure, never a 0. On Coverage and Frequency it adds a Not validated yet column, a By brick sheet and, with a rep open, a Doctors sheet of the rows on screen. Pinned by `runKpisBoardWorkbookTests`.
- 🔒 **ONE BRICK READ COVERS EVERY REP ON THE COVERAGE AND FREQUENCY TABS — THE TEAM'S BY BRICK TABLE AND EACH ROW'S NOT-VALIDATED COUNT COME FROM IT** (owner, 2026-10-29). `getKpisBoard` makes one `loadKpiDoctorBricks` call over every rep's list (above `WHOLE_READ_FROM` it pages the whole validated set, then narrows) and the open rep reuses his stamped list. `buildKpiBrickRows` (`kpis-brick-table.ts`, client-safe) counts each rep's list on its own; per brick, on plan + below + above = judged, and below holds Not visited, as the rep row's Below does. `board.teamBricks` leaves out just-started reps, as the team tiles do, and a just-started rep gets no `notValidatedDoctors`. 🔴 A failed read stamps no count and builds no table: the row prints nothing, the file prints "Not read", never 0. The admin Frequency tab carries the lists too. Pinned by `runKpiBrickTableTests`, `runKpiBrickWiringTests`, `runKpiDoctorBrickChooserTests`.
- 🔒 **IN THE PORTAL, EACH SUMMARY ROW LINKS INTO THAT REP'S REPORTS** (owner, 2026-10-14: polish the KPIs Summary page). `summaryReportLinks` puts Coverage, Frequency and Open visits under the rep's name (desktop `RepCell` action and the phone card); portal root only, doctor filters reset, Coverage/Frequency only for a rep with a measurable plan. Pinned by `runKpisReportsSplitTests` block 7.
- 🔒 **THE KPIs BOARD CARRIES A MANAGER ROW FOR EVERY MANAGER, BUILT BY AVERAGES** (owner, 2026-10-20: "every dm kpis is calculated based on his reps average and nsm based on dsms av and so on"). `buildKpiManagerRows` (`src/lib/crm/kpis-manager-rows.ts`) fills `board.managers`, never the rep table: a District Manager's row is the plain average of his own reps' printed rows; every manager above averages only the rows DIRECTLY beneath him (his managers plus any rep reporting to him straight), never all the reps at the bottom. Each of the twelve measures is averaged on its own — AM and PM are never blended — and a row printing "No plan", "No calls" or "No GPS feed" is left out of that measure, never counted as zero. Built from the scoped reps BEFORE any filter; rep rows, team totals and the podium are untouched. "Reports to" matches the whole chain (`managersAbove`). Pinned by `tests/kpis-filters.test.ts` (`runKpiManagerRowChecks`) and `tests/manager-filter.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A REP HIRED INSIDE THE WINDOW IS MEASURED OVER HER OWN EMPLOYED DAYS, AND UNDER A THIRD OF THEM READS "JUST STARTED" AND IS JUDGED BY NOTHING** (owner, 2026-10-26: a hire of 24 Oct read "Never reached a planned doctor", "Below floor · 95%" and a 0% overall). `clipToEmployedDays` shortens an older-rules row to hire/last day; `kpiJustStartedOf` reuses `isTooEarlyToScore` (`KPI_VERDICT_THRESHOLD`, a third) — never a second threshold. A just-started rep carries the one flag "Just started · N working days in", no band, floor or Overall, and leaves the podiums, `sumKpiTeamTotals` (a head only) and `kpiRepMeasures` (her manager's average). The footer prints human dates and says the Overall averages the four capped measures. Pinned by `runKpisNewHireTests`.
- 🔴 **ONE KPIs BOARD ASKS ITS DISCOVERY ONCE AND READS A NAMED PLAN PASS ONCE — A FEW PEOPLE'S TWO HALVES RUN SIDE BY SIDE, A COMPANY-WIDE BOARD'S STILL RUN IN TURN** (2 Nov 2026, "fix the portal home slow opens"). A rep's tile read his month's plan rows six times, one after the other, because its discovery, doctor lists and Phoenix half each asked again; it was the last card on his landing to finish, and reps are 95 of the morning's 130 opens. `getKpisBoard` makes one `PlanChunkMemo` and one `kpiBoardDiscovery` and hands them to both halves (`boardReads`, `planChunks`). The memo lives for one board and no other caller passes one, so nothing else starts reading a stale plan. In turn stays for the company-wide read so one open never doubles the day-slice requests on the database. Pinned by `tests/portal-home-card-reads.test.ts`; account: `docs/performance-engineering-standards.md`, "The portal home's cards".
- 🔒 **EVERY ACTIVE MEDICAL REP IN REACH IS ON THE KPIs BOARD, EVEN WITH NO VISIT OR PLAN YET** (owner, 2026-11-09: "y are not pulling data of these reports from phoenix crm" — Hany's Coverage read 9 of 13 reps and dropped Sahar El Gallad's whole district). `kpiBoardRoster` (active, medical-rep title, hired by the window's end, in scope) feeds `joinKpiRows` a blank row per missing rep, matched by hr id or CRM id so nobody is twice; the scope filter still runs after, so the roster never widens reach. A blank rep reads none and "no plan", never 0%. Pinned by `tests/kpis-full-roster.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE COVERAGE REPORT GROUPS REPS BY REGION AND DISTRICT MANAGER, AND EVERY FIGURE ON IT IS THE BOARD'S OWN ARITHMETIC** (owner, 2026-11-09: "group reps by district manager"). Teams come from `groupKpiRepsByTeam` and add up to the Coverage tile (`sumKpiTeamTotals`); a rep keeps their rank on the whole board. The open rep's panel (`kpi-coverage-rep-detail.ts`) counts their two lists only: Covered + Late + Not due yet = Planned, the floor is ⌈planned × 95 ÷ 100⌉, and each group link opens exactly the doctors its row counted. 🔴 The Coverage row hides other reports' flags (`KPI_FLAGS_OFF_REPORT`) but the flag FILTER still matches them. Flat finish only. Account: `docs/kpi-coverage-report.md`. Pinned by `runKpiCoveragePremiumTests`, `runKpiCoverageRepDetailTests`.
- 🔒 **THE FREQUENCY REPORT GROUPS REPS LIKE COVERAGE, HAS NO FLOOR, AND SHORT IS MEASURED AGAINST WHAT HAS COME DUE** (owner, 2026-11-09, the same pass). Teams are `groupKpiRepsByTeam` + `kpiTeamFrequency` (`sumKpiTeamTotals` + `kpiTeamFigures`) and add up to the Frequency tile; a team with nobody judged prints "No plan", never 0%. Never invent a frequency pass mark for a rep. The open rep (`kpi-frequency-rep-detail.ts`): short against `dueSoFar`, extra against the whole month; a doctor not due owes nothing; "one more visit each" counts only doctors one visit passes. The screen and the export print the gap from `kpiFrequencyGapText`. Account: `docs/kpi-frequency-report.md`. Pinned by `runKpiFrequencyPremiumTests`, `runKpiFrequencyRepDetailTests`.

- 🔴 **A VISIT TO A CUSTOMER STILL WAITING TO BE VALIDATED COUNTS IN NO KPI** (owner, 1 Oct 2026: "not counted in kpis till validated"; sweep, 11 Nov 2026: 1,358 such visits were counting). `applyVisitFilter` with `kpiOnly` adds `phoenix_customer_validated is not false` beside `counts_in_kpi`; a reader that must SHOW those visits as awaiting validation (call rate, the rep's visits list) passes `keepAwaitingValidation: true` and sorts them itself. The view is unchanged, so work-evidence readers still see every visit. Pinned by `tests/crm-counts-in-kpi-readers.test.ts` (`KEEPS_AWAITING_VALIDATION`).
