The NIGHT reads with the strong reader and takes a second look at the chosen sign cropped out of the frame, which decides in either direction; the DOOR reads once in the rep's 8 s; a HELD place is re-read strong, then guided, counted only where a blind close look READ the name. 🔴 **CLAUDE READS FIRST SINCE 2026-10-18 — Sonnet at the door, at night and on the re-read, Haiku for a lost call; OpenAI only when Claude's account is dead** (`preferProvider`, `SignboardReader`): on one rep's 21 photos stored 19 "Not readable", Claude confirmed 15 where the stored OpenAI readings confirmed 1. - 🔴 **A FACILITY NAME'S QUORUM IS COUNTED ON THE WORDS THAT SAY *WHICH* ONE, NEVER ON مستشفى OR عام** — `signNamesCustomer`'s place axis folds through `distinctiveWords`, as the Google listing comparison always has. - 🔴 **A PERSON'S NAME COUNTS EACH COMPOUND AS ONE WORD, AND DROPS الأستاذ** (owner, 2026-10-12: "fix the signboard reader's name matching for titles and spaces"). ابراهيم عبدالشافى was filed as another person off a clear plate reading عبد الشافى, and عبد/ابو counting as words matched ابو الدهب on an ابو الوفا plate. `personNameWords` (`signboard-rules.ts`) joins a head (عبد, ابو) with the next word and a tail (الدين, الله) with the one before, on both sides. 🔴 **A compound's core still answers when it stands ALONE on the other side** — a reader that drops عبد has read the name; joining strictly filed a false flag. 🔴 **A name's ال is optional on any of its words, the given name too, and nothing more is forgiven** (2026-10-18): البدوى answers for بدوي, حسن for الحسن; a misread letter is another doctor. PERSON axis only; never move it into `normalizeContactName`, which keys `phoenix_crm_fold_name`. Pinned by `checkNameSpelling`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "One name, two spellings".
- 🔴 **ON A PERSON'S NAME THE ONE-WORD ALLOWANCE IS NEVER HIS GIVEN NAME LOST BEHIND ANOTHER MAN'S** (2026-10-18: of the night's 341 confirmations, judged again against names that are not the customer's, 106 also matched one). So his father's and family name behind another given name, his given name as another man's father, or his words gathered from two names on one notice, is not his. - 🔴 **`recordPlaceSignboard` SWALLOWS ITS ERROR BY DESIGN, SO A CALLER MUST READ THE ROW BACK — AND WHAT IT SENDS IS `jsonbSafe(check)`.** On 2026-10-09 the re-read pass printed "written to the place row" for all five places and اسلام عبدالعال still held its old verdict: `jsonb` had refused the payload over one character the reader transcribed, and the silence that protects the rep at the door hid it. Pinned by `checkManySigns` case 11b and the server-source case 14.
- 🔴 **A WRONG READING IS NOT ALWAYS ON THE BOARD, SO `--place` RE-READS A PLACE WHETHER IT IS FLAGGED OR NOT.** `scripts/phoenix-crm-reread-signboards.mjs` was scoped to flag rows, and the board is not the set of places a wrong reader has damaged: a `match` raises no flag at all and leaves the nightly backlog (`SIGNBOARD_REREAD_REASONS`), and `other_name` raises one only where the detector met the place in a visit. Measured 2026-10-12 on the six places #5756's compound-name fix moved — ONE was on the board, so five were unreachable by the only tool that can correct them, among them a false `match` and an unflagged `other_name`. A run with no `--place` is still board-driven. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE SAME PHOTOGRAPH DOES NOT READ THE SAME TWICE — ONE RE-READ IS EVIDENCE, NEVER A VERDICT ON A FLAG.** Measured on the five held places, 2026-10-09: مستشفى المنشاوى العام read `other_name` on the board's Arabic text at 15:12 and `unjudged / unreadable` (`"TANTA GENE"`) at 15:34, and اسلام عبدالعال went `unjudged` at 15:35 and `other_name` at 15:48 once the nameplate `د/ اسلام حشيش` came back `clear`. Legibility is what moves, and legibility is what decides whether a sign may hold a place — so a flag the pass calls SUPERSEDED on one reading can be correct on the next. Never clear a flag on a single re-read, and never report a re-read's verdict as the answer about the place.

- 🔴 **`unreadable` IS NOT A VERDICT, IT IS AN ATTEMPT — THE NIGHT'S YIELD WAS 4 ANSWERS OUT OF 60 READS BEFORE THIS.** Measured 2026-10-09: 42 of 60 ended `unreadable`, which was TERMINAL, and 14 lost their model call and waited a day. Legibility is not a property of the photograph — the same frame read `other_name` at 18:12 and `TANTA GENE` at 18:34, and another read blank at 18:35 and a clear nameplate at 18:48. So a lost call is retried inside the night, an unreadable frame is read again up to `SIGNBOARD_MAX_READ_ATTEMPTS` (3) counted by `attempts` ON THE ROW, and the third one becomes `unreadable_final`, which no backlog query takes. 🔴 **Since 2026-10-18 that is a FALLBACK reader's allowance only — the strong reader's `unreadable` is final on its first answer** (the rule above). 🔴 **The stopping condition is a value in the row, never a filter over a JSON number** — a wrong filter reads as an empty backlog with nothing going red. The structural cases are `name_unusable` (a pharmacy, a name that folds away), never `unreadable`, or they return every night for ever.
- 🔴 **NOTHING REACHES THE INTEGRITY BOARD ON ONE READING — A FLAGGABLE VERDICT IS READ TWICE AND FILED ONLY IF THE TWO AGREE.** The flag is this pass's only human-visible output and it carries a person's name; 2026-10-09 produced a false match AND two flips on re-reading the same frame. On disagreement the place is `unjudged` / `unconfirmed`, KEEPING the first reading's text and signs, and is read again later — nothing flagged, nothing discarded.
