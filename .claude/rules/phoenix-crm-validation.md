- 🔒 **A VALIDATED PLACE NEEDS THE CUSTOMER'S NAME ON ITS SIGNBOARD, AND A PERSON'S NAME IS NEVER READ ON AN AM PHOTO** (owner, 2026-10-08: "require the customer's name always" and "NEVER A PERSON AM SIGN / PM SIGN"). The old check validated a LOCATION; he grades an IDENTIFICATION. `judgeSignboard` (`signboard-rules.ts`): a confident "no signboard" holds on either list; AM reads place names only; PM must name the person. 🔴 **At the DOOR everything but a match HOLDS a SIGNBOARD since 2026-10-17** ("Hold; re-read; else you decide"), an unreadable sign and a lost call included: `server/signboard-holds.ts` re-reads it ONCE, approves it itself on a confirmed match, else it is the Super Admin's. 🔴 **Since 2026-10-18 the signboard is a SCANNER, like the timetable: a photo with no readable name (`unreadable`, or no nameplate at a PM door) is REFUSED at the door with what to fix, twice, and the THIRD is saved and held** (`signboardDoorAction`, rep + customer + Cairo day, `SIGNBOARD_RETAKE_CODE`); another doctor's name and a lost call are never refused. The integrity board still never holds on `unjudged`; a TIMETABLE is held after 3 tries, a lost call never. Pharmacies are never read. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`.
- 🔒 **A REFUSAL TELLS THE REP WHAT IS WRONG WITH HIS PHOTO, FROM NINE FIXED REASONS, AND NEVER WHO REFUSED** (owner, 2026-10-08; `schedule_other_name` and `schedule_missing` 2026-10-09 for the timetable; `short_name` 2026-11-07). `VALIDATION_REFUSAL_REASONS` (`validation-feedback.ts`) is closed, and `reasonsFor(kind)` splits it: a signboard is never offered the timetable pair, a timetable never `photo_of_paper` (a timetable IS paper), and a building never `short_name` (`buildingRefusalReason`; the venue's own CHECK holds eight). 🔴 **The live CHECK is the newest migration's, `20261107195700`**: each recreation drops the one before, so an older file under-counts. The comment beside it reaches the rep and their manager since 2026-11-01 (`tellDecisionComment`, module Place checks); an older note stays the record's. The notice `phoenix_crm.validation_not_confirmed` is in-app and Telegram only, push-blocked, never email; the system's sentence says "this place". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-email-block.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A ROW SHOWS "Validate again" OR "Not validated yet", NEVER BOTH** — the pill and title come from `google-place-rules.ts`; `list-customer.tsx` once hard-coded the first and showed the pair one under the other.
- 🔴 **THE LIVE "TO VALIDATE" ORDER IS `validateFirstIn`, NOT `rankValidateNext`** — capture-again customers first (oldest reopening first, rose), then visits-waiting-first. `rankValidateNext` has no caller in `src/` or `app/`; changing it changes nothing a rep sees. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validate-next.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE SAME-SEAT DUPLICATE MERGE ARCHIVES THE LOSER, NEVER DELETES IT, AND RUNS DRY BY DEFAULT** — a deleted reference row is re-minted. `scripts/phoenix-crm-merge-same-seat-duplicates.ts` writes a report, a plan and a pre-image; `--apply` needs all three and an actor, and `phoenix_crm_merge_duplicate_customer` re-checks every guard under the seat's lock. The keeper keeps every place. A bulk production change: announce it first. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-duplicate-merge.test.ts`.

- 🔒 **THE VALIDATION SCREEN IS A FORM OF SIX STEPS, ONE AT A TIME, IN THE ORDER THE REP DOES THEM — NEVER ONE LONG PAGE** (owner, 2026-10-14: *"it opens on the step I should do… one, two, three, finish a step, press Next"*). Customer · Where · (Signboard · Location, or Building · Timetable) · Hours · Save, on the same `CrmStepForm` as Add visit and the weekly plan. Next is held until its step is answered (`stepGap`), the rail opens only answered steps (`reach`), a tap on the door moves on, and a refused photo returns him to the step that retakes it. The shared editors take a `part` and a step prints no numbered block title (`CrmUntitledBlocks`); every other screen keeps its numbering. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validate-steps.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **A BY-EYE NOTE'S VERDICT IS ITS `advice` COLUMN, AND IT ONLY ORDERS AND TAGS THE ONE-BY-ONE REVIEW — IT DECIDES NOTHING** (owner, 2026-10-19: "Show my advice by the buttons"). `phoenix_crm_place_notes.advice` (approve · your_call · new_photo · reject) is allowed on a `system_admin` note only (CHECK); the review shows it as a coloured tag above Approve and Reject (a polyclinic: above the building's decision and each seated customer's) and puts each tab's slides in that order, stably, ranked from the notes as the review OPENED. A by-eye note written later sets `advice` in the same insert, or it sorts with the unadvised. Never read it on the rep's side. Pinned by `tests/place-review-deck.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "The by-eye advice in the review".
- 🔴 **THE LIVE CAMERA ALWAYS OPENS ON THE REAR LENS; THE REP MAY SWITCH TO THE FRONT ONE AND BACK** (owner, 2026-11-02: *"when live camera is open to be able to switch front camera to back camera"*). Switch camera sits beside the shutter in `LivePhotoCapture`, drawn only when the phone lists a second lens (`hasSecondCamera`); `openFrontCamera` asks `exact` then the preference, a refusal is never retried, a lens that will not open puts the previous one back, and the frame is never mirrored. Never make the front lens the default, and never drop the rear `exact` ladder to simplify the switch. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-rear-camera.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A COLD FIRST READING NEVER BECOMES THE PHOTO'S — A LATER READING THAT CONTRADICTS ONE TAKEN IN THE RECEIVER'S FIRST 30 SECONDS WINS, WHATEVER ACCURACY EITHER CLAIMS** (2026-10-22). Dr Tharwat Milad's signboard photo read Faw Bahari at ±65 m, 10,070 m from his clinic; the pin the same phone took five seconds later read Dishna at ±20 m. A receiver just switched on answers from nearby networks first, and the camera kept that answer because the shutter's reading replaced it only when sharper. `laterReadingOverrules` (`photo-reading-rules.ts`): apart by more than both accuracies and never under 250 m; asked by the running watch and at the shutter, and the stamp then names the reading kept. A warm receiver's reading is never overruled. Every capture screen holds the receiver on (`useLocationWarmUp`), only where location is already allowed. It never holds the shutter and never tells the rep. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-photo-reading.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE CAMERA STARTS FROM THE WARM-UP'S READING** — "Take the photo" never waits for a reading the page already holds (owner, 12 Nov 2026, validating in training: "the location took hilarious time"). The warm-up used to throw its readings away, so the camera's fresh `maximumAge: 0` watch held the shutter until the phone's next update, which comes slowly indoors. `noteWarmReading` / `latestWarmReading` (`location-receiver.ts`) keep it; `startLocation` seeds `fix` from one under `FIX_STALE_MS` old, and the watch, the fresh shutter read and the cold rule then treat it like any first reading. 🔴 **The Capture-location pin and `readShutterFix` never read it** — each stays its own fresh reading, or the photo-versus-pin check compares one reading with itself again (2026-10-12). 🔴 **The seed takes a reading up to `WARM_SEED_MAX_AGE_MS` (60 s) old, and the rep's own first reading switches the warm-up on (`locationAllowedOnThisPage`)** (13 Nov 2026) — Safari's "Ask" and a first-time rep kept it off, so every retake waited again; it still never asks. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-location-warm-up.md`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-photo-reading.test.ts` §3.
- 🔴 **A CLINIC DOCTOR IS WRITTEN AS FIRST, MIDDLE AND LAST NAME, AND ANY DOCTOR ON THE LIST WITH ONE OR TWO NAMES IS NEVER VISITED UNTIL HE WRITES THEM — BUT IS PLANNED AS NORMAL** (owner, 2026-11-07: "any dr in the list must be 3 names"). PM only. Each box is a real name (`doctorNamePartProblem`); a box holding only الدين, الله, عبد or ابو is refused, and the joined name must count 3 (`doctorFullNameProblem`) — علاء / الدين / الجمل once saved as two words and blocked him for ever (8 Nov 2026). The full name is held against the board already read: 60%+ saved, less waits on Place checks, never refused. Add visit and the visit save refuse him, and Add visit's "Write the full name" opens his Change the details (`pendingFullName`, `?fullname=`); the plan does NOT (owner, 2026-11-08). Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-doctor-full-name.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A DOCTOR ALREADY ON THE LIST WITH ONE OR TWO NAMES IS VALIDATED ONLY WITH ALL THREE, IN THE SAME SAVE — AND PLACE CHECKS NEVER APPROVES ONE** (owner, 2026-11-07: "Don't accept from the beginning the two names … why make it one step only?"; for those already waiting, "Send them back to the rep"). The validation screen asks the three boxes on its first step (`validationNeedsFullName`); the server refuses without them before any check or write (`fullNameAtValidation`, `VALIDATION_FULL_NAME_CODE`), reads the signboard and timetable against the FULL name, and `phoenix_crm_validate_customer` (`20261107195700`) renames him through `phoenix_crm_rename_customer` in the same transaction. Approve and every bulk approve are disabled on him and refused by `refuseApprovalOfShortName` — never the Super Admin's take-back of a rejection (2026-11-10, `phoenix-crm-place-checks`); the reason `short_name` sends him back. The rep reads only his own fix. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-doctor-full-name.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A NAME WRITTEN TWICE SIDE BY SIDE IS NOT A FULL NAME — EXCEPT محمد محمد AS FIRST AND MIDDLE NAME** (owner, 2026-11-10: a rep validated "مصطفي مصطفي شلش" off a sign reading "مصطفى شلش"; he chose "Block all but محمد محمد"). `doubledNamePart` (`pm-name-rules.ts`) refuses any name beside itself (ى/ي, أ/ا and ال folded) in the three boxes and on the server, and `validationNeedsFullName` asks a doubled name for the three again, so Place checks sends it back. 160 doctors had first = middle that day, 56 of them محمد محمد. Visits and places already validated are untouched. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-doctor-full-name.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE SUPER ADMIN ALONE VALIDATES ON A TRUSTED POINT, WITH NO PHOTO** (owner, 2026-11-10: "compare with the locations of checkins of this customer from the old crm until we find a trusted point"). For a doctor no rep can validate at the door: 5+ readings on 3+ Cairo days within 30 m. Readings: Phoenix visits, every place ever captured (retired too: photo reading + distinct pin) and old-CRM check-ins with `source <> 'phoenix'` only; a visit copied from iReports360 counts once. Point = median of the core (`findTrustedPoint`); brick and area from `resolvePlaceLocation`. The place is `point_source 'trusted'`, `photo_path` null, `trusted_basis` set — every photo reader must take a null path (never `String()` it). Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-trusted-point.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-trusted-point.md`.
- 🔴 **VALIDATED ON ONE LINE IS VALIDATED ON ALL THREE** (owner, 2026-11-12, "Both"). Twins (`areLineTwins`): different lines, same area code, same AM/PM list, 3+ name words, `sameName`, no speciality clash — Phoenix-validated rows only, never old-CRM contacts. A waiting twin is validated with copies of the validated twin's places (`point_source 'line_twin'`, no photo, the source's validator); two validated twins within 150 m are MERGED onto the stronger point (moved > shared > trusted > photo > pin) through `phoenix_crm_move_place` — this partly lifts "only the Super Admin moves a validated place". A copy follows its source's move and retire (trigger). Hooked after every validation via `afterResponse`; the boards read `.neq("point_source", "line_twin")`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-line-twins.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-line-twins.md`.
- 🔴 **"APPROVE IT AFTER ALL" STOPS AND SAYS WHY** (owner, 14 Nov 2026) — `phoenix_crm_restore_validation` refuses by name a dropped customer, a place in a retired building, and a door captured again (another active place of his in the same building or within 150 m): `restore_customer_dropped`, `restore_building_retired`, `restore_door_captured_again`, each with its own sentence in `server/db.ts`. Never approve past one. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-audit-decisions.test.ts`; account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "The validation audit".
- 🔴 **A PENDING REVIEW COUNTS ONLY WHILE ITS PLACE IS ACTIVE, AND "VALIDATE AGAIN" IS WITHDRAWN WHEREVER A CUSTOMER ENDS VALIDATED** (audit, 14 Nov 2026) — `activePlaceIds` in the rep's list and the request holds; `withdrawValidateAgainNoticeQuietly` after every approval, restored building and line share. His latest answer is the row decided last (`decidedLater`), never the row created last.
