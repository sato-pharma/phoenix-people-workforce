## Rules

- 🔒 **A VALIDATED PLACE NEEDS THE CUSTOMER'S NAME ON ITS SIGNBOARD, AND A PERSON'S NAME IS NEVER READ ON AN AM PHOTO** (owner, 2026-10-08: "require the customer's name always" and "NEVER A PERSON AM SIGN / PM SIGN"). The old check validated a LOCATION; he grades an IDENTIFICATION. `judgeSignboard` (`signboard-rules.ts`): a confident "no signboard" holds on either list; AM reads place names only; PM must name the person. 🔴 **`unjudged` NEVER holds** (no key, a failed call, an unreadable sign) — the key has run dry silently twice. Pharmacies are never read. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`.
- 🔒 **A REFUSAL TELLS THE REP WHAT IS WRONG WITH HIS PHOTO, FROM EIGHT FIXED REASONS, AND NEVER WHO REFUSED** (owner, 2026-10-08; the seventh and eighth added 2026-10-09 for the timetable — `schedule_other_name`, `schedule_missing`). `VALIDATION_REFUSAL_REASONS` (`validation-feedback.ts`) is closed, and `reasonsFor(kind)` splits it: a signboard refusal is never offered the timetable pair, and a timetable refusal is never offered `photo_of_paper` — a timetable IS paper, so that reason is wrong by construction. 🔴 **The live CHECK is the one `20261010013001` recreates**: it drops the 2026-10-08 constraint, so reading only the older migration reports six while the database takes eight. The free note stays the record's. The notice `phoenix_crm.validation_not_confirmed` is in-app and Telegram only, push-blocked, never email; the system's sentence says "this place". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-refuse-validation.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-email-block.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A ROW SHOWS "Validate again" OR "Not validated yet", NEVER BOTH** — the pill and title come from `google-place-rules.ts`; `list-customer.tsx` once hard-coded the first and showed the pair one under the other.
- 🔴 **THE LIVE "TO VALIDATE" ORDER IS `validateFirstIn`, NOT `rankValidateNext`** — capture-again customers first (oldest reopening first, rose), then visits-waiting-first. `rankValidateNext` has no caller in `src/` or `app/`; changing it changes nothing a rep sees. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-validate-next.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE PLACE CHECKS PAGE READS EVERY ROW, PAGED** — 598 of 643 validated places had never been looked at when it showed a capped slice (2026-10-08). Every read goes through `readEveryPage` / `selectByIds`; `capped` tells the board when a read hit its ceiling. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-checks-in-overflow.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE SAME-SEAT DUPLICATE MERGE ARCHIVES THE LOSER, NEVER DELETES IT, AND RUNS DRY BY DEFAULT** — a deleted reference row is re-minted. `scripts/phoenix-crm-merge-same-seat-duplicates.ts` writes a report, a plan and a pre-image; `--apply` needs all three and an actor, and `phoenix_crm_merge_duplicate_customer` re-checks every guard under the seat's lock. The keeper keeps every place. A bulk production change: announce it first. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-duplicate-merge.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE SIGNBOARD READ CROPS THE EVIDENCE STAMP OFF FIRST, FROM `drawStamp`'s OWN ARITHMETIC** (`stampBandFraction`), or the model reads the stamp's customer name and calls it a match. The timetable read (`server/schedule-check.ts`) crops the same band for the same reason. `brick_lookup` records whether a place found a brick, found none nearby, or could not look, and `resolvePlaceLocation` lives in `server/place-location.ts` so a validation, a move and a venue share ONE resolver without `list.ts` and `shared-places.ts` importing each other. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-brick-lookup.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **A SHARED PLACE IS CAPTURED ONCE AT ITS DOOR AND ITS CUSTOMERS ARE SEATED IN IT WITH A TIMETABLE PHOTO — NO SECOND SIGNBOARD, NO LOCATION AT ALL** (owner, 2026-10-09: validate the polyclinic once, then register each doctor's times in it, max 3 a day, a timetable photo at each one, "the administrator has the right to review the schedule"). The venue (`phoenix_crm_shared_places`, kinds polyclinic · medical centre · hospital outpatient — 🔴 **the medical tower was taken off the chooser the same day**, *"remove medical tower option"*, narrowed in the table CHECK and the create RPC while 0 venues existed) takes the full place gate — two readings inside 250 m and a live photo whose sign names the BUILDING, so `judgeSignboard` runs in its **AM place-name** mode — and 🔴 **no Google listing check at all**, because that check judges the customer's name from the list and a venue has no list name ("even if no Google check, he must validate by a live photo and location"). A seated customer is an ORDINARY `phoenix_crm_places` row: `shared_place_id` set, `point_source = 'shared_place'`, the venue's point, brick, area, region and address copied, `photo_path` the TIMETABLE and `schedule_check` its read — so he is on the Place checks page from the moment he is saved and `refuseValidatedPlace` already reopens him. 🔴 **Rejecting the VENUE retires it, retires every place seated in it and sends each of those customers back to To validate** (`phoenix_crm_decide_shared_place`); the venue keeps its own decision columns rather than a `phoenix_crm_validation_reviews` row, whose `place_id`/`customer_id` are NOT NULL. `unjudged` never holds, here as everywhere. 🔒 **A VENUE IS VISIBLE ONLY TO THE SEAT THAT CAPTURED IT — NEVER A SHARED POOL** (owner, 2026-10-09: *"every polyclinic appears only to the rep validated it. No sharing."*): `loadSharedPlacesFor` filters `.eq("seat_id", …)`, two reps on one building each capture it, and those duplicate rows are CORRECT — a venue carries the capturing rep's own signboard photo, his two readings and his `created_by_hr_id`, so seating a customer on another rep's capture would stand that place behind a photo its own rep never took. Widening it to `.in("seat_id", …)`, a line, area, region or brick is the defect, not a tidy-up. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`.
- 🔴 **THE NIGHTLY SIGNBOARD PASS READS 200 A NIGHT AND IS ENDED BY ITS BUDGET, NOT BY ITS LIMIT** (raised 60 -> 200 on 2026-10-09, at the owner's instruction). The first night read its 60 between **03:35:04 and 03:35:45 Cairo — 42 seconds**, about 1.4 places a second with four in flight, and stopped on the LIMIT with 547 waiting and its 120-second budget barely touched. 🔴 **The limit now deliberately exceeds what the budget can reach (200 ≈ 140 s), and that mismatch is the design, never something to tidy** — a normal night stops on `SIGNBOARD_SWEEP_BUDGET_MS` with `moreWaiting` true, oldest first, the rest tomorrow, and a night where every read burns its full 8-second timeout still fits only ~60. Raising the BUDGET spends the behaviour pass's room inside the same 300 s cron; raising the limit does not. Concurrency stays 4. Pinned by `checkSweepRate()` in `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`.
