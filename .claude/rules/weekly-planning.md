## Rules

Moved out of `crm` on 2026-10-18 so its injected pointer stays under the 4,000-byte margin `tests/module-memory.test.ts` enforces. **`crm` keeps every glob it had**, so a session working on these files loads BOTH files.

### Week keys

- 🔴 **A WEEK KEY NAMES THE ISO WEEK THAT ENDS ON ITS SUNDAY, SO `sundayFromWeekKey` IS Monday(W) + 6 — NEVER Monday − 1.** `buildWeekKey` is the convention's only authority: `employee_plan_headers.period_key` and `phoenix_crm_plans` already hold keys it produced, and production has 2026-W41 running 11–15 October 2026 (read live 2026-10-12), which is its answer. Reading the Sunday as the day before the ISO Monday made `submissionDeadlineForWeek`, `managerApprovalDeadlineForWeek` and `ceoApprovalDeadlineForWeek` fire a WHOLE WEEK early — W41's submission deadline landed on Thursday 1 October, ten days before the week it gates — and the foundation test pinned that wrong direction ("Sunday of W24 = 2026-06-07" when `buildWeekKey` answers `2026-W23`). Fixed 2026-10-12. Never move `buildWeekKey` to meet a helper; the stored keys decide. `planDeductionGraceDeadlineForMonday` takes a Monday DATE deliberately and stays as it is. Pinned by block 9b of `tests/weekly-planning-foundation.test.ts` — a 427-day round trip against `getSundayOfWeek`.

### Which days and halves must be planned

- 🔴 **THE GROUP PRODUCT MANAGER — AND ONLY SHE — MAY PLAN A WORKING DAY AS AM ONLY OR PM ONLY; SHE NEVER HAS TO SUBMIT A FULL DAY** (owner, 2026-10-18: *"only the group product manager make her okay to work only AM or only PM … doesn't have to submit a full day"*). `resolvePlanWorkweek` gives her `GROUP_PRODUCT_MANAGER_WORKWEEK` (Sunday–Thursday, every day `"half"`); every working day still needs one half. `planDayGapText` is the one completeness rule the form's banner and `assertSubmissionComplete` both read, and the form shows her "AM only" / "PM only" beside "Full day" and "AM / PM". A Product Manager, the Marketing Manager and everyone else keep full days. Pinned by `tests/plan-workweek-linkage.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **AN APPROVED HALF-DAY VACATION OR EXCUSE COVERS ONLY ITS OWN HALF, SO THE OTHER HALF OF THAT DAY STILL NEEDS A PLAN** (owner, 2026-10-19). `planDayGapText` takes a third set: the halves held by work rather than leave (`isPlanLeaveActivity` names Vacation and Excuse). On a half-day planner's day, leave alone on one half reads "(afternoon missing)" or "(morning missing)". Leave on both halves, or a full-day leave, completes the day, and a National Vacation is seeded as the whole day. The form's banner and `assertSubmissionComplete` both pass the work-only halves. Pinned by `tests/plan-workweek-linkage.test.ts`.

### Deadlines

- 🔴 **THE NO-WEEKLY-PLAN DEDUCTION DEADLINE IS THE END OF THE MONDAY, 23:59:59.999 CAIRO, SO A PLAN SENT BEFORE MIDNIGHT IS ON TIME** (owner, 2026-10-19). `planDeductionGraceDeadlineForMonday` adds the last 59.999 seconds to the 23:59 instant. The loader tests `submittedAt > deadline`, so a plan sent at 23:59:30 used to read late. The deduction's reason text still says "(23:59 Cairo)". Pinned by `tests/daily-deductions-no-weekly-plan.test.ts`.

### Editing, reviewing and moving a plan (audit of 2026-10-18)

- 🔴 **WHO MAY SAVE, SUBMIT OR REVISE A PLAN IS `viewerMayEditPlan` — ITS OWNER OR A SUPER ADMIN — AND THE EDITOR AND THE SERVER READ THE SAME FUNCTION** (`plan-editor-state.ts`; `assertPlanOwner` calls it). A manager who opens a report's plan reads it; the form never offers Save, Submit or Add day on someone else's plan. Pinned by `tests/weekly-plan-approver-ui-parity.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **REVIEW COMMENTS ARE PER PLAN — `Record<planId, string>`, NEVER ONE STRING BOUND TO EVERY REVIEW CARD**: the text typed for plan A was recorded on plan B when B was decided. A plan that leaves the queue leaves the bulk selection too (`pruneSelectedPlanIds`). `listPlanComments` asks `read`, like `addPlanComment`, so the CEO and the Manager role see the thread.
- 🔴 **A MONTHLY PLAN MOVED TO ANOTHER MONTH KEEPS EACH ITEM'S DAY-OF-MONTH, AND THE WHOLE MOVE IS REFUSED, NAMING THE DATES, WHEN THE TARGET MONTH LACKS A DAY** — never a clamp and never a fixed day count (a fixed count put Jan 31 in March and Oct 31 on Dec 1). Only a WEEKLY move uses one delta. `movedPlanItemDate` and `planMoveRefusal` in `plan-v1.ts`; the service reads the items before it re-dates the header. Pinned by `tests/weekly-plan-move-period.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE ADHERENCE HISTORY LEAVES OUT DRAFT, CANCELLED, MANAGER-REJECTED AND CEO-REJECTED PLANS** (`PLAN_ADHERENCE_HISTORY_EXCLUDED_STATUSES` in `plan-adherence.ts`): a rejected plan scored "no trace" and counted its week twice beside the replacement. A returned plan is the same plan reworked and IS scored; a pending revision is hidden while the approved plan it revises stands.
- 🔴 **RE-PICKING THE WEEK ALREADY SHOWN, "RESET WORKING DAYS" AND "DISCARD DRAFT" NEVER DROP THE APPROVED-LEAVE AND HOLIDAY ROWS** (`pickedPeriodIsShown`, and `resetRows` merges the fetched prefill). A half-day planner's banner reads "Plan at least the morning or the afternoon of every working day" (`incompleteDaysHeading`), and `planDayRowsForMode` in `plan-slot-merge.ts` is the one day-mode switch, so two rows never share a key.

### Amending an approved plan (migration `20261018233000`)

- 🔴 **THE APPROVED PLAN STAYS THE PLAN OF RECORD UNTIL ITS REVISION IS APPROVED, AND `phx_weekly_plan_decide` CANCELS IT IN THE SAME TRANSACTION AS THE REVISION'S FINAL APPROVAL** — never in application code. Before 2026-10-18 every amendment of an approved plan raised 23505, the form blamed "an active plan", and 23 empty draft revisions piled up on production. `employee_plan_headers_active_period_uidx` counts a row only if it has no parent or is already final_approved / hr_acknowledged, and `employee_plan_headers_one_live_revision_uidx` allows one live revision per parent.
- 🔴 **`parent_plan_id` IS SET ONLY ON A REVISION OF AN APPROVED PLAN; A REVISION OF A REJECTED OR CANCELLED PLAN IS SPAWNED UNLINKED** (`chooseRevisionTarget` in `plan-revisions.ts`), a retry edits the open revision instead of spawning another, and a failed item write takes back the draft that call made. A reader that lists pre-approval statuses passes its headers through `dropPendingRevisionsOfPlansOfRecord`, or the period shows twice.

### Words, exports and bulk approve

- 🔴 **PLAN WORDING IN NOTIFICATIONS, EMAILS, EXPORTS AND CARD TITLES COMES FROM `plan-labels.ts` — NEVER THE RAW `plan_kind`, A `period_key`, AN HR ID OR AN ELLIPSIS** (`planNounLabel`, `planSentenceLabel`, `planHeadingLabel`, `planEmailMetrics`, `decisionActorLabel`). Long text wraps or says in words what was left out; the plan exports carry a Part column (AM / PM / Full day) beside the date. Bulk approve is read per plan from the server rows (`summarizeBulkApprove`): a plan with no answer is failed, failures are named by employee and period, and only the approved ids are un-ticked.
- 🔒 **AN ACTING DISTRICT MANAGER ON A REP TITLE IS A DISTRICT MANAGER FOR PLANS AND THE PLANNING CALENDAR** (owner, 2026-10-20). `summarizePlans` uses `isDistrictManagerPlanSubmitter` and no longer counts a plan waiting on a second approver as CEO pending; the calendar admits District Manager login-role holders (`districtManagerHrIds`). A failed role read falls back to title-only. The CEO approval list in `plans-workspace.tsx` is still title-only (latent: no such seat today). Pinned by `tests/weekly-plans-district-manager-bypass.test.ts`, `tests/weekly-plan-calendar-owner-scope.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **SAME REP + SAME DAY + TWO OF HIS MANAGERS ON THE PLANNING CALENDAR IS A CHAIN VISIT, SHOWN NEUTRALLY, NEVER A WARNING OR A CLASH** (owner, 2026-10-24: *"show as chain visit, not a warning"*). `buildPlanningCalendarConflicts` fires only when ≥2 distinct `employeeHrId` share one `date|medicalRepHrId` key — a Phoenix CRM chain visit (the rep out with their District Manager AND a senior manager), not a booking collision. The field `conflictWarning`/`conflictKey` keeps its internal name but the text it carries reads "Chain visit — … are out with … on the same day"; in `planning-calendar.tsx` and the XLSX export (`calendar-export.ts`) every surface is SKY (the person keeps their own hue, a thin sky ring marks the co-visit), the icon is `Users`, and the words "warning"/"conflict"/"same-line" never reach the user. The live Plans-FORM warning `sameLineBookingWarning` is a different surface and still reads as a warning. No test pins the UI wording.
- 🔒 **THE TRAINING MANAGER SEAT HAS NO WEEKLY / MONTHLY PLAN TAB, NO INTERVIEW CALENDAR TAB, NO VACANCIES TAB AND NO "SUBMIT WEEKLY PLAN" SHORTCUT** (owner, 2026-10-24: "remove interview calendar and old weekly/monthly plan … from training manager account", then "also remove vacancies report"). `resolveEmployeePortalNavVisibility` turns `showPlansTab`, `showInterviewCalendarTab` and `showVacanciesTab` off for that role even when the calendar is shared with him; the Planning Calendar stays. The plan routes themselves are untouched, so the tabs are hidden, not the pages. Pinned by `tests/training-manager-no-plan-interview-tabs.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE PLANNING CALENDAR NEVER DRAWS AN OFFICE OR ADMIN DAY, AND NEVER SHOWS AN EMPTY AREA WHEN THE REP HAS ONE** (owner, 2026-10-24). `getSharedPlanningCalendarWorkspace` drops `Office` and `Admin` items through `withoutOfficeDays` before conflicts, `planCount` and the returned items, so the admin page, the portal page, the API and the XLSX export agree; `submittedOwnerCount` still counts the person. An item with no stored area takes the area of the rep it is planned with, then where he actually was that day (`buildPlaceEvidence`: the area of the customers he visited in Phoenix CRM, else the area on a non-rejected expense trip covering the date; owner, 2026-10-24: "check his CRM visits or his expenses, where was he"), then the planner's own (`resolvePlanItemArea`). Pinned by `tests/planning-calendar-hides-office.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **ON THE PLANNING CALENDAR ONE PERSON WITH THE SAME PLAN IN THE AM AND THE PM OF ONE DAY IS ONE "FULL DAY" ENTRY, NEVER TWO HALVES** (owner, 2026-10-24: *"if same employee same day am and pm the same plan area then full day and do not separate in calendar or details"*). `mergeFullDayPlanItems` runs in `getSharedPlanningCalendarWorkspace` right after `withoutOfficeDays`, so the month grid, agenda, details table, XLSX export and `planCount` all show it once. "Same" means activity, title, region, area, line, rep, status and with-manager all match; it keeps the AM item's id and joins distinct notes. Two different halves stay two. Pinned by `tests/planning-calendar-full-day-merge.test.ts`.
