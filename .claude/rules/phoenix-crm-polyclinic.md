## Rules

- 🔒 **A SHARED PLACE IS CAPTURED ONCE AT ITS DOOR, ITS TIMETABLE PHOTO IS ASKED ONCE FOR THE WHOLE BUILDING, AND EVERY CUSTOMER AFTER THE FIRST IS SEATED AGAINST THAT SAME BOARD — NO SECOND SIGNBOARD, NO POINT OF HIS OWN** (owner, 2026-10-09: validate the polyclinic once, then register each doctor's times in it, max 3 a day, "the administrator has the right to review the schedule"). The venue (`phoenix_crm_shared_places`, kinds polyclinic · medical centre · hospital outpatient — 🔴 **the medical tower was taken off the chooser the same day**, *"remove medical tower option"*, narrowed in the table CHECK and the create RPC while 0 venues existed) takes the full place gate — two readings inside 250 m and a live photo whose sign names the BUILDING, so `judgeSignboard` runs in its **AM place-name** mode — and 🔴 **no Google listing check at all**, because that check judges the customer's name from the list and a venue has no list name ("even if no Google check, he must validate by a live photo and location").
- A seated customer is an ORDINARY `phoenix_crm_places` row: `shared_place_id` set, `point_source = 'shared_place'`, the venue's point, brick, area, region and address copied, `photo_path` the BUILDING's one TIMETABLE and `schedule_check` its read — so he is on the Place checks page from the moment he is saved and `refuseValidatedPlace` already reopens him. 🔴 **Rejecting the VENUE retires it, retires every place seated in it and sends each of those customers back to To validate** (`phoenix_crm_decide_shared_place`); the venue keeps its own decision columns rather than a `phoenix_crm_validation_reviews` row, whose `place_id`/`customer_id` are NOT NULL. A lost call never holds; a timetable unreadable or missing is refused up to 3 tries, then held (2026-10-17, the scanner bullet below). Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`.
- 🔒 **A VENUE IS VISIBLE ONLY TO THE SEAT THAT CAPTURED IT — NEVER A SHARED POOL** (owner, 2026-10-09: *"every polyclinic appears only to the rep validated it. No sharing."*): `loadSharedPlacesFor` filters `.eq("seat_id", …)`, two reps on one building each capture it, and those duplicate rows are CORRECT — a venue carries the capturing rep's own signboard photo, his two readings and his `created_by_hr_id`, so seating a customer on another rep's capture would stand that place behind a photo its own rep never took. Widening it to `.in("seat_id", …)`, a line, area, region or brick is the defect, not a tidy-up.
- 🔴 **A REP SEATS A CUSTOMER ONLY WITHIN 300 m OF THAT BUILDING — NO READING SEATS AS BEFORE** (owner, 3 Nov 2026, chose "Refuse at seating": a doctor seated in a look-alike 3.9 km away refused every visit at his real door). Save reads the location in silence (`standingAt`), `seatingAwayFromBuilding` asks `visitAwayFromValidatedPlace` of the building's two door readings against `visit_far_meters`, both doors ask it before the timetable scan so no try or board is spent, and the row never keeps the reading. A manager beside the rep and the Super Admin's move are not asked. The rep reads `SEATING_AWAY_FROM_BUILDING`; the SFE board reads `far_from_place` with the building. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-seat-at-building.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "Seating is refused away from the building".
- 🔴 **ONE TIMETABLE FOR THE BUILDING, NOT ONE PER DOCTOR** (his amendment, 2026-10-10: *"ask him for only one timetable, not for every doctor"*) — the venue carries it (`schedule_photo_path`, `_captured_at`, `_by_hr_id`), and 🔴 **the SERVER decides reuse from that column, never a flag on the body**: `effectiveSeatedPhoto` takes a sent photo as a REPLACEMENT of the board on the wall, otherwise reuses the venue's, and raises `SEATED_PLACE_NO_PHOTO` only where the venue is the one thing that can answer it — the FIRST customer in a building. So the parse ALLOWS `photoPath: null`, the phone asks for the camera only for that first customer or a rep who said the board changed, and the stamp burned into the image is the BUILDING's name, which is what made one image per doctor unavoidable before.
- 🔴 **Two things had to move with it:** `phoenix_crm_places_photo_uidx` is now PARTIAL (`where shared_place_id is null`), because a single-use photo slot is what used to make a seating idempotent — the refusal `already_seated`, behind a `for update` lock on the venue row, replaces it; and the reused board carries NO device skew (its slot was issued on another day, possibly to another rep) and is read through `downloadVenueTimetablePhoto`, which drops exactly one condition, `issued_to_hr_id`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`.
- 🔴 **A HELD VENUE CAN BE CHOSEN AND SEATED IN, BUT ITS CUSTOMERS STAY PENDING, IN NO KPI, UNTIL THE SUPER ADMIN APPROVES THE VENUE — NEVER GREY IT OUT** (owner, 2026-10-14: *"make him able to choose him even if the system is still checking"*, then *"not counted in KPIs until the admin confirm the polyclinic and the doctors in it"*). The picker, `sharedPlaceForSeating` and `phoenix_crm_insert_place` accept it; `phoenix_crm_validate_customer` (`20261014200147`) leaves the seated customer `pending` (planned and visited, but every KPI reads `customer_validated`); APPROVING the venue confirms them, except no board, a board read `other_name`/`no_schedule`, or a pending validation review; settle never releases one while held; a rejection still retires them all. The rep's row never says "checking"; the Super Admin's own MOVE still refuses a held venue. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-shared-places.md`, "A held venue can be chosen".
- 🔴 **A REJECTED VENUE TELLS EVERY REP IT REOPENED, AND IN THE VENUE'S OWN WORDS — NEVER THE SIX SENTENCES ABOUT A CUSTOMER'S OWN DOOR** (owner, 2026-10-09: "fix the polyclinic rejection so the rep is told the reason"). It reopened them in SILENCE until then. `decideSharedPlace` now raises `validation_not_confirmed` for each customer the RPC returns in `reopened` (only those really back to `pending`), with `via: "shared_place"` → `sharedPlaceRefusalRepText`, and the VENUE's id as the review id: a venue has no review row, and a rejected one is retired for good, so that is one key per refusal. 🔴 **And the board cannot read the reason off a review row either — a venue refusal writes none**: "Waiting to be captured again" showed "A rep" and "No reason was chosen" while `no_signboard` sat on the venue, so `loadSharedPlaceRefusalsForCustomers` joins the customer's RETIRED seated place → its venue and names it beside the reason. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-shared-place.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **ONLY THE SUPER ADMIN SEATS A STANDALONE CUSTOMER IN A VALIDATED POLYCLINIC, THE PLACE ADOPTS THE VENUE'S POINT EXACTLY, AND A BUILDING WITHOUT A TIMETABLE HOLDS HIM OUT OF KPIs BY SENDING HIM BACK TO `pending`** (owner, 2026-10-10). `seatPlacesInVenue` gates on `canDecideSharedPlaces`, never the SFE Manager; `phoenix_crm_seat_places_in_venue` sets `shared_place_id`, `photo_path` and `point_source` in ONE statement and nulls the `moved_*` fields, or two live constraints fail. 🔴 **`unjudged` never holds him, and a seated pending customer is never offered Validate** — his own door would undo the move.
