## Rules

### The bot's own words, and who owns them

- 🔴 **EVERY WORD A WHATSAPP APPLICANT READS IS REWORDED BY THE OWNER ON THE SCREENING QUESTIONS SCREEN — rewording one is never a code change again** (owner, 2026-10-03: *"i want to be in control to freely edit them through phoenix anytime"*). Three stores, one rule: the six automatic replies in `ats_whatsapp_auto_replies`, the six questions and their answers in `ats_whatsapp_question_prompts`, the owner's own questions in `ats_whatsapp_screening_questions`. **An ABSENT row or NULL column means the code default** — reset is a DELETE, blank is "keep the code's own", and no default text is ever copied into the database. **The list's BUTTON and its ANSWER HEADING are editable too** (`button_label` 20, `section_title` 24 — Meta refuses the whole message over either): drawn AROUND the message, not inside it — an Arabic question used to sit under an English "Choose a role". **Keep `{maxMb}` in the too-large reply.** Never editable: an answer's CODE, the closing summary's generated middle lines. Pinned by `tests/whatsapp-auto-replies.test.ts` and `tests/whatsapp-question-prompts.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **EVERY WORD A WHATSAPP APPLICANT READS IS ENGLISH — the bot READS Arabic and ANSWERS in English** (owner, 2026-10-04: *"i want whatsapp replies all to be in english"*). Before it, four of the six automatic replies opened with an Arabic block and all 8 region + 49 area second lines were Arabic bricks — and the tests of the day pinned exactly that (*"Arabic comes FIRST"*), so those pins were **inverted, not deleted**. 🔴 **What the bot READS is untouched**: `ENQUIRY_MARKERS` and the alef/ya/ta-marbuta folding in `whatsapp-text-only-applicant.ts` are Arabic on purpose and are how an Arabic enquiry is recognised at all. The place hints are now transliterations (`Smouha · Sporting · El Hadra`); the bricks keep their Arabic names in the sales database, because nothing reads a hint back. **It pins the DEFAULTS only** — he can still word any reply in any language on the screen. One sweep over replies + questions + region hints + area hints + built menus: `tests/whatsapp-auto-replies.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE WHATSAPP ROLE LIST IS FULL — 10 OF META'S 10 ROWS; THE NEXT ROLE REPLACES ONE, NEVER APPENDS** (owner, 2026-10-06 added HR Section Head, then had the Human Resources row removed; Personnel Specialist took the last slot 2026-10-31). Rows now: Medical Rep, District Manager, Regional Manager, Product Manager, National Sales Manager, HR Section Head, Talent Acquisition, Office Coordinator, Personnel Specialist, Other. `finance` and `human_resources` are the two rows taken off — still families (the title classifier files a typed "Other" into them) and still in the pool the Screening Questions screen can switch back on; do not restore either unasked. `hr_section_head` needs no migration (`job_title_family` is unconstrained) and is not asked background, region or area (their saved audiences list families and omit it, as for HR).
- 🔒 **THE HR SECTION HEAD FLOW RUNS ON TWO GENERIC RULES, `show_if` AND `end_on`, THAT ANY QUESTION CAN USE — AND SINCE 20 OCT 2026 IT KEEPS ONLY THREE `hrsh_*` QUESTIONS OF ITS OWN** (owner, 2026-10-06; migration `20261006120000_ats_screening_questions_conditional_flow`; account: `docs/recruitment-hr-section-head-flow.md`). The other five were merged into the shared keys by "one question, one name" (next rule): Cairo is `oc_cairo` (its `end_on` message was already identical), age `field_age_band`, start date `oc_start_date`, ATS and med-rep hiring `hr_ats_experience`/`hr_medrep_hiring` — now asked WITHOUT the `hrsh_pharma` condition. What stays its own: `hrsh_residence` (`show_if` re-pointed to `oc_cairo`, sort 1 so it still follows Cairo), `hrsh_pharma`, `hrsh_current_salary`. `show_if` asks a question only after an earlier answer (an option CODE, never a title); `end_on` ENDS the conversation on an answer and sends the owner's message instead of the summary, answers still recorded. The Cairo question ends on No — one of the TWO notices he allows automatically, the other being the Product Manager's experience gate below; every other rejection stays manual. **A role added later must be added to the built-in `experience` and `salary` audience lists** (they are explicit lists excluding `hr_section_head`) to be asked them. The careers form (`role=hr_section_head` in `website-apply.ts`) enforces both rules server-side and sends NO confirmation e-mail to an ended application.
- 🔒 **THE PRODUCT MANAGER SEAT'S FIRST QUESTION IS A GATE — UNDER A YEAR AS A PRODUCT MANAGER ENDS THE APPLICATION, IN BOTH CHANNELS** (owner, 2026-10-16: *"no pass if more than 1 year make him continue"*; migration `20261016151244`). Five `pm_*` questions in order: years as a Product Manager in pharma (`end_on` over `not_yet` AND `lt_1` — a gate listing one of them passes a sub-year applicant), new-product launch, Greater Cairo (a PREFERENCE, so no `end_on`), Chest/ENT/Urology, PowerPoint/Excel/Word — then the built-in expected salary and the SHARED `oc_start_date` row, widened to this family at sort 300 so it stays last for all five that get it. 🔴 **No English question — he excluded it — and no self-rated communication question.** `product_manager` is registered on the careers form in `website-apply.ts`, which forces its `ROLE_MATCHERS` regex in `app/api/public/apply/counts/route.ts`. `oc_start_date` exists on PRODUCTION only, so the widening is a no-op on staging. Pinned by `tests/website-apply-form.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE PERSONNEL SEAT ASKS WHICH GRADE FIRST AND TURNS NOBODY AWAY** (owner, 2026-10-31; migration `20261031194736_ats_personnel_specialist_hiring`). One family, `personnel`, one tab and one careers key for both Personnel Specialist and Senior Personnel Specialist; `pers_position` (sort 2) asks the grade. Five `pers_*` work questions (sort 90–94: years in personnel, headcount, insurance forms, payroll inputs, labour office), seven shared rows widened to it, the built-in expected salary ON and experience/background OFF; NO `end_on` anywhere. A built-in is always asked before every extra, so expected salary comes first. The Talent Acquisition seat never sees this tab (`HR_ROLE_TABS`, `HR_ROLE_FAMILIES`, `ats_workspace_filter`).
- 🔒 **ONE QUESTION, ONE NAME — A QUESTION TWO ROLES ARE ASKED IS ONE ROW WITH BOTH ROLES ON IT, NEVER A TWIN UNDER A SECOND KEY** (owner ticked it 2026-10-20; migration `20261020020000_ats_recruitment_one_question_one_name`). Age is `field_age_band` (5 bands) for every role that is asked it, start date `oc_start_date`, Cairo `oc_cairo`. The key is immutable, so a merge KEEPS one row, widens `applies_to_role_families`, sets the twin `is_active = false` (never deleted) and rewrites the twin's key in candidates' `screening_extra_answers`, sessions' `extra_answers` and `extra_current_question_key`, and every `show_if`. `oc_age_band`'s 3-band answers are kept as they are — never mapped onto 5 bands by a guess — and `AGE_BAND_ANSWER_KEYS` reads both. Talent Acquisition got the `human_resources` questions (`human_resources` stays beside it only for three month-old open chats); RM/AM + NSM got age, start date and `mgr_team_size`.
- 🔴 **EVERY ANSWER REACHES THE CANDIDATE THE MOMENT IT IS GIVEN — NEVER ONLY WHEN THE CHAT COMPLETES** (owner ticked "recover the half-finished chats", 2026-10-20: 27 chats' answers had never reached the record). `applyAnswerToCandidate` (`whatsapp-answer-sync.ts`) finds the candidate by the SAFE three-rule link only (never `findCandidateForSender`: a forwarder's newest CV is the wrong person), merges one key through `ats_merge_screening_answer` (never a whole-map replace, which clobbered HR edits), upserts the placement on a role/region/area answer and recomputes completeness. The applicant answering a key clears HR's "added by HR" mark on it. The pending backfill applies OPEN sessions too.
- 🔴 **THE IN-WINDOW RE-ASK IS OFF UNTIL HE SWITCHES IT ON, AND THE AFTER-24-HOURS TEMPLATE IS SENT ONLY ON HR's PRESS** (owner, 2026-10-20: *"stays off until you switch it on"*). `ats_recruitment_settings.whatsapp_reask_enabled` starts `false`; the cron re-sends the current question ONCE (`reasked_at`), and a run where every send failed throws (`assertReaskReachedSomeone`), so the job register reads it as failed; since 2 Nov 2026 it also holds a Healthchecks check, handed over from the fraud scan on his word. "Ask again on WhatsApp" sends `phx_application_followup` (his wording: *"Hello {name}, thank you for applying to Sato Pharma. A few questions about your application are still unanswered. Reply to this message to continue."*) through `startInboxConversation` from the recruitment line; the reply resumes the chat and asks only what is missing (`decideTextOnlyAction`'s `resume` comes BEFORE `hasRecentCv`/`new_contact`, or a person answering the template is told to send a CV).
- 🔒 **A WHATSAPP USERNAME SENDER (NO PHONE FROM META) IS ASKED THE QUESTIONS TOO — THEIR CONVERSATION IS KEYED ON THEIR USER ID, AND EVERY SEND ADDRESSES IT THROUGH `recipient`, NEVER `to`** (owner, 2026-10-20: a woman wrote to the HR line from a username and nothing was asked). `whatsapp-recipient.ts` moves an id shaped `EG.<digits>` into `recipient` on the wire only; the webhook opens the questions on such a sender's CV document and routes their taps and typed answers by `message.userId`. Interns (videos, FTP submission ids) never reach this branch, and a greeting from a username sender is still only recorded. Pinned by `tests/whatsapp-username-recipient.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE STORED `show_if` IS `{question_key, values}` — NEVER READ THE RAW COLUMN, ALWAYS `showIfFromRow`** (found 2026-10-06: the raw column has no `questionKey`, so the three HR Section Head follow-ups never showed on WhatsApp or the careers form while camelCase unit tests stayed green). Account: `docs/recruitment-hr-section-head-flow.md`.
- 🔴 **THE PUBLIC CAREERS FORM FAILS CLOSED AND HAS A DAILY AI CEILING** (23 Oct 2026 audit, P5-02). reCAPTCHA with its secret unset or Google unreachable answers 503 `captcha_unavailable` — never a pass; a failed challenge stays 400. The paid model read of a CV from that form is capped at `PUBLIC_APPLY_AI_READS_PER_DAY` (100, a proposal awaiting his figure) across all senders; past it, or when the counter cannot be read, the CV is still captured and read the free way (rules, then OCR). Email, WhatsApp and recruiter uploads are not gated. Pinned by `tests/public-intake-limits.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **LOSING THE OPEN-CONVERSATION RACE IS `already_open`, NEVER `send_failed`.** Two CVs from one sender in the same second both find no open session; the partial unique index lets one insert land and that request asks question one, so the loser’s 23505 answers `already_open` in `startClassification` instead of logging a crash (3 Oct 2026 21:12 Cairo, System Audit APP-B05C8CA4). Pinned by `tests/system-audit-root-cause-fixes.test.ts`.
