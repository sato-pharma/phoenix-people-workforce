## Rules

Every ruling below is the owner's, given in chat on 2026-10-16 across five rounds. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.md`.

- 🔒 **A DISTRICT MANAGER'S WEEK IS ITS OWN TABLE FAMILY — NEVER A ROW IN `phoenix_crm_plans`.** Seven `phoenix_crm_manager_*` tables (`20261017000000`). Every rep-plan reader scans by seat or week (`crm_plan_entries_unified`, the reports, the expiry passes, the visit save's sent-plan check), and `phoenix_crm_save_plan` refuses a seat that owns no customers. A rep-plan reader that starts reading these tables is the bug.
- 🔴 **TICKING A REP IS NOT A DOUBLE VISIT** — the actual of a with-a-rep half-day is only the double or chain visit the REP logs naming him, per customer (`phoenix_crm_countable_visits` + `countsAsManagersDoubleVisit`; `phoenix_crm_report_manager_visits` has no customer in it); a single-visit half-day is judged by his own `phoenix_crm_manager_visits`; an Admin half-day is not checkable. Only a SENT week is judged (`isManagerPlanSent`). Unplanned visits are listed apart. No calendar or report calls a with-a-rep half-day a Double Visit — it reads Field.
- 🔒 **HIS OWN SINGLE VISITS COUNT IN HIS OWN REPORT ONLY** — nothing reads `phoenix_crm_manager_visits` into `phoenix_crm_countable_visits`, `crm_visits_unified` or any KPI. The old "My own visit" is CLOSED for a DM or acting-DM seat holder once he plans here (`managerOwnVisitClosedNow` — seat AND switch, unreadable = open; refused in `saveVisit`, hidden in the visit form); the 25 already logged stay as they are. Location optional, NO photo, products and comment optional.
- 🔴 **A REP STILL IN THE OLD CRM SHOWS HIS iReports360 WEEK ON THE BOARD, AND THE DM APPROVES IT FROM THE TEAM TAB** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"their weekly plans on the CRM must appear to the district manager … make sure the district manager can validate the reps' CRM and the old CRM"*; board "their old-CRM plan, tickable", validate "approve the rep's weekly plan"). `loadRepWeeks` reads the old plan ONLY for a rep with NO Phoenix customer that week (state `old_crm`, never over a Phoenix draft), from the vendor's OWN rows — `crm_plan_entries_unified` hides every started-team rep and read 0 of 22 (2026-10-18); the vendor plan has no AM/PM, so a contact goes by his Phoenix LIST's half, a pharmacy is left out, an unmatched contact shows and cannot be ticked. Approval is the vendor's own supervisor approval (`setPlanVerification`, status 0 → 1, take-back 1 → 0, an area manager's 2 never touched), reached by `POST plans/old-crm/decide` with the admin board's gates; the Super Admin and a test login decide nothing; no "return" exists. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.md`, "A rep who still works in the old CRM".
- 🔒 **THE REP NEVER SEES ANY OF IT** — no screen, no notice, no flag. Notices go in-app, Telegram and phone, never email, behind the live-team gate on the DM seat.
- 🔴 **A DISTRICT MANAGER'S APPROVER IS HIS RSM OR NSM (`territoryReviewer`), AND AFTER A REGIONAL MANAGER APPROVES, THE NSM ABOVE HIM ALWAYS APPROVES TOO — NEVER SKIPPED** (owner, 2026-10-17: *"if regional manager applicable then after the rm approve the nsm also must approve never skip"*). Read from the organogram in `managerPlanApprovers`, so every RSM seat gets it — not only Hany Saad → Ali Mohamed Ali; an empty NSM seat passes the step up to the CEO, and the database closes a week at one approval only when no second approver was stored on send, never by who decides. The same two steps decide his ask to edit a sent week and his ask to remove a customer from his own list (*"Week + edit asks + removals"*): the second approver is stored on the ask, the regional manager's approval passes it on, a refusal at either step closes it, and the page sends the `stage` it showed. No HR acknowledgement, no SFE step. Decided in the portal Approvals Hub — Approve, Return the week, Return one day. The Administrator may act in place of any holder at any stage, recorded and shown as "by the Administrator".
- 🔴 **THE HUB COUNTS A MANAGER PLAN ONCE — through `getPlanApprovalsForResponder` and nothing else.** That one merge feeds the badge, Needs You, the overdue check and the bell's `plan` domain; adding it to `countPhoenixCrmWeeklyPlansWaitingForYou` or `overview.waitingForYou` counts it twice. Its notices register under the `plan` domain — `phoenixCrmPlan` reads only `phoenix_crm_plans` and would archive them on first read.
- 🔒 **TWO LISTS, NEVER MERGED — HIS REPS' LISTS AND "MANAGER'S OWN LIST".** A typed name matching an existing customer is OFFERED first, never refused (namesakes are real); there is no validation step ("they are managers"); A/B/C and potential grade both required, and an **area** of his region; the **speciality, phone, address and brick are all OPTIONAL** (owner, 2026-10-18 — "make everything optional" reversed a "must enter" of the night before), filled in by hand or by Capture location (`customers/locate` reads the capture and selects the area and brick; he can still correct it). **Area and brick are CASCADING drop-lists** over his region (`customers/bricks`), the brick one of the chosen area's own, never typed. Removing one from his own list needs his RSM/NSM's approval in the Hub.
- 🔴 **A REP'S DRAFT CUSTOMERS SHOW AS "not sent", A REP WITH NO PLAN IS SKIPPED (UNLESS HE FILED THE WEEK IN THE OLD CRM — THEN THAT PLAN SHOWS), AND A CUSTOMER THE REP LATER DROPS KEEPS THE DM'S ROW FLAGGED "no longer on the rep's day"** — worked out when READING. There is deliberately no foreign key to `phoenix_crm_plan_items`: the rep's own save rewrites those rows. A rep who sends after the DM: the DM asks to edit and his RSM/NSM approves the reopening.
- 🔴 **ONE WEEK ONLY — THE COMING SATURDAY'S, DUE THURSDAY 23:55 CAIRO; A LATE SEND IS ACCEPTED AND STAMPED LATE.** Saturday–Wednesday AM and PM, Thursday AM only, never Friday, a holiday holds nothing.
- 🔒 **THE LEGACY WEEKLY PLAN SWITCHES OFF PER MANAGER, ONLY ONCE A TEAM OF HIS IS LIVE** (`dmPlansInPhoenixCrm`) — a DM with his own team; an RSM or NSM with the first live team below his seat (`seniorPlansInPhoenixCrm`, 2026-10-17); a product manager never — and never for a title that still owes it (`owesAWeeklyPlan`, the no-weekly-plan charge's own reading).
