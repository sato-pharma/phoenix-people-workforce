## Rules

Moved out of `phoenix-crm-approvals` on 2026-10-20 so its injected pointer stays under the 4,000-byte

- 🔒 **A REP PLANS ONE WEEK — THE ONE STARTING THE COMING SATURDAY — AND A SENT WEEK CHANGES ONLY BY REQUEST: THE DM, THEN SFE, AND SFE'S YES ALONE REOPENS IT — THE ADMINISTRATOR IS NO STEP SINCE 2026-10-08** (owner, 2026-10-01). `repPlanWeek`/`repWeekState`; the server refuses every other week on save and send — **except the week he is IN while he never sent it: he still sends it late, stamped late, and its visits count as planned only from the send** (owner, 2026-10-02); the rep has no month send and no take-back. A week is due the Thursday before it **at 23:55 Cairo, an INSTANT and never the end of that day** (owner, 2026-10-02 — `phoenix_crm_week_deadline_at` / `planDeadlineAt`), written on every due date a rep or a reviewer reads (`withPlanDeadline`); a late one is still accepted and stamped from the SERVER clock — never refused. `plannableWeeks` is the REVIEWERS' window, not the rep's. Every step of the request, like the rest of Phoenix CRM, goes in-app, by Telegram and to the phone and NEVER by email (`PHOENIX_CRM_EMAIL_EVENT_TYPES` holds only the recorded-visit decision since 2026-10-17); the EXPIRY, which no person takes, is in-app and Telegram only, not even the phone (owner, 2026-10-02 — see the expiry rule above). Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "Weekly only" and "The deadline is 23:55 Cairo".
- 🔴 **THE PM / AM DAILY CAPS ARE RE-CHECKED WHEN THE REP *SENDS* THE WEEK, NOT ONLY WHEN HE SAVES A DAY** (production, 2026-10-03: an APPROVED week held **18 PM** on its Saturday against `pm_daily_cap` 15). `resolvePlanItems` tests the caps against the items of THAT SAVE — only the days he touched — and `transitionPlan` did no item or cap check at all; the `phoenix_crm_plan_transition` RPC checks none either. 🔴 **One rule, one counter, one sentence for both paths** (`planCapRefusal` / `countPlanDays`): the send calls `planSendCapRefusal` over EVERY stored item of the week in ONE read, and narrowing that read back to the changeable days is the hole itself. 🔴 **It may only NAME a day the rep can still change** (`repChangeableDays`), so an over-cap day that has passed — or a week returned for one OTHER day — sends rather than trapping a plan nobody can fix, and a reviewer's `approve` is deliberately NOT refused. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-plan-cap-on-send.md`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-cap-on-send.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A PLAN DAY IS ASKED DAY BY DAY — `planDayStatus`/`isPlanDaySent`, NEVER `isPlanSentForReview` ON THE WEEK, AND NEVER THE PLAN'S STATUS COLUMN** (the audit of 2026-10-02 found six of these in one module). A week returned for ONE day reads `returned`, so a week-level test answers wrong on every day of it; and in the DATABASE the question is the plan's **latest EVENT**, which a `saved` event overwrites — so a comparison against `hr_acknowledged_at` silently stops lifting a one-day return the moment the rep saves. That is why the reopening (SFE's approval since 2026-10-08, HR's acknowledgement before) now CLEARS `returned_day` **and writes the event** (`20261002221734`) rather than leaving the readers to work it out. Two more of the six were the same confusion counted: the landing chips divided every visit of the day by the PLAN's own denominator ("AM 3 of 2", green with a planned stop undone — off-plan work is counted APART, `alsoTodayParts`), and the week line asked the week's status and so lost its planned figures for a one-day return. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-day-edges.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE MANAGER'S "WEEKLY PLAN TO REVIEW" NOTICE IS WITHDRAWN ONCE THE WEEK IS NO LONGER HIS TO DECIDE** (owner, 2026-10-07). It is the notice this module sends most — thirteen in one evening — and nothing cleared it: measured on production that night, ALL FOUR of one district manager's were dead, two of them the same rep for the same week (the first send, and the re-send after his own return), word for word identical. He opens one, lands on a week reading "Returned for changes", and there is nothing on it to decide — which is what "it comes back with no option to approve" was. **The approve cycle itself is sound: a re-send reaches him and `canApprove` reads `submitted` alone, whatever came before it.** 🔴 **`recordId` IS NOT PERSISTED** — `notification_events` has no column for it — so the plan id travels in the METADATA or the bell can never tell which week a notice is about. `phoenixCrmPlanIdsAwaitingDecision` answers "still HIS" through `canReviewTerritory`, never a second copy of the approve rule, and it is its OWN bell domain because `phoenixCrm` is matched against off-plan VISIT ids. Pinned by `tests/notification-auto-archive.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A WEEK THIS VIEWER CANNOT DECIDE MUST STILL SAY WHOSE TURN IT IS** (owner, 2026-10-07: *"no buttons, but say whose turn it is"*). `PlanDecision` returns null the moment `canApprove` and `canReturn` are both false, and the week panel ENDED there — so a district manager reopening a week he had just returned read a screen with the buttons missing and reported the plan screen as broken. `planTurnSentence` (plan-rules.ts, pure) fills exactly that place: who holds it, when it was decided and by whom, and what brings it back. 🔴 **The RULE did not change — the rep still answers a return before anyone approves again**, and nothing was added to `canApprove`; the sentence is the Team side only, since the rep's own board says it in his send step. A sentence that reads "nothing is waiting" on a week still with someone is the defect it replaced. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-review.test.ts` (section 6).
- 🔴 **THE PLAN STATUS NAMES ITS PERSON, NEVER JUST ITS STATE** (owner, 2026-10-08: *"make the plan status be appearing ex drafted by Mostafa Galal, waiting Dr Taha approval, waiting Mostafa to edit and resend and so on"*). `planStatusLine` (plan-rules.ts, pure) is the ONE composer for every pill, week picker, month picker and Team row — *Drafted by …*, *Waiting for … to approve*, *Waiting for … to edit and resend*, *Approved by …*, *Not started — … drafts it* — fed `describeTerritory`'s resolved `repName`/`reviewerName` plus the week's OWN `decided_by_hr_id`, so an approved week keeps who approved it after the manager changes. 🔴 **No name, no invention:** a vacant seat falls back to `planStatusLabel`'s bare word, never "waiting for null" — and a mapper called without the territory's people is the silent half, since it still returns a valid bare word. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-review.test.ts` §7. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-approval-notices-audit.md`.
- 🔴 **A SENT WEEKLY PLAN IS CHASED, COUNTED ON THE BOARD, AND THE TEAM TAB OPENS ON THE WEEK THAT IS WAITING** (2026-10-08, finishing the 2026-10-07 audit). Four holes, one family: the nudge read no plans at all — `weekly_plan` is a nudge kind now, named FIRST and read from `submitted_at`, so a re-sent week is chased from the RE-SEND; `countPhoenixCrmWeeklyPlansWaitingForYou` is the ONE producer, read by `server/overview.ts` (which held the only copy, inlined) and by the Needs You board, which counted 0; `loadPlanReviews` opens on the earliest week with something at HIS step, never `plannableWeeks()[0]`, because a rep plans the COMING Saturday's week; and the SFE Manager who RETURNED one is told by its own `plan_resent` type, never a recipient on `plan_waiting`, which the bell withdraws the instant the week is not the READER's to decide. 🔴 **A plan has NO SFE step** (`canReviewTerritory`). Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-approval-notices-audit.md`, "the sent weekly plan, chased at last". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-approval-nudge.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-needs-you-source.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-approval-notices.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A SAVE FROM THE MONTH BOARD ANSWERS WITH ITS WEEK AND THE MONTH'S METERS, AND THE BOARD NEVER RE-READS THE MONTH AFTER AN ANSWERED SAVE** (performance audit, 2026-10-16). `loadMonthlyWeekAfterSave` builds the answer and `plan-board-answer.ts` decides whether the board can take it; an answer that is not this board's (another month, a week it does not hold or files differently) or is older than what it holds falls back to the quiet reload, never to a stale board. `phoenix_crm_save_plan` leaves an item sent back unchanged exactly as stored (`20261016180100`), yet still moves the week's version on every save. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-screen-extras.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A REP ASKS TO EDIT ONE WEEK TWICE AT MOST, AND EVERY ASK COUNTS, A REFUSED ONE INCLUDED** (owner, 2026-10-20). `PLAN_EDIT_ASKS_PER_WEEK` (`plan-edit-rules.ts`); `raisePlanEdit` counts the week's requests with NO status filter and refuses the third before anything is written; the week's `canAskToEdit` needs an ask left, so the button gives way to "No asks left this week". No migration: the database lets one request wait per week under the plan's lock, so two presses at once still end at two. A district manager's own week is not capped by it. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-edit-requests.test.ts` §6.
- 🔴 **ON HIS OWN SCREEN THE STATUS SAYS "YOU", NEVER HIS OWN NAME** (owner walk, 2026-10-28: *Drafted by Rasha Sabry* on Rasha Sabry's own board). `planStatusLine` and `managerPlanStatusLine` take `self` — *Drafted by you*, *Waiting for you to edit and resend*, *Not started — you draft it* — set by `selfReader` in `plans.ts` and by `managerHrId === viewer.hrId` in `manager-plans.ts`; the reviewer and the approver are still named. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-review.test.ts` §7 and `tests/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **SUGGEST AND THE COPIES SAY WHAT THEY DID IN HIS WORDS — THE DAY'S HALVES, THE BRICKS BY NAME, AND WHERE THE REST WENT** (the role walk, 2026-10-28: *Placed … across 1 brick* for a day in 7 bricks; *Copied 6* from a week of 14). A customer not validated yet is in iReports360's brick (`reference.brick`) for Suggest's one-area order, its brick names and the brick filter. A full day reports what it holds (`summary.held`); a copy counts a passed day apart (`CopySummary.passed`); the board keeps the day's report (`fillNote`) on the day screen. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-suggest.test.ts` (j) and `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-board.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A NAME OR A SPECIALITY TYPED TWO WAYS IS ONE — SEARCH, SORT AND THE PICKERS FOLD THE SPELLING** (owner walk, 2026-10-28: مستشفى بنها found one spelling; Uro and Urology were two choices). `name-fold.ts` folds أ إ آ to ا, ى to ي, ة to ه and the diacritics; `specialityKey` joins only plain short forms of ONE speciality (`SPECIALITY_SAME`) and drops hidden direction marks — *GP + Pedia* stays apart. The day screen's pickers come from the list ON SCREEN (`plannerFilterOptions`), never the whole list. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-filters.test.ts`.
