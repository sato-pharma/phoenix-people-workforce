## Rules

- 🔴 **A MANAGER'S WEEK HOLDS ONE CONVERSATION FROM ITS FIRST SEND ONWARD, READ AND WRITTEN BY WHOEVER OPENS THAT WEEK IN THE APPROVALS HUB, NEVER A REP AND NEVER BY EMAIL** (*"in new managers weekly plan i want an option to open conversation and people in approval window visible to it"*). Told: the manager, the stored approvers, whoever decided, approvers of a live ask, and anyone who has written. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.md`, "The week's conversation".
