What a number IS stays in `sales-closing`.

## Rules

- 🔒 **THE EMPTY SEATS BOARD RANKS ONLY BY FIGURES PRINTED ON THE ROW, AND AN UNKNOWN IS NEVER A ZERO** (owner, 2026-11-03: "build 2"; `src/lib/reports/empty-seats.ts`). Published closing sales, then target, then days empty; no score. Seats come from the Reps List's cells, figures from the closing engine for each line's newest published month, cut by `resolveVsTargetBound`; the viewer needs both `vacancies:read` and `sales:read`. A seat is folded by today's merges and a row by its own month's, so match through `closingAreaCodesFor`. Visits are "logged", deliberately not a KPI count, and skip copied iReports360 rows. It offers no action.
- 🔴 **EVERY CLOSING LOAD GOES THROUGH `closing-load-gate.ts` — AT MOST 4 AT ONCE PER PROCESS, ONE SHARED READ PER LINE AND MONTH WHILE IT LOADS, AND ONLY THE SCREEN FEED MAY REUSE A READ OF THE LAST 10 SECONDS** (19 Oct 2026, 15:18:50 Cairo: about twenty loads started inside ten seconds, the database median went from 0.15 s to 8.7 s and every other request hit its 15 s limit together). `loadMonthlyClosingPartsWithStoreSplit` is the gate; `reuseMs` is passed only by `app/api/sales/monthly-closing/route.ts`, and Refresh sends `fresh=1` to skip it. Exports, publish, lock and the drift and truth checks never pass it. Every caller gets a copy. Never raise the cap to hide a slow screen; `CLOSING_LOAD_CONCURRENCY` overrides it. Pinned by `tests/closing-load-gate.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A COMMITTED OFFICE BATCH KEEPS THE AREA SHARES OF ITS UPLOAD MOMENT FOR EVER, AND `scripts/check-office-allocation-drift.ts` IS THE ONE THING THAT SAYS SO — the line total agrees on both sides, so no units-based check can.** `src/lib/sales/office-allocation-drift.ts` compares the stored rows with today's shares applied to each line's OWN stored total — never a re-run of the allocator, which drops a net-negative product and moves the total — and names the batches re-touched after the commit. 🔴 **It REPORTS, never repairs, and exits 0 even on drift:** a published month is never corrected, and the read-time respread rule (`closing-office-respread.ts`) is what absorbs this from the October closing. 🔴 **TWO TRUE AUGUST CNS NUMBERS, MEASURED AT DIFFERENT POINTS — NOT A CONTRADICTION TO CHASE:** 3,698 units (3.37%) comparing the Office BATCH's own rows with today's shares, and 6,834 packs (6.23%) through the closing LOADER after the city-split rails. The commit instant is the batch's `created_at`, because a 2026-09-04 18:33 UTC backfill rewrote every older `updated_at` — reading that as the commit reports Jan–Jul clean.

- 🔴 **A month the respread covers is NOT a task on the Needs You board** — the stored-row drift row moves to `worth-knowing` and says the closing figures are already right. Pinned by `tests/closing-office-respread.test.ts`, which also reads the three derivation points and the three Office grids out of SOURCE, and compares the shares with the LOCKED `buildOfficeAreaWeights` because importing it is a cycle. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The Office channel's read-time respread".

- 🔴 **"HAS A PUBLISHED MONTH MOVED" TAKES BOTH LAYERS, AND THE PUBLICATION CHECK'S OWN SUMMARY CANCELS TO ZERO.** `phx_closing_drift_status`'s net units and net value are SUMS over the drifted cells, so a redistribution BETWEEN areas reads `0.000000` on each — Anti Infective 2026-09 read 27 cells and two zeros while 25 areas had moved. 🔴 **The blind spot is the LAYER, not the grain** — its snapshot is `source_of_sales_fact_rollup`, so `au.monthly_store_split_net` never enters it, and publishing ONE line freezes the split for EVERY line in the month, so an unpublished line's frozen net goes stale with nothing announced. Both halves: `src/lib/sales/closing-publication-drift.ts` + `scripts/check-closing-publication-drift.ts`; the Needs You row reads the cheap layers only and SAYS SO (`splitLayerRead`, and `publicationDriftProvenClean` is the only clean verdict). Rebuilding or re-issuing is HIS call on the publication screen, never a session's. Account and every measurement: `docs/closing-publication-drift.md`. Pinned by `tests/closing-publication-drift.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **"ARE THIS MONTH'S BOOKS ALL IN?" IS ANSWERED AGAINST THE MONTH BEFORE, NEVER AGAINST THE WHOLE HISTORY** — `summarizeMonthCompleteness` (`sos-month-complete.ts`; `pnpm run check:sos-month-complete <period>`). Expected = what fed the PREVIOUS month plus whatever another source's file carried for it then, so EPDA-BULK stays expected the month its 2026-07..08 merge window ends — the very input the EPDA-BULK contribution rule spreads — while chimipharm, last seen 2026-05, is not this month's hole; a month with no validated batch is never "complete". 🔴 **The drift read waits for COMPLETE** — on an incomplete month it changes with the next upload. The owner parked September's rebuild on this gate (2026-10-09) and `september-upload-completeness` watches it at 21:30 Cairo. Pinned by `tests/sos-month-complete.test.ts`. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "September's split is rebuilt once its books are all in".

- 🔴 **A SOURCE THE OWNER DECLARES QUIET IS NOT A HOLE — TWO SURFACES HONOUR THAT, AND THE AUDIT BANNER MUST NOT.** The declaration is `SOURCE_QUIET_PERIODS` in `source-quiet-periods.ts` (a leaf; `sos-month-complete.ts` re-exports it). Honouring it: the completeness GATE, and the CLOSING's coverage banner through `partitionAbsentSources` (`source-coverage.ts`), which feeds the screen note, the premium engine note and the workbook's coverage sheet — none of them may say "understated until these arrive" about a book that is never coming (owner, 2026-10-09: "fix the closing banner that still calls epda and mec a hole"). 🔴 **The SoS AUDIT banner (`buildSosCoverageReport`) keeps counting it ABSENT on purpose** — the volume genuinely is missing, so month-over-month is not like-for-like. The per-source table always keeps every row; only the alarm goes, and `quietSources` masks like `missingSources`. Pinned by `tests/source-coverage.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **THE CLOSING LOADER MATCHES A LINE ON ITS LABEL AND FALLS BACK TO THE FIRST ONE SILENTLY** — `loadMonthlyClosingPartsWithStoreSplit({line:"cns"})` returned **Anti Infective's** whole matrix for 2026-09 (measured 9 Oct 2026): no error, the right period, every area row populated, so a restatement prints one line's money under another line's name. Pass the label as the engine spells it (`CNS`, `Anti Infective`, `Pedia`) and **assert `matrix.line` equals what you asked for before printing a number**. Area names carry no space either (`Sohag1`, `Sohag2`). `scripts/restate-area-month.ts` does both, and restates one area through the live engine — own sales, the Office credit as the closing now reads it against the stored one, and the achievement each way.

- 🔴 **THE PUBLISH EMAIL'S ONE LINK IS RESOLVED BY `resolveClosingPublishDestination`, AND ITS ROLE SET MUST STAY EQUAL TO THE MATRIX PAGE'S OWN GUARD.** A single CTA serves every recipient, so the route decides each clicker's page server-side — and it still encoded the pre-2026-09-07 rule (Super Admin, system scope, SFE Manager). When the owner answered the CEO's "Monthly Closing does not appear for me" by admitting him to the PAGE read-only, nothing updated the link: for five weeks the CEO, Cc'd on every line's publish (`FIXED_CC_ROLES`), landed on `/admin/ceo-command-center` instead of the month he had just been told was published. The rule now lives in `src/lib/sales/closing-publish-destination.ts`, and `tests/closing-publish-email-destination.test.ts` reads that page's guard source and fails when the two disagree. 🔴 **A portal recipient gets the PERIOD only** — they never pick a line, it is resolved from their HR_ID.

- 🔴 **A `?line=`/`?period=` THE CLOSING CANNOT HONOUR IS NAMED ABOVE THE MATRIX, NEVER SUBSTITUTED IN SILENCE.** `loadMonthlyClosingParts` validates each one by EXACT membership and otherwise falls back to `availableLines[0]` / `availablePeriods[0]` — the newest month of the first line — so a stale email or bookmark link rendered a complete, ordinary closing for a line or month nobody had asked to see, with nothing on screen saying so. `resolveClosingDeepLinkMiss` and `describeClosingDeepLinkMiss` (`closing-deep-link.ts`) compare what was asked for against what loaded, and the page states both in the same amber row the client picker already uses for a month it could not read. A casing-or-space-only difference is NOT a substitution (the loader's miss lands on the same line), the empty state is not one either, an unparseable month is quoted as it arrived, and the notice clears the moment the reader loads a month themselves. Pinned by `tests/closing-deep-link-silent-substitution.test.ts`, which also pins that fallback as the reason the notice exists.

- 🔴 **THE DETAILS TAB IS ONE MONTH — THE LAST OF THE SPAN — AND IT NOW SAYS SO ON SCREEN.** `mergeMonthlyClosingPeriods` leaves `periods[periods.length - 1]` in a merged matrix's `period`, the workspace hands that single month to `ClosingDetailsPanel`, and the panel loads, totals, filters and **exports** that month alone while the matrix above it totals the whole span. The rows were never wrong — they were unlabelled, which on a reconciliation page reads as the two tabs disagreeing. `describeClosingDetailsSpan` (`closing-details-span.ts`) names the month, the span and how many months are in the figures above but not in these rows; the single-month portal and standalone mounts pass no span and are untouched. 🔴 **Never "fix" this by re-querying each month beside the engine** — a closing figure comes from the closing engine, and the Details grain over a span would be exactly that second query. Pinned by `tests/closing-details-span-notice.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **NOTHING IN THE CLOSING MATRIX IS CUT WITH AN ELLIPSIS — THE HEAD INCLUDED — AND ITS STICKY OFFSET IS MEASURED, NEVER THE LITERAL 44.** The product-name and family-name banners still carried `truncate` long after the area-name cell was brought into line (2026-09-26); the grid is `tableLayout: "fixed"`, so a long name can only wrap or be cut. **The cut was the hidden premise behind `top: 44`** (`h-11` on the row above, written as a number) on every metric sub-header: let a name wrap and the sub-headers stay 44px down. Both banners are now `break-words`, and every sub-header sticks at `var(--phx-closing-subhead-top, 44px)`, published to the table node by a layout effect plus a ResizeObserver — **written to the DOM, never to React state**, because this grid can be hundreds of rows. LATENT when fixed, so no rendering moved: the longest live names are "Tebofortin 120 mg Intens" (24 chars, ~450px banner) and "Lacto Advanced" (14, ~250px). Pinned by `tests/closing-matrix-head-never-ellipsises.test.ts`, which strips comments first — that file's own comments discuss `truncate` at length.

- 🔴 **REFRESH KEEPS THE READER'S FILTER — ONLY THE LINE AND MONTH SELECTS CLEAR IT.** `reload(line, periods, { keepFilter: true })` is the Refresh button alone: same line, same months, same pickers, and clearing there cost a manager their region on every press through an upload, silently. The two selects still clear, because a different line offers different areas and products. A kept filter is narrowed by `narrowClosingFilterToOptions` (`closing-filter-narrowing.ts`) **against the matrix just loaded** — a refresh exists so the closing CAN change under the page, and a selection pointing at an area merged away reads as an empty grid — and whatever fell out is named in the amber row with the **LABEL the picker showed, never a code**. 🔴 **`search` is never narrowed**: it matches text, so a term that finds nothing is a result, not a stale selection. Pinned by `tests/closing-refresh-keeps-the-filter.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **LOCKING A MONTH THAT IS ALREADY LOCKED IS A 200 THAT SAYS SO, NEVER AN ERROR** (2026-11-04). Publishing locks the month by trigger (`phx_freeze_published_closing`, same instant as the publication), and the screen did not re-read the lock, so the owner's next "Lock month" hit `monthly_closing_locks_one_active` (23505) and wrote two `app_error_events` rows. `lockClosing` reads the lock first and treats that 23505 as `alreadyLocked` with "October 2026 Anti Infective is already locked, by <name> at <Cairo time>" (`monthly-closing-lock-conflict.ts`); the screen re-reads the lock after Publish and a ref stops a second request in flight. It never touches the existing lock. A database outage is a 503 "nothing was changed", a failed lock read is UNKNOWN (never "not locked"), and a failed press re-reads the lock (2026-11-06). Pinned by `tests/monthly-closing-lock-already-locked.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **The SoS dashboard cache stores PACKED rows — never hand `unstable_cache` the raw rows.** Raw, a CNS cross-line month was 2.08 MB, over Next's 2 MB entry: production silently stopped caching it, dev crashes. `packSosRows` (gzip + base64, the `packAchievementRows` precedent) returns null on a failed RPC or over `SOS_DASHBOARD_CACHE_MAX_BYTES`, and null reads live. 🔒 **The CEO / Super Admin view is cached too** (owner, 2026-10-04) — never restore the `canSeeCrossLineGeography` bypass. Pinned by `tests/sos-dashboard-packed-cache.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **ONE PUBLISHED LINE AND PERIOD IS RECOMPUTED ONCE — `phx_closing_drift_overview` IS THE ONLY DOOR FOR A DRIFT READ, AND `phx_closing_publication_drift` HAS EXACTLY ONE CALL SITE.** `phx_closing_drift_status` used to pay for `phx_closing_publication_snapshot` twice (the fingerprint, then its own count) and the per-area read paid for it again, so three published lines cost nine recomputes of the whole month: measured on production 2026-10-23, the status call alone 3,635 ms over 77,339 buffers against the overview's 1,808 ms over 39,602. That is what crossed the 8-second cap at 06:36 Cairo and took four Needs You sources amber with it. 🔴 **Never answer an amber drift row by widening a lane or raising the board's budget** — make the source cheaper. Pinned by `tests/closing-drift-acknowledgement.test.ts`, which counts the call sites and re-pins the fingerprint formula on the live migration.
- 🔴 **THE PUBLISHED-CLOSING SNAPSHOT RESOLVES EACH PRODUCT'S PERIOD PRICE ONCE, NOT ONCE PER ROLLUP ROW — `phx_factory_price` IS SECURITY DEFINER, SO THE PLANNER CANNOT INLINE IT AND EVERY ROW-LEVEL MENTION IS A REAL INVOCATION.** `phx_closing_publication_snapshot` called it 18,558 times for CNS 2026-09 to resolve 15 products — 1,645 ms over 38,963 buffers against 177 ms / 702 without the `value` aggregate — which is what timed the board drift source out on 12 of the 15 mornings 9-23 Oct 2026. A distinct-product CTE took it to 241 ms / 969 and the three-line board read to 747 ms against 8 s, byte-identical over 14 line × period pairs (Head Office and both price-override months included). Its column is `period_price`, never `factory_price` — the master's FLAT column. 🔴 **21 other functions still call it per row**, and fixing those means dropping SECURITY DEFINER, which needs its own review. Pinned by `tests/closing-snapshot-prices-once.test.ts`; account in the migration header.
- 🔴 **THE SALES TRUTH CHECK RUNS NIGHTLY; ONLY `sales-truth-declared.ts` IS QUIET** (owner, 2026-11-14). The reconciliation cron carries one check per month (`sales-truth-<YYYY-MM>` from 2026-01, plus `sales-truth-other-months`) in one lane, one month at a time (`reconciliation/checks/sales-truth.ts`); a difference reaches him through System Audit. Declared, each as narrow as its fact: no Sales vs Target before 2026-01 (missing areas only); Jan–Jun 2026 area moves while the line total agrees within 1 EGP; March 2026 CNS 520.10 EGP short (12 CP units of Tebofortine 40 mg the closing books as Tebofortin 120 mg Intens); September 2026's four Assuit rows by line, area and amount. Never widen one to quiet a red check — find what moved and ask him. Pinned by `tests/sales-truth-declared.test.ts`.
