- 🔴 **EVERY HIRING FOLLOW-UP STEP LEAVES A LEDGER ROW AND NEVER CHANGES THE DOOR'S REPLY** (REC-027, 2026-11-13). Both doors (Add Employee and the joining form) wrap the offer link, candidate hired, login, CV, mailbox task, offer stamp and acceptance audit row in `startHireHandoff(...).run` (`src/lib/employees/hire-handoff.ts`, table `hire_handoff_steps`). The ledger re-throws what the step threw and returns what it returned; its writes are queued and a failed write is only logged. The hourly `hire-handoff-reconcile` job retries a step through an idempotent writer and gives up after 5 attempts; Needs You `hire-handoff-gaps` shows the person by name. Never unwrap a step, never let a ledger write fail a hire, and never add a retry that sends an email. Pinned by `tests/hire-handoff-steps.test.ts`; account: `docs/recruitment-offer-hire-sync.md`.
