An employee belongs from the later of their joining date and the policy's start until `employeeExitDay`. Exclude is one press on the add list (a `keep_out` exception, reason "Does not want medical insurance") or, for someone on the register, an end on their first day with `keepOut`; the exceptions list reverses it. The list decides nothing: a person acts on it.
- 🔒 **A DELETION GOES WITH THE DATE TYPED BESIDE THE PERSON, AND STARTS AS FAR BACK AS LIBANO-SUISSE ACCEPTS** (owner, 2026-11-27: "It must follow what I say … always go back as far as you can"). Premium and tax split evenly over 4 quarters, the first also carrying every card and the fee: instalment 1 (22 Nov 2026 to 21 Feb 2027) billed 239,763.75, the code gives 239,763.47. An opening bill is recorded with its first covered day, which picks the instalment; the form and the server refuse one without it.
