- 🔴 **A HELD RE-READ THE READER NEVER ANSWERED SPENDS NONE OF THE PLACE'S TWO READS** (2026-10-18: the AI account ran out of credit mid-run and 241 calls came back `call_failed`). `SIGNBOARD_HELD_MAX_READS` is 2 — the door's read and ONE re-read — since the owner stopped the four the same day: one held photo cost typically 10 and at most 13 answered calls on a photograph that never changes. `heldSignboardReadHappened` treats `call_failed` and `no_api_key` as no read: nothing is written, nothing spent, and nobody is paged about a photograph nobody looked at. Three unread with none answered stop the hourly run (`heldSignboardReaderIsDown`). A photo that cannot be cropped is `no_bytes`, a read that answered, or it waits for ever. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-held-signboard.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "A reader that never answered read nothing".
- 🔴 **ONE READ PER PHOTO: AN UNCHANGED PHOTO IS NEVER PAID FOR TWICE, AND ONLY A NEW PHOTO FIXES ONE NOBODY CAN READ** (owner, 2026-10-18: "a total wasting of money … fix all the unreadable"). The strong reader's `unreadable` is FINAL at once (`strongRead` → `unreadable_final` in `signboardNextAttempt`); only a fallback's answer keeps a try, and a lost call is retried without a cap (a cap would strand places after an outage). The 776 places filed "Not readable", lost or never read were read ONCE each (`rereadUnreadablePlacesOnce`, `scripts/phoenix-crm-reread-signboards.mjs --unreadable`, dry run unless `--apply`) and each ended confirmed (a held one approved by the system), filed with another name, or **sent back to its rep** — `signboard_check.retake` with the reason, written `unreadable_final` so no deployed pass reads it again. 🔴 Sent back (and refused at the door) ONLY when the PHOTO is the fault (`signboardAsksForAPhoto`): a sign the reader calls legible that did not confirm, a decorative plate, or an AM building's sign is kept final for the Super Admin. 🔴 **A sideways scene is turned upright and read once more off the door** (the reader's `upright` word); the door asks the rep to hold the phone upright instead. 🔴 **A board too full for one answer (`max_tokens`) is read in four overlapping quarters** (`signboardQuarters`, `mergeSignboardParts`) within the time the first read left, never a longer budget, and a timeout is never split. 🔴 **The owner's spelling rulings (18 Oct): ؤ is و and ئ is ي, ذ/ز/د are one letter and ح/خ one — and long vowels are NEVER forgiven** (it merges محمود/محمد, stranger matches 52 → 220); the three-word allowance stays as it is. Applied word against word in `nameWordIsThere` (`spelledOneWay`), never in `normalizeContactName`. 🔴 **A name on paper on a PM capture (the reader's `no_plate`) is never a match — it is sent back for a nameplate**; AM keeps "a hospital signs itself". A ruling reaches the places already read through their STORED readings, never a new read: `--rejudge` (`rejudgeStoredSignboardsOnce`, `signboardRejudge`) writes only a confirm or a paper send-back and LISTS the board cases it supersedes, never settling one. The rep sees "New signboard photo needed" and retakes it at the place (500 m + the photo's radius up to 250 m) through the same door scanner (`retakePlaceSignboard`, `phoenix_crm_retake_place_signboard`). 🔴 **A signboard photo is stored at up to 1920 px on its long side, not 1280** (`place-photo-size.ts`, only when `frameGuide` is set; the outstation helper is never changed). Pinned by `checkDoorScanner`, `checkOneReadPerPhoto`, `checkOwnersSpellingRulings` and `checkStoredReadingsRejudged`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "Every Not readable place".
- 🔴 **THE SIGNBOARD READ CROPS THE EVIDENCE STAMP OFF FIRST, FROM `drawStamp`'s OWN ARITHMETIC** (`stampBandFraction`), or the model reads the stamp's customer name and calls it a match. The timetable read (`server/schedule-check.ts`) crops the same band for the same reason. `brick_lookup` records whether a place found a brick, found none nearby, or could not look, and `resolvePlaceLocation` lives in `server/place-location.ts` so a validation, a move and a venue share ONE resolver without `list.ts` and `shared-places.ts` importing each other. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-brick-lookup.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE NIGHTLY SIGNBOARD PASS READS 200 A NIGHT AND IS ENDED BY ITS BUDGET, NOT BY ITS LIMIT** (raised 60 -> 200 on 2026-10-09, at the owner's instruction). The first night read its 60 between **03:35:04 and 03:35:45 Cairo — 42 seconds**, about 1.4 places a second with four in flight, and stopped on the LIMIT with 547 waiting and its 120-second budget barely touched. 🔴 **The limit now deliberately exceeds what the budget can reach (200 ≈ 140 s), and that mismatch is the design, never something to tidy** — a normal night stops on `SIGNBOARD_SWEEP_BUDGET_MS` with `moreWaiting` true, oldest first, the rest tomorrow, and a night where every read burns its full 8-second timeout still fits only ~60. Raising the BUDGET spends the behaviour pass's room inside the same 300 s cron; raising the limit does not. Concurrency stays 4. Pinned by `checkSweepRate()` in `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`.
- 🔒 **THE SIGNBOARD READER RETURNS EVERY SIGN IN THE FRAME, AND ONLY A SIGN READ `clear` MAY HOLD A PLACE** (2026-10-09). Measured on production: of the five places the first night flagged, **four were wrong** — a clothes shop read clearly beside a faded practice board, a room sign under an unreadable nameplate, and two boards the reader's own note called illegible. Rules in `signboard-rules.ts`: **any** sign naming him is a match; a frame where something defeated the reader while nothing it read answers the question (a PERSON for PM, a PLACE for AM) is `unjudged`; at a PM door the nameplate is the SMALLEST plate, never the biggest board. 🔴 **A cross-script compare may find a MATCH and may never file a FLAG on a name it cannot pin** — a word with a second ال inside it (عبدالعال) is `not_comparable`, not a mismatch. The NIGHT reads with the strong reader and takes a second look at the chosen sign cropped out of the frame, which decides in either direction; the DOOR reads once in the rep's 8 s; a HELD place is re-read strong, then guided, counted only where a blind close look READ the name. 🔴 **CLAUDE READS FIRST SINCE 2026-10-18 — Sonnet at the door, at night and on the re-read, Haiku for a lost call; OpenAI only when Claude's account is dead** (`preferProvider`, `SignboardReader`): on one rep's 21 photos stored 19 "Not readable", Claude confirmed 15 where the stored OpenAI readings confirmed 1. Never move a reader back without replaying the photos (account: "Claude reads the signboard"). Pinned by `checkManySigns` and `checkTwoReaders` in `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`, which replay all five production rows. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "The four false holds".
- 🔴 **A FACILITY NAME'S QUORUM IS COUNTED ON THE WORDS THAT SAY *WHICH* ONE, NEVER ON مستشفى OR عام** — `signNamesCustomer`'s place axis folds through `distinctiveWords`, as the Google listing comparison always has. It cost a FALSE MATCH on 2026-10-09: مستشفى المنشاوى العام matched a board reading مستشفى طنطا العام, because a three-token name may lose one and the two it kept were the generic pair — and the night's ONE correct flag had only ever been correct because that board was in English and the fold could not compare scripts. 🔴 **And the second, cropped look crops the sign that DEFEATED the reader (`chooseSignToReadAgain`), never the one already read clearly (`chooseSignboard`), and is judged WITH the rest of the frame** — judging the crop alone turns a correctly `unjudged` photograph into a flag, because every frame-level rule has nothing left to see. Pinned by `checkManySigns` cases 12 and 13. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "What the same five photographs say".
- 🔴 **AN AM SIGN IS COMPARED WITH THE LIST'S SHORT FORMS WRITTEN ONE WAY — صدرية IS صدر, الكهرباء IS كهربا, المقاولين IS المقاولون, النفسه IS النفسية, AND تعاقد IS DROPPED** (owner, 2026-10-19: "teach the matcher"). `placeWordsOnSign` in `signboard-rules.ts`, on the sign axis only (never the Google listing check). 🔴 **A contract row keeps NO one-word allowance**, or the town alone matches another building. Reaches stored readings through `--rejudge`, never a new read. Every new pair is replayed against all stored AM readings first. Pinned by `checkPlaceShortForms`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "Not readable, New photo asked, and the AM short forms".
- 🔴 **A PLACE WHOSE REP WAS ASKED FOR A NEW PHOTO IS WAITED FOR, NEVER RECOMMENDED FOR REJECTION** (owner, 2026-10-19: "only what needs you"). `adviseOnSign` answers "look" on `signboard.retake`, and the review files it under New photo asked, out of Not readable (`placeAwaitsNewPhoto`). Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-place-check-advice.test.ts` and `tests/place-review-deck.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A PERSON'S NAME COUNTS EACH COMPOUND AS ONE WORD, AND DROPS الأستاذ** (owner, 2026-10-12: "fix the signboard reader's name matching for titles and spaces"). ابراهيم عبدالشافى was filed as another person off a clear plate reading عبد الشافى, and عبد/ابو counting as words matched ابو الدهب on an ابو الوفا plate. `personNameWords` (`signboard-rules.ts`) joins a head (عبد, ابو) with the next word and a tail (الدين, الله) with the one before, on both sides. 🔴 **A compound's core still answers when it stands ALONE on the other side** — a reader that drops عبد has read the name; joining strictly filed a false flag. 🔴 **A name's ال is optional on any of its words, the given name too, and nothing more is forgiven** (2026-10-18): البدوى answers for بدوي, حسن for الحسن; a misread letter is another doctor. PERSON axis only; never move it into `normalizeContactName`, which keys `phoenix_crm_fold_name`. Pinned by `checkNameSpelling`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "One name, two spellings".
- 🔴 **ON A PERSON'S NAME THE ONE-WORD ALLOWANCE IS NEVER HIS GIVEN NAME LOST BEHIND ANOTHER MAN'S** (2026-10-18: of the night's 341 confirmations, judged again against names that are not the customer's, 106 also matched one). A person name read off the sign must OPEN on one of his words (the practice's kind and a lone letter skipped), and the sign's whole text must carry his words in his order as one run opening on his given name, at most two words between two of his. So his father's and family name behind another given name, his given name as another man's father, or his words gathered from two names on one notice, is not his. A given name the plate's damage took still matches; requiring it outright lost three such plates. `readsTheCustomersName` (`signboard-rules.ts`), pinned by `checkNameSpelling` part 7. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "His own name first".
- 🔴 **`recordPlaceSignboard` SWALLOWS ITS ERROR BY DESIGN, SO A CALLER MUST READ THE ROW BACK — AND WHAT IT SENDS IS `jsonbSafe(check)`.** On 2026-10-09 the re-read pass printed "written to the place row" for all five places and اسلام عبدالعال still held its old verdict: `jsonb` had refused the payload over one character the reader transcribed, and the silence that protects the rep at the door hid it. The recorder answers `boolean` now (the rep's path still ignores it), `jsonbSafe` in `signboard-rules.ts` drops the NUL and any unpaired surrogate, and `scripts/phoenix-crm-reread-signboards.mjs` re-reads the row and prints `NOT WRITTEN` with a non-zero exit when the stored verdict is not the one it meant to store. Pinned by `checkManySigns` case 11b and the server-source case 14.
- 🔴 **A WRONG READING IS NOT ALWAYS ON THE BOARD, SO `--place` RE-READS A PLACE WHETHER IT IS FLAGGED OR NOT.** `scripts/phoenix-crm-reread-signboards.mjs` was scoped to flag rows, and the board is not the set of places a wrong reader has damaged: a `match` raises no flag at all and leaves the nightly backlog (`SIGNBOARD_REREAD_REASONS`), and `other_name` raises one only where the detector met the place in a visit. Measured 2026-10-12 on the six places #5756's compound-name fix moved — ONE was on the board, so five were unreachable by the only tool that can correct them, among them a false `match` and an unflagged `other_name`. A run with no `--place` is still board-driven. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-signboard-check.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE SAME PHOTOGRAPH DOES NOT READ THE SAME TWICE — ONE RE-READ IS EVIDENCE, NEVER A VERDICT ON A FLAG.** Measured on the five held places, 2026-10-09: مستشفى المنشاوى العام read `other_name` on the board's Arabic text at 15:12 and `unjudged / unreadable` (`"TANTA GENE"`) at 15:34, and اسلام عبدالعال went `unjudged` at 15:35 and `other_name` at 15:48 once the nameplate `د/ اسلام حشيش` came back `clear`. Legibility is what moves, and legibility is what decides whether a sign may hold a place — so a flag the pass calls SUPERSEDED on one reading can be correct on the next. Never clear a flag on a single re-read, and never report a re-read's verdict as the answer about the place.
- 🔴 **`unreadable` IS NOT A VERDICT, IT IS AN ATTEMPT — THE NIGHT'S YIELD WAS 4 ANSWERS OUT OF 60 READS BEFORE THIS.** Measured 2026-10-09: 42 of 60 ended `unreadable`, which was TERMINAL, and 14 lost their model call and waited a day. Legibility is not a property of the photograph — the same frame read `other_name` at 18:12 and `TANTA GENE` at 18:34, and another read blank at 18:35 and a clear nameplate at 18:48. So a lost call is retried inside the night, an unreadable frame is read again up to `SIGNBOARD_MAX_READ_ATTEMPTS` (3) counted by `attempts` ON THE ROW, and the third one becomes `unreadable_final`, which no backlog query takes. 🔴 **Since 2026-10-18 that is a FALLBACK reader's allowance only — the strong reader's `unreadable` is final on its first answer** (the rule above). 🔴 **The stopping condition is a value in the row, never a filter over a JSON number** — a wrong filter reads as an empty backlog with nothing going red. The structural cases are `name_unusable` (a pharmacy, a name that folds away), never `unreadable`, or they return every night for ever.
- 🔴 **NOTHING REACHES THE INTEGRITY BOARD ON ONE READING — A FLAGGABLE VERDICT IS READ TWICE AND FILED ONLY IF THE TWO AGREE.** The flag is this pass's only human-visible output and it carries a person's name; 2026-10-09 produced a false match AND two flips on re-reading the same frame. On disagreement the place is `unjudged` / `unconfirmed`, KEEPING the first reading's text and signs, and is read again later — nothing flagged, nothing discarded. A `match` or an `unjudged` costs no second call, because nobody is named by either. 🔴 **One place can therefore cost TWO full reads, never three** (a lost call is `unjudged`; only `other_name`/`no_name` is confirmed), and a place is finished once started — so `SIGNBOARD_SWEEP_BUDGET_MS` is **100 s**, holding `budget + 2 × 25 s` under the 150 s half of the cron. The pace gate multiplies by 2; at 1 it measures 25 s short and passes.
- 🔴 **`unjudged` IS FOUR ANSWERS NOW, AND ONE SENTENCE IS WRONG FOR THREE OF THEM.** `unreadable` (coming back), `unreadable_final` (every attempt spent), `unconfirmed` (two readings disagreed) and `name_unusable` (nothing to compare) each get their own words on BOTH boards — `validated-place-list.tsx` and `shared-place-list.tsx` — through `SIGNBOARD_UNJUDGED_TEXT`, with the verdict's own sentence as the fallback so a reason added later can never render as a code. `ValidatedPlaceRow.signboard` carries `reason` for exactly this. Pinned by `checkManySigns` case 16.
- 🔴 **THE PHOTO'S OWN BRICK ANSWERS FOR THE TOWN WORD OF AN AM NAME, NEVER FOR THE COMPANY, AND THE SIGN MUST STILL SAY WHICH PLACE** (owner, 2026-10-19: "brick counts"). `signNamesCustomer` takes `photoBrick` (the row's `brick_name`) on the place axis; a brick word spends the whole allowance, a match on brick words alone never counts, the sign's own word is never صحة or جامعة, and a board calling itself a clinic never matches a listed hospital (the replay before the first apply confirmed a health poster from Asyum, a clinic in Helwan and any university in 6th of October City). Passed by the door, the held re-read, the sweep and `--rejudge`, never `spotCheck`. Pinned by `checkPlaceShortForms`. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-validation-feedback.md`, "The 31 left in Not readable".
