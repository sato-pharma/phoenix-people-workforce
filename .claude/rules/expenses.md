## Rules

### Production hooks (moved from AGENTS.md §2.3)

- **`expense_claims` is the ONE live RLS read path in production.** Put a precondition in every shared-queue production `WHERE`; **"Log out everywhere" is THREE writes.**
- **The `expense_claims` mirror builds `claimed_total` and `approved_total` from TWO tables**, so a stale request header breaches `expense_claims_approved_total_range`. **Read the expense components before touching an approved amount**; the rebuild flags `data_quality_hold`.
- 🔴 **AN APPROVE OR REJECT IS REFUSED (409) UNLESS IT CARRIES THE `updated_at` THE REVIEWER SAW** (risk review of the chained-journey PR, 2026-10-18; built 2026-10-19). A claim keeps moving in the queue — extra tickets, a journey stop — and approving paid the row's CURRENT figures. `expectedRevision` is REQUIRED on `approveOutstationRequestForPayroll` and `rejectOutstationRequestAsAdmin` (never default it to "unchecked"); the approval's write is pinned to the stamp it checked, the queue Save skips a row without `requestRevision`, a comment-only edit is exempt. His comment is APPENDED to the system's `Journey check:` sentence, never written over it. Every writer that moves money must stamp `updated_at`. `claim-revision.ts`, `decision-note.ts`, `tests/outstation-stale-approval.test.ts`.

### Owner rules (moved from AGENTS.md §4)

- **The system FLAGS an expense; it NEVER auto-rejects one.** Never auto-publish a deductions month or approve a batch yourself; a same-day CRM check reads zero — never report it as measured.
- 🔒 **A DUPLICATE EXPENSE CLAIM IS HELD, NEVER REFUSED** (built 2026-09-26 on the owner's instruction, after 351.00 was reimbursed twice for one May trip). All FOUR of person, day, destination and amount must match inside ONE payout run; the extra copies leave the PROPOSED figure only — they keep their full amount, stay in the trip breakdown naming the copy that is paid, and are subtotalled as "On Hold — Duplicate", so approving one or typing the amount still pays it. 🔴 **Never widen it to same-day-only** — all ten same-day trip pairs on production go to different destinations — **and never turn it into a unique constraint**, which refuses a real claim with no override and no words. `src/lib/automated-payroll/duplicate-expense-hold.ts`, pinned by `tests/duplicate-expense-hold.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **An NSM's outstation expense is NEVER held** — NSM only. `nsm-outstation-expense-is-never-held`
- 🔴 **An NSM's same-day-return outstation allowance is 300** — when `allowance = 300` exceeds `total`, raise the total; never trim the allowance.
- 🔒 **A CAR TRIP WITH ADDED STOPS IS PAID DRIVE BY DRIVE, ONE WAY, ONLY GOVERNORATE TO GOVERNORATE OVER 40 KM** (owner, 2026-10-18 — "Minya → Fayoum × 1.8, Fayoum → Beni Suef × 1.8, Beni Suef → Minya × 1.8"). A stop in the same governorate (Assiut → Dairut) or 40 km or less changes nothing: the trip stays on today's home-to-destination-and-back price. A stop is a live photo with GPS from the employee's own undecided car trip, at most 6, every km is Google's road distance between photo positions, and every suspicious shape (impossible speed, ping-pong, a stop at home, another trip that day, weak GPS, a chain over twice the old price) is FLAGGED onto the approval reason — never refused. A new car trip on a day a journey already reached through a stop is held. `src/lib/outstation/chained-journey.ts`, pinned by `tests/outstation-chained-journey.test.ts`. `outstation-chained-governorate-journey-fuel-rule`
