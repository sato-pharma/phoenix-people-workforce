## Rules

- 🔒 **THE CEO COMPOSER IS THE SATO COMPOSER COMPONENT WITH A `ComposerVariant`, NOT A FORK — never copy `sato-email-composer.tsx`.** Owner 2026-10-06 asked for one "exactly as sato email composer copy paste", and the only way a second screen stays exactly like the first is for it to BE the first: a literal copy drifts on the first fix applied to one of them, and the one left behind is the one that mails the company over the chief executive's signature. Everything that differs — `apiBase`, the assist style, the mic, the closing-line labels — lives in `CEO_COMPOSER_VARIANT` and nowhere else. Account: `docs/ceo-email-composer.md`.
- 🔴 **A FILE DROPPED OR PASTED INTO THE COMPOSER'S BODY IS ATTACHED, AND AN EMAIL THAT SAYS "ATTACHED" WITH NOTHING ON IT IS CONFIRMED AS "SEND IT WITHOUT AN ATTACHMENT"** (owner, 2026-10-24: "fix the composer so an attached PDF uploads before Send"). That evening a welcome email said "Your New Starter Handbook is attached" in five inboxes with nothing on it — the PDF went into the body, which kept it from opening in the tab and then dropped it. `EmailBodyEditor`'s `attachFiles` prop takes the non-pictures (`nonPicturesFromTransfer`) on BOTH drop and paste; `promisesAnAttachment` (`src/lib/sato-email/attachment-promise.ts`) is the second net. Never widen it to the bare verb "attach" — "please attach your CV" promises nothing. Pinned by `tests/sato-email-attachments.test.ts` §9.
- 🔴 **THE CEO'S SIGNATURE IS RESOLVED SERVER-SIDE FROM THE EMPLOYEE DIRECTORY ON EVERY RENDER AND IS NEVER POSTED BY THE BROWSER** — `resolveCeoSignature()` in `src/lib/ceo-email/identity.ts`. The Super Admin seat can open that screen too, so a name arriving on the wire would be a name that seat could set, on a letter the whole company reads as the chief executive's. The composer's own field is the CLOSING LINE ("Sincerely"), which is why the column is `closing_line` — never widen it into a signatory, and never take a name off a draft. 🔴 **Three things here are HIS ANSWERS, asked and answered 2026-10-06, not defaults left in place:** TWO seats (the CEO and his own Super Admin workspace, the CEO alone offered and declined); the block is TYPED NAME AND TITLE ONLY (a scanned signature offered and declined, because the image would travel inside every letter); and the identity is From `ceo@send.satopharma.com` with replies and the visible To on `anwar.gaweesh@satopharma.com`, so a copy of every letter reaches his own inbox. Changing any of the three is reversing a decision, never tidying a loose end.
- 🔴 **THE CEO ASSISTANT HAS NO MODE BUTTONS, AND THE STRICT/REPORTING GUARD IS CHOSEN ON THE SERVER, NEVER SENT** — `/api/admin/ceo-email/assist` takes no `mode`. `effectiveCeoGuard()` reads the intent the model declares, then falls back to a structural floor: `figuresLostFromBody()` compares the numeric tokens he wrote against the ones that survive, and any loss forces `strict` whatever the model claimed. A `mode` on the wire would hand the browser the choice of how carefully his own figures are checked.
- 🔴 **THE CEO MIC NAMES ITS LANGUAGE AND NEVER ASKS THE SPEECH SERVICE TO DETECT ONE — omitting the `language` field makes Whisper TRANSLATE the speech into fluent English**, measured in this repo on Egyptian Arabic and the reason the CRM hardcodes `ar`. A letter he dictated in Arabic arriving as an English instruction is the worst outcome available, because nothing on screen looks wrong. So `CEO_DICTATION_LANGUAGES` is a one-tap control beside the button, opening on Arabic, and `form.set("language", …)` in `groq-transcription.ts` is UNCONDITIONAL — `ar` is now its default, not its only value, and no caller can ask for none. The auto-detect design was built first and withdrawn on that measurement; reinstating it needs a real clip measured with the production key, which a laptop with no speech key cannot do. The TYPED box is unaffected — the assistant answers in whatever language he writes in.
- 🔴 **`prepareSatoEmailBody` ENDS WITH A SANITIZER, BECAUSE EVERY HOUSE REWRITE BEFORE IT IS A REGEX OVER A STRING** (technical audit 23 Oct 2026, P7-02). `stripFills` deleting ` bgcolor='…'` from inside a `title` up to a quote in the following text turned that text back into live attributes (`onmouseover` reproduced), and the same string is the composer's `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` preview AND the sent mail. `sanitizeFinishedBody` only REMOVES: the first pass's tags, its attributes plus `class`/`role`/table spacing, styles verbatim (`parseStyleAttributes: false`) and entities as written (`decodeEntities: false`, so the spacer `&nbsp;` survives). A new rewrite goes BEFORE it, never after. Pinned by `tests/app-layer-audit.test.ts`.
