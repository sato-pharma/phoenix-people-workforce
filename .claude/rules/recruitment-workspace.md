## Rules

### The rebuilt workspace (owner, 19–20 Oct 2026 — `/recruitment`)

- 🔒 **A CANDIDATE'S TAB COMES FROM THEIR OWN REPLY, NEVER FROM THE CV** (owner, 2026-10-20: *"we do not look at the cv we folder based on whatsapp replies"*). Nine role tabs (Medical Rep · District Manager · Product Manager · RM/AM + NSM · HR Section Head · Talent Acquisition · Office Coordinator · Personnel, added 2026-10-31 · Other) plus Needs placing and Folders; Medical Rep splits by region then AU area NAME (+ Unspecified), District Manager by region. The filing is STORED in `ats_candidate_placements` — written by the bot's role/region/area answer, the careers form's role, or HR — and no WHOLE row = Needs placing (a Medical Rep row needs its area, a District Manager row its region — owner, 2026-11-15; see "PLACED MEANS POSITION, REGION AND AREA"). An AI reading of the CV never decides a tab and never fills an answer chip; a reply that maps to nothing waits in Needs placing, never guessed. Map: `roleTabForFamily` (`workspace/role-tabs.ts`), pinned by `tests/recruitment-role-tabs.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **EVERY ROW AND EVERY COUNT ON THE WORKSPACE IS READ THROUGH ONE SQL FILTER — `ats_workspace_filter(p)`** (the old pool's 72-vs-86 and 38-vs-4 disagreements, 19 Oct 2026, came from counting two ways). `ats_workspace_page` and `ats_workspace_counts` both call it; never compute a tab, place, status or funnel count in TypeScript, and never add a filter to one without the other. The server builds `p` with `workspaceDbParams` and sets `allowed_tabs`/`hide_hr_people` from the SESSION, never the request.
- 🔒 **THE TALENT ACQUISITION SEAT NEVER SEES AN HR APPLICANT — FENCED BY PERSON, AT EVERY BOUNDARY THAT RETURNS A CANDIDATE** (owner, 2026-10-20: every tab but HR Section Head, Talent Acquisition, Personnel). Hidden = an HR placement, or `declared_role_family` ∈ `HR_ROLE_FAMILIES` (so a careers-form Talent Acquisition application or a legacy `human_resources` tap is hidden even in Needs placing), answered as not-found. The fence sits at the route/page boundary (`workspace/fence.ts`), never inside `listCandidates`/`getCandidate`/`searchCandidates`, which intake and merge call too; `tests/recruitment-ta-fence.test.ts` walks every boundary file. The CEO READS the workspace through a named gate in `role-tabs.ts` and every write refuses him — never grant him `candidates:read`; his opened CV shows the answers first and the CV file one press away through the narrow read-only `/api/recruitment/workspace/cv/<id>` (owner, 2026-10-20: *"answers and optional the cv file"*) — never notes, the chat or the timeline. Typing a role IS filing: the `whatsapp-answer` route refuses an HR role for her (`workspace/typed-role-filing.ts`) and every write there asks `assertWorkspaceWorker`.
- 🔴 **EVERY DECISION HAS A 10-SECOND UNDO — A STATUS APPLIES AT ONCE AND UNDO REVERTS IT; ANYTHING THAT TELLS SOMEONE OR REMOVES SOMETHING WAITS OUT THE 10 SECONDS** (owner changed 5 to 10, 2026-10-20). Send to manager, the rejection message, ask again and delete are sent only after the window, so Undo cancels them; reverting out of `rejected` clears `rejection_reason`. A rejection from the new workspace needs a reason from the fixed list (`workspace/vocabulary.ts`); the column stays NULLABLE because offer-outcome sync, the bulk reject and a manager's Skip reject without one.
- 🔒 **REJECTIONS LEAVE THE TABS AFTER 30 DAYS FOR THE ARCHIVE — NEVER DELETED, STILL SEARCHABLE, ONE CLICK BACK** (owner, 2026-10-20). Measured from `status_changed_at`; a manager's Skip still waiting in the Returned filter is not archived.
- 🔴 **RELEASE 1 KEEPS THE OLD POOL AT `/recruitment/previous` — THE SAME COMPONENT AT THE SAME PATH — UNTIL HE HAS WORKED IN THE NEW ONE; RELEASE 2 DELETES IT** (owner, 2026-10-20: "two releases"). The four tests that read the page by path point at `previous/page.tsx`, and `app/employee-portals/recruitment/page.tsx` stays on the old workspace until Release 2. Its Pipeline, Requisitions, Folders and Advertise tabs are retired from the new screen only — their back ends (the requisition autopilot, offers, job-post channels, the folder data) keep running.
- 🔒 **THE CANDIDATES ARE A BORDERED TABLE, NEVER THE OLD TWO-LINE ROWS** (owner, 2026-10-20: *"put the data in a polished premium table with borders, neat and tidy and comfortable to my eye"*). `workspace/candidate-table.tsx`: the tick, the candidate (name, status, source and received, every flag), his six in his words — Applying for · Graduated in · Years of experience · Expected monthly net salary · When could you start · Age — then Region · Area and every other answer; "Not asked" when that role is never asked, amber "Missing · add" edited in place. It switches on its OWN width (`@container`, `TABLE_WIDE_MIN_PX` = 1180): ten columns, else the six stacked in two columns; a phone gets the `CandidateRow` cards. One `<colgroup>` carries both layouts' widths. 🔴 **Its head stays PINNED as the rows scroll** (owner, same day): head cells sticky at `--ws-head-top`, in a `!overflow-visible` frame — `globals.css` makes every `:has(> table)` box a scroll box. Columns and their shares: `candidate-table-columns.ts`, pinned by `tests/recruitment-workspace-table.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **AN OPENED CV HALVES THE PAGE: PAGE 1 WHOLE WITH NO SCROLLING, THE ACTION BUTTONS ALWAYS IN VIEW** (owner, 2026-10-20: *"i want the full page to be halfed and the cv 1 page is fully appearing with no need to scroll … add the action buttons"*). `role-workspace.tsx` splits the whole page 1fr/1fr from 1100px of the page's width; the right half is a pane of fixed HEIGHT (the window less the app's bar — never a `max-height`), pinned and never scrolling as a whole: header, the action bar (`h-8`, a "Call result" drop-down), one tab row with the zoom, then the document at `fit="page"` (`cvFitZoom`, the tighter of across and down). 🔴 **The page is measured with `offsetWidth`/`offsetHeight`, never `getBoundingClientRect`** — the pane is read in its own pixels and the admin shell's 90% screen zoom reaches only getBoundingClientRect, so the mix fitted page 1 1/0.9 too big (live 2026-10-20: zoom 0.7086 where 0.6393 fits, its foot 66px under the pane). An action's panel opens as a drawer OVER the document. Opening a CV scrolls the page just far enough for the pane to pin. An old Word .doc has no page 1 and stays at fit width.
- 🔒 **THE DECIDED TOGGLE IS THE STATUS FILTER IN ONE PRESS — ALL · NOT DECIDED · DECIDED, ON THE FILTER BAR'S TOP LINE** (owner, 2026-10-20: *"add a toggle in the screen to filter by decided and all not decided"*). Not decided = New; Decided = every other status; All is the clean default. No new database filter: `statusesForDecisionMode` (`workspace/filters.ts`) sets `statuses`, the numbers come from `by_status` (which ignores the status filter, so they hold still), and the two halves must cover the whole status list — a new status lands in Decided by itself. Status, source and call result also HIDE (owner, 2026-10-20: *"exclude shortlisted, exclude rejected"*): each value keeps-only by its name or hides by its eye, never both (`pressKeep`/`pressHide`); `ats_workspace_filter` reads `exclude_*` (latest call only), and `by_status` ignores hides too, so the tiles hold still. Pinned by `tests/recruitment-workspace-filters.test.ts` and `tests/recruitment-workspace-exclude-filters.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **NEEDS PLACING IS FILED FROM HR'S OWN ANSWERS** (owner, 2026-10-20: pick Applying for from a list, then the Region, then only that region's Areas, and answer the WhatsApp questions by hand so the candidate is placed exactly from them). `workspace/placing-form.tsx`, the opened row's detail row on Needs placing and the "Place in a tab" drawer everywhere; ONE save, `PUT /api/recruitment/candidates/<id>/answers` → `placeWithAnswers` (`workspace/placing.ts`): every check before the first write (`placingRefusal`, the area inside the region), each changed answer saved "added by HR", the role saved as its LABEL (`roleFamilyForTypedRole` reads it back), then ONE `placeCandidate`. The next Needs placing CV opens after a save.
- 🔴 **LEAVING SAVES TOO** (owner, 2026-11-15, "Save on leaving" — 120 stayed 120 because choices made and then left by opening the next CV were thrown away unsent): a form he TOUCHED and did not press places the person when it closes (next CV, arrows, Close, another tab) through `placeOnLeave` → the same `placeWithAnswers` and Undo; a refusal or failed save is said on screen by name. The form loading its own answers is never a touch. Pinned in `tests/recruitment-workspace-table.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **PLACED MEANS POSITION, REGION AND AREA** (owner, 2026-11-15: *"if not placed in an area consider him still needs placing — placing is confirmed if moved to position region and area"*). A Medical Rep filing with no area, or a District Manager filing with no region, keeps the CV in Needs placing and out of the role tab and its counts; every other tab is whole with the role alone. The row is KEPT (role and region not lost) — only the counting changes: `placementIsComplete` (`workspace/role-tabs.ts`) and `public.ats_placement_is_complete` (migration `20261115130110`) say the same, used by `ats_workspace_filter`/`ats_workspace_counts`, the daily digest and the export's "Placed in" cell. A part-made save stays in the Needs placing list and says so. Never place a Medical Rep by region alone to clear the count. Pinned in `tests/recruitment-role-tabs.test.ts`, `tests/recruitment-workspace-table.test.ts`, `tests/recruitment-workspace-export.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **REGION AND AREA ARE CHOSEN FROM THE COMPANY'S LIST, NEVER TYPED, AND A SAVE THAT LEAVES THE CV IN NEEDS PLACING SAYS WHY** (Talent Acquisition, 24 Nov 2026: "she does Needs placing but the count does not decrease" — typed "kom hamada", "Cairo", "Behiera 1" filed nobody and answered "Saved"). The table and opened-CV cells get `placeAnswerOptions` (`workspace/answer-place-options.ts`): the eight regions by code, every active area by its canonical name; an area alone files a Medical Rep whole. The `whatsapp-answer` route returns `placing` from `placingGapAfterAnswer` and the toast carries its sentence. Pinned by `tests/recruitment-answer-place-options.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **OPENED CV: THREE TAKE-AWAYS** (owner, 2026-10-20) — Download CV, Download CV + application, Print CV + application, for a seat that works the workspace. `workspace/cv-take-away.tsx` reuses the screening page's candidate-keyed file and print page (`/api/recruitment/candidates/<id>/application`, `/print/candidate-application/<id>`) — never a second builder — and Print opens a new tab. Drawn only for `hr && canWork && actions`: the CEO reads without `candidates: read`, so both application endpoints would refuse him. Pinned by `tests/recruitment-cv-take-away.test.ts`.
