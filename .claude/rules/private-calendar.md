## Rules

### Production hooks (moved from AGENTS.md §2.3)

- 🔒 **AN APPROVED PLAN COVERS NO DAY IN THE NO-ACTIVITY ENGINE** (owner, 2026-10-12). `isCovered` in `src/lib/private-calendar/logic.ts` names no plan: a day whose only entry is an approved plan reads **No Activity Recorded**, plus an `info` note **"Planned, but nothing recorded"** naming what was planned — the comparison beside the finding, never instead of it. The flag is `plannedActivityIsRecordedNowhereElse` (renamed from `plannedActivityCoversTheDay`, which is how a display fact became a money decision) and it is DISPLAY only. Account: `docs/private-calendar-plan-is-never-coverage.md`. Pinned by `tests/private-calendar-plan-source.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **AN OUTSTATION TRIP COVERS EVERY DAY IT SPANS** (owner, 2026-10-29: "if their is an expense it must automatic appeared in his private calendar"). `src/lib/private-calendar/outstation-trip-items.ts` reads the SAME fold deductions uses (`loadOutstationTripDays`, any trip not rejected, cancelled or withdrawn — a submitted claim counts), in all three passes of `service.ts`; never a second state rule. Pinned by `tests/private-calendar-outstation-trips.test.ts`.

### Private calendar sources

- 🔒 **EVERY MANAGER SEAT SEES HIS WHOLE TEAM BENEATH HIM ON THE PRIVATE CALENDAR, NOT ONLY HIS DIRECT REPORTS** (owner, 2026-10-27: *"apply that same whole-team rule"* — a Marketing Manager could pick his Group Product Manager but not the Product Manager under her). `canReadPrivateCalendarEmployee` (`logic.ts`) walks the subtree for the `hierarchy`, `line` and `team` scopes — the same seats as the fingerprint attendance's `isHierarchyAttendanceViewer` — so whoever can open someone's attendance can open his calendar. Downward only; a self-scoped seat never walks. Never narrow it back to one level. Pinned by `tests/private-calendar.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A NOTICE PERIOD IS STILL WORK — THE PRIVATE CALENDAR DROPS A LEAVER THE DAY AFTER HIS RESIGNATION DATE, NEVER AT SUBMISSION** (owner, 2026-10-23: *"drop after his resignation date not submission date"*). `employment_status` flips to `resigned` and `organogram_visible` to false the moment a resignation is SUBMITTED, so every surface built on `isActiveDirectoryEmployee` loses someone still at work. `privateCalendarEmployees` (`service.ts`) reads `{ includeInactive: true }` and keeps anyone `isWithinNoticePeriod` covers — the day asked about on or before the LATER of `last_working_date` and `resignation_date`; a departed row with NEITHER date is never kept, and `isWithinEmploymentWindow` still decides each day. 🔴 **`getCompanyNoActivityDays` STAYS ACTIVE-ONLY** — widening it would raise deduction candidates against people on notice, which is his call in Deductions. The Field Absence rule below (`includeResignedAgents`) is the same ruling. Account: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The Private Calendar keeps a leaver until his exit day".
- 🔴 **AN EMPLOYEE THE DAY-EVIDENCE DOOR NEVER READ IS NOT A DAY WITH NOTHING ON IT.** `getPrivateCalendarDayEvidence` writes NO entry for an employee outside the set its loaders were given; every caller already reads a missing entry as "could not be read". Writing an empty one instead reported 22 logged Phoenix CRM visits as "lost at the calendar itself" on 2026-10-23 — the audit's VISIT-S4-MISSING, all three occurrences the only two resigned reps in a scan whose other 80 rep-days read correctly. Both rules pinned by `tests/private-calendar-notice-period.test.ts`.

- 🔒 **EVERY PHOENIX CRM VISIT IS SHOWN IN FULL ON THE PRIVATE CALENDAR, HUNG ON THE ONE ENTRY THAT ALREADY COUNTS IT — IT NEVER ADDS OR REMOVES AN ENTRY** (owner, 2026-10-21: *"exactly as mina farouk"*). `attachVisitDetails` (`src/lib/private-calendar/visit-detail-items.ts`) runs on the workspace and on `getPrivateCalendarDayEvidence`, never on the company-wide deduction pass, so the no-activity engine reads exactly what the loaders built. A rep's visit goes to the "logged before the team was started" entry first (a door entry beside it is his iReports360 work and keeps its own customers), then his door or received entry of the same kind; a visit a manager or product manager rode goes to his performed entry, then his own single-visit entry. A visit with no entry to sit on is left off — coverage is the loaders' call. Best-effort: a failed read leaves every entry as it was. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-manager-visit-details.test.ts` (`everyonesVisitsInFull`).

- 🔒 **THE MONTH CELL IS A SUMMARY TILE — COUNTS AND TAGS, NEVER A CUSTOMER OR A PHARMACY NAME; EVERY VISIT IS READ IN FULL IN THE DAILY TIMELINE TABLE UNDER THE GRID** (owner, 2026-10-23: *"in the calendar tile itself show the count of the AM and the count of PM and the count of pharmacies, and if double or single … the area … don't mention names … when I press on the day, the details appear down"*). A visit entry draws `VisitDayTile` (`src/components/employee-portal/private-calendar.tsx`), built by the pure `privateCalendarDayTile` (`src/lib/private-calendar/day-tile.ts`): Single / Double / Chain, Regular / Campaign, Planned / Unplanned (ONLY where a Phoenix visit says it — an iReports360 day claims nothing), AM · PM · Pharmacies apart with the pharmacy in the Phoenix CRM calendar's violet, who it was made with, the brick and the area. 🔴 **Every field is STRUCTURED — `tileWith`, `tileAreas`, `employeeAreaName`, `managerVisits` — and the tile never parses `chipLines`, which stay text for the surfaces that print lines.** A visit entry with no tile prints its first two chip lines (kind, counts) and no customer. `ManagerVisitChipRows` is gone; the table (`ManagerPlanActual`) and the cards are where a visit is listed. The page is flat and square-edged: no `rounded-*`, no card shadows. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.md`, "The address is the DETAILS'". Pinned by `tests/private-calendar-day-tile.test.ts`.

- 🔒 **A REOPENED WEEKLY-REVIEW DAY IS RE-DECIDED THROUGH THE LINE, AND EVERY READER OF A SETTLED DAY MUST GO THROUGH `effectiveSettlement`** (built 2026-10-09 for the Dispute Center). A settlement row is written once and never changed, so a second decision moves the DAY's own action and method and reverses the leave charge in one transaction (`phx_attendance_revise_digest_dispute`); for a dispute outcome the LINE is then the truth and the settlement is its first draft. 🔴 **The hole this closes: a day upheld, then reopened and REJECTED, still read as upheld by month-end payroll** — so the bridge (`digest-bridge.ts`) and the Deductions "was this day already cleared?" guard both select the line beside the settlement and judge it through `effectiveSettlement` (`src/lib/attendance/digest-rules.ts`). A rejected day with no method on the line falls back to SALARY, never to a null a reader treats as nothing. 🔴 **A revision is refused unless the case was really reopened** (`reopenedSince`), and a day whose month is already PAID raises a notice naming Automated Payroll rather than inventing a cross-month charge. Account: `docs/dispute-center.md`. Pinned by `tests/dispute-center-digest-revision.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **A CALENDAR DISPUTE AND A WEEKLY-REVIEW DISPUTE BOTH STAY VISIBLE AFTER THEY ARE DECIDED, AND EITHER SIDE MAY ATTACH PICTURES** (owner, 2026-10-09). The employee reads and answers them at `/employee-portals/disputes`; the reviewer at `/admin/disputes`. **Only the reviewer reopens** — a decided case shows the employee an "ask for another look" box, never a reopen. Pictures (4 max, 5 MB each) go through the shared `checkDisputeImage` rule into the private `dispute-attachments` bucket and are served only by `/api/dispute-center/attachments/:id` after the participant check — never a storage link, and the key never carries the uploaded file name. A named weekly-review reviewer never rules on their own week (the Super Admin is exempt), and `attendance:read` alone opens the page but no colleague's case. Account: `docs/dispute-center.md`.
- 🔴 **AN ACCEPTED WHOLE-DAY REVIEW REQUEST IS AN ENTRY ON ITS DAY, AND THAT DAY STOPS READING "NO ACTIVITY RECORDED"** (2026-10-29, after a Medical Representative's 11 October "Training" request was accepted three times and her calendar never changed). A request filed against an empty day has no item to annotate, so `applyAcceptedDisputeOverlays` alone added nothing and the red block stood. Every accepted request with no `item_source` for one person on one date becomes ONE `correction` entry (lime, "Admin-verified correction", each claim with where it was verified) through `buildAcceptedCorrectionItems`, and `withoutCorrectedDayFindings` drops that day's `no_activity_recorded` and `planned_but_nothing_recorded` — every other finding stands. 🔴 **Display only:** `getCompanyNoActivityDays` (the Deductions No Records Activity read) does not read review requests and is untouched. `src/lib/private-calendar/logic.ts`, pinned by `tests/private-calendar.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A REVIEW REQUEST IS READ BY ITS HR ID WHATEVER THE CASE — `.ilike` WITH `privateCalendarHrIdPattern`, NEVER `.eq`** (2026-10-29). Requests are written with the signed-in login’s spelling (`pd-0026`) and the calendar asks with the employee master’s (`PD-0026`), so the exact match found NONE of the 31 requests on production, from 11 people: no pending badge, no verified status and no correction ever reached a calendar. Both reads in `src/lib/private-calendar/disputes.ts` (the calendar’s and the employee’s own list) match case-blind; pinned by `tests/private-calendar.test.ts`.


- 🔒 **THE APPROVED WEEKLY PLAN COVERS A PRIVATE-CALENDAR DAY ONLY WHERE PHOENIX RECORDS THAT ACTIVITY NOWHERE ELSE — Travel, Admin, Meeting, Meeting with Team, Training, Event, Other** (added 2026-10-07, for the 4 October review request where an `hr_acknowledged` Travel day read "No Activity Recorded"). A planned **Field** or **Double Visit** with no logged visit, a planned **Office** day with no punch, a planned **Vacation**/**Excuse**/**National Vacation** with no request each KEEP the exception: the CRM, the fingerprint and the leave workflow are the records, and the plan proves only that the day was EXPECTED. Measured 1 Aug – 7 Oct 2026, excusing every type would have erased 364 real gaps, 318 of them planned double visits. Approved = `manager_approved`/`hr_acknowledged`, weekly only. Read by BOTH engines — the calendar and `getCompanyNoActivityDays`, which feeds the Deductions No Records Activity connector — so the two can never disagree. `src/lib/private-calendar/plan-items.ts`, pinned by `tests/private-calendar-plan-source.test.ts`.

- 🔒 **A PLANNED OFFICE DAY WITH NO PUNCH STAYS FLAGGED — including for the people who have NO fingerprint device mapping at all and therefore cannot punch** (owner, 2026-10-08, asked directly with both options and the measured cost: 25 such days in 1 Aug – 7 Oct 2026, 18 of them belonging to non-enrolled employees). An Office day EXPECTS a punch — the plan's own office lookup says so in as many words (`src/lib/attendance/plan-office-days.ts`) — so the remedy for a non-enrolled employee is enrolment or an attendance exception, never letting the plan excuse the day. 🔴 **Never "fix" this back by adding `Office` to `PLAN_ACTIVITY_TYPES_RECORDED_NOWHERE_ELSE`**, which is the one-word change that looks like a kindness and quietly removes 25 days from the Deductions No Records Activity connector.
- 🔴 **THE APPROVED WEEKLY PLAN DECIDES WHETHER A DAY IS OFFICE OR FIELD; THE MONTHLY PLAN ONLY FILLS DATES NO WEEKLY PLAN COVERS, AND IS SHOWN BESIDE IT WHEN THEY DISAGREE** (owner, 2026-10-19: show both, attendance follows the weekly plan). `resolvePlanOfficeDay` in `src/lib/attendance/plan-office-resolution.ts` is the one resolver. `getPlanOfficeDayDetailMap` reads each header's `plan_kind` (anything not `monthly` counts as weekly) and reads a regional or national manager's Phoenix CRM halves as weekly. The Weekly Plan cell reads e.g. "Field (monthly plan: Office)", and the day's reason says which plan was followed. Pinned by `tests/attendance-reconcile-day.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **AN OFFICE PLAN FOR ONE HALF OF THE DAY IS HALF AN OFFICE DAY, JUDGED ON THAT HALF IN THE DAY-BY-DAY REPORT AND IN THE DEDUCTIONS** (owner, 2026-10-19: "deductions follow it too"). This applies on an `office_days_only` seat only. The fingerprint report passes `judgedHalfDayPart` to both of its judgments, so the half is judged with the half-day vacation math. An approved half-day vacation keeps its own half, and the cancel rule still reads the real vacation. On a day with an Office half, a Meeting or Meeting with Team half counts as office too, but a meeting alone never makes an office day. Pinned by `tests/attendance-reconcile-day.test.ts`.

- 🔴 **THE WEEKLY GAP NOTICE IS THE EMPLOYEE'S OWN, AND IT JUDGES NOTHING** (owner, 2026-10-08: "add an employee notice when his own calendar has an unexplained day" - his words; the notice's own wording is neutral, per the 2026-10-05 rule). Saturday 08:09 Cairo, the completed Sat-Thu week, **one notice per employee and never one per day** - in-app and push, **NEVER email** (bulk outbound email from a business system is banned, and every automated email mirrors to the owner's Telegram, so a weekly sweep over the roster would page him for news that is not his). 🔴 **The audience is the EMPLOYEE ALONE - never a manager, never HR, never an admin digest**: a recording gap is not an accusation, and nobody else is told before he has had the chance to answer. It calls `getCompanyNoActivityDays`, the SAME engine call the Deductions No Records Activity connector makes, so the day the notice names, the day the calendar shows and the day that can become a deduction candidate are one day by construction - never re-derive the gap here. A day already carrying a review request of ANY status is named to nobody (a rejected one was answered by a Super Admin in person), and that suppression read fails CLOSED: a notice is unretractable and a retry is free. Why it exists: the Request review control had been used ONCE since 22 July across 111 active employees, not because it was hidden or wrongly gated but because nothing ever told anyone to look. Account - the measurement, the three reasons the control sat unused, and every rule above: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The weekly unexplained-day notice is the employee's own". `src/lib/private-calendar/gap-notice-rules.ts` + `gap-notice.ts`, pinned by `tests/private-calendar-gap-notice.test.ts`.

### Tile and timeline wording

- 🔴 **A PRIVATE CALENDAR TILE NAMES EACH PERSON ONCE — PEOPLE ARE DEDUPED BY HR ID, NEVER BY THE NAME STRING** (NSM audit, 2026-10-26: "WITH Hana Abdelaziz Samir Abdelaziz, Hana Abdelaziz" — her full name from one source and her nickname from another). `withPeople` carries `hrId`; `distinctTilePeople` in `day-tile.ts` keeps one per id. Pinned by `tests/private-calendar-day-tile.test.ts`.
