## Rules

### Moved out of `phoenix-crm` on 2026-10-09 (the pointer split — see `scripts/module-memory/modules.mjs`)

- 🔴 **EVERY PHOENIX CRM REPORT CARRIES THE QUESTION IT ANSWERS AND A SUBJECT — the catalogue is `src/lib/phoenix-crm/report-guide.ts`, never the tab** (owner, 2026-10-03: *"reoprts not clear"*). Fifteen equal chips with a three-word note each told a reader who already knew the report which one it was and told everyone else nothing. A new report needs a family, a one-sentence `answers` and a short `note`, and must be `offer(...)`ed by the tab — the test refuses each omission, the last because a report nobody is offered cannot be opened. The admin panel prints `answers` for the chosen report; the portal keeps the tab's own one line. 🔴 **`SECTION_TITLES` IS THE ONE PLACE A REPORT IS NAMED, so a rename is ONE edit** — FOUR surfaces read it — the worksheet tab, the heading inside the sheet, `packSections` (the cover's promise) and the FILENAME of a single-report download (`reportFileSlug`) — and the tests too. The one exception is the visits sheet's heading, "per rep" because its rows are reps. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-admin-surface.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-reports-export.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **EVERY DAY THE EXCEPTIONS BOARD AND THE 06:55 DIGEST COUNT IS A *WORKING* DAY — SATURDAY TO WEDNESDAY, NATIONAL HOLIDAYS OUT — AND THAT IS `isCallRateWorkingDay`, NEVER A SECOND RULE.** Until 2026-10-02 `exceptions.ts` had its own weaker one, `isFieldDay`, which excluded **Friday alone**: a rep at exactly 10 PM calls on each of Saturday to Wednesday — a perfect week — read 50 / 6 = 8.3 and was listed "Below the PM call rate". 🔴 **And the digest's one-day window made every FRIDAY morning a verdict on THURSDAY**, a day that holds no PM call by design, so every active territory got "Below the PM call rate · 0.0" on its manager's Telegram (and "No visit" on a holiday Thursday such as 8 October). The digest now SKIPS a day that is not a working day and reports that it did (`skippedNotAWorkingDay`), because a correct silence must be tellable from a broken one; `lastFinishedWorkingDay` returns **null** rather than inventing a date. 🔴 **An unreachable holiday calendar is SAID** (`holidaysUnread`, on the board and in the workbook): the lookup never throws, it returns `complete: false`, and a missing holiday leaves the denominator too generous — a rate reading low for a reason nobody performed. `isWorkingDay` / `RATE_WINDOW_WORKING_DAYS` in `src/lib/phoenix-crm/exceptions.ts`, pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-exceptions.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-daily-digest.test.ts`. Account — the 8.3 figure and the Friday-judges-Thursday flood: `docs/agents-owner-rules.md`, "The exceptions board and the 06:55 digest count WORKING days".
- 🔴 **BRICK COVERAGE IS KEYED BY LINE *PLUS* BRICK — a brick carries ITS OWN line's money and nothing else.** The closing's brick grain is per line and `loadClosingBrickSales` runs once per line per landed month; flattening those results to `{ brickId, areaCode, value }` drops the line, and a `salesByBrick` keyed by brick alone then sums CNS, Anti Infective and Pedia together. Two silent consequences: a covered brick showed every line's money, and a brick only ANOTHER line sells into appeared as **"Sells here, nobody on the list"** — the one row the board exists to raise, manufactured out of somebody else's geography. It hit the **default** "All lines" view of the SFE Manager, the CEO and the Super Admin. A seat with no `lineCode` reads no line's closing at all, which is the honest answer. `BrickSalesTotal.lineCode` / `BrickTerritory.lineCode`, pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-brick-coverage-report.test.ts` case 2b.
- 🔴 **`phoenix_crm_report_plan_vs_actual` IS THE SQL HALF OF THE PLAN-DAY RULE — `isPlanDaySent`, which lives in `phoenix-crm-approvals` — PLUS: A DROPPED CUSTOMER LEAVES THE DENOMINATOR.** `sent_days` counted a `returned` week as sent while `phoenix_crm_save_visit` stamps a visit on such a day `is_unplanned` — so the rep read "off the plan, waiting for your manager" and his manager read **"missed"**, about the same customer on the same day. It now applies `isPlanDaySent` / `v_on_sent_plan`: submitted or approved, or a one-day return of ANOTHER day that no acknowledged edit has since opened. 🔴 **And a customer DROPPED mid-week kept his later planned days as misses**, though the save raises `phoenix_crm:customer_dropped` for him — the rep was refused the very visit the report blamed him for not making; his days after the **Cairo** day he left are out (`status_changed_at::date` is the UTC one). Migration `20261002221947`; the header of `20261002125244` is wrong where it says replacing this report "would change nothing". Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-plan-review.test.ts` §5e, which before this **asserted the defect**.
- 🔴 **A SCREEN READING `phoenix_crm_visits` RAW COUNTS THROUGH `visitCountsInKpi`, NEVER BY COUNTING ROWS — AND IT PAGES.** The day page's AM · PM · Pharmacies counted every row while the calendar cell that OPENS it counts what `phoenix_crm_countable_visits` and `customer_validated` agree on: one rep's Tuesday read 3 on the grid and 5 on the page, and on a team that has just started the gap is EVERY visit. `visitCountsInKpi` (`unplanned-rules.ts`) is the TypeScript twin of that view plus its column, and what is held back is **reported** — `awaitingValidation`, `awaitingApproval` — never dropped. 🔴 **Separately, `loadVisitLocationReport` read each seat batch unpaged and so stopped dead at PostgREST's 1,000-row cap**, computing `total`, every flag count and the farthest distance over a truncated set; the farthest row is exactly what a cut-off drops. Every such read is a `fetchAllRowsKeyset` on `id`. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-wave2-reports.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **THE TEAM CALENDAR DRAWS EVERY MANAGER ABOVE ITS TERRITORIES AS A ROW OF THEIR OWN, AND ITS AM · PM · PHARMACIES TOTALS STAY THE REPS' ALONE** (owner, 2026-10-19, asked why district, regional and national managers were absent: *"yes, all manager levels"*). A row was a REP seat, so a manager showed only as a rep row's "Under" name. Managers come from `managersInScope` (never upward, never the CEO), only those above a territory on the grid, none in test mode, highest seat first. A manager's day is their own single visits (through `loadManagerOwnVisitDays` — the one door out of that table — whose `split` counts each by its CUSTOMER'S list: a rep customer's AM, PM or Pharmacy wins, and only a customer of his own list falls back to the half, `ownVisitBucket`) plus the double or chain visits that name them (`countsAsManagersDoubleVisit`), their approved leave, and — since 2026-10-23 — a meeting, training or travel recorded on their own seat (`loadActivityDays` reads the manager seats too), drawn as Other activity. 🔴 **`teamCalendarTotals` skips `kind: "manager"` rows** — a ride is already on the rep's row — and a manager's cell never links to the rep day page. Its cell now opens the manager's own page (next rule). Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.md`, "A manager's work, counted everywhere it is shown".
- 🔴 **A MANAGER'S CELL OPENS THE MANAGER'S OWN PAGE, `<shell>/manager-day/<seat>/<day>` IN THE SHELL THE CALENDAR WAS READ IN (`phoenixCrmDayPath`), WHICH IS ONE PLAN-AGAINST-ACTUAL TABLE — PLAN, ACTUAL, AND THE DETAIL OF EVERY VISIT IN THE ACTUAL** (the admin calendar opens `/admin/field-crm/…` with a route map the Super Admin and SFE alone get, `loadDayRoute` — owner, 2026-10-24, "Admin tab plus a map"; pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-admin-day-page.test.ts`) (owner, 2026-10-20: *"I want full details … every single detail"*, then *"show a premium table, plan vs actual vs detail per visit in actual"*; `ManagerPlanActualTable`, `buildPlanVsActual`, the same table the private calendar's manager day draws). 🔴 The verdict on a planned row is the WEEKLY REPORT'S (`computeManagerReports`), never re-derived; a week never sent is not judged; a plan that fails to load is SAID. Account: `docs/phoenix-crm-manager-plan.md`, "A manager's day: plan, actual, and the detail of every visit". Gated by the SAME `managersInScope` list the row is drawn from, refused in test mode, ride-alongs kept to territories the viewer reads; its AM · PM · Pharmacies are the cell's own `managerDayFigures`, apart, an unvalidated ride-along held out. A manager's cell opens only on a day worked — there is no missed record to enter on a manager seat.
- 🔒 **EVERY REP'S DAY PAGE CARRIES THE SAME PLAN-AGAINST-ACTUAL TABLE, EVERY VISIT IN FULL — EXACTLY AS A MANAGER'S DAY** (owner, 2026-10-21: *"make everyone's visits appear in details in private calendar and phoenix crm calendar exactly as mina farouk"*). `day-detail.ts` reads `loadRepVisitDetails` (the countable view by seat, `source: "rep"`) into `visitsInFull`, drawn by `ManagerPlanActual` above the cards: one row per visit, "On the plan" or "Not on the plan" from the visit's own `is_unplanned`, never re-derived. A rep's single reads "Alone." A failed read is SAID (`visitsInFullUnread`), never an empty table. The day's AM · PM · Pharmacies stay the cards' own — the table adds detail, never a second count.
- 🔴 **EVERY SQUARE CARRIES EVERY FIGURE ITS DAY HAS — AM, THEN PM, THEN PHARMACIES ON VIOLET — FOR A REP AND A MANAGER ALIKE; A DAY OF PHARMACIES ALONE IS THE `pharmacy` SHAPE, NEVER BLANK** (owner, 2026-10-23, replacing his 2026-09-30 "pharmacies never in the cell"). Printed apart, never added (`CellFace`, `PHARMACY_FIGURE_INK`); a pharmacy never changes an AM, PM or AM + PM shape. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-wave2-reports.test.ts`.
- 🔴 **A FAILED LEAVE READ IS SAID, AND THE TRACKING SHEET'S SPACING NOTE NEVER JUDGES A DAY NOBODY HAS LIVED.** The team calendar and the day page caught `loadApprovedLeaveDays` and discarded it, so a rep's approved vacation drew as a run of blank days — read as a week he did nothing; both now carry `leaveUnread` and print `LEAVE_UNREAD_NOTE`, as the plan report already did. And `buildTrackingSheet` was handed `month.monthEnd`, so on the 3rd of a month every customer read a 28-day silence — "then not again" about a customer seen yesterday; the window ends at **min(month end, today)**. The workbook's two off-plan columns added AM and PM, and it printed the raw brick key `sales-no-list` where the screen says "Sells here, nobody on the list" (`BRICK_STATUS_LABELS` is now the one map both read).
- 🔒 **THE REPORTS TAB HAS A MANAGER FILTER, AND THE LINES' PRODUCT MANAGERS ARE ROWS ON THE PER-MANAGER BOARD AND THE TEAM CALENDAR** (owner, 2026-10-20). `managerHrId` narrows the manager, daily-plan and brick reports to that manager's territories and everyone beneath him (`territoriesUnderManager`; outside the viewer's scope is a 403), and the export's scope line names him. The filter offers seat holders only; with no filter set, a company reader or a National Sales Manager also gets the product managers as rows. On the team calendar they are drawn for a COMPANY reader only, as `seatless` rows (`product-manager:<HR id>`) that carry their ride-alongs and leave and, since 2026-10-21, open the manager day page on that key (owner: *"Open a day page"*), and stay out of every total; none in test mode. The National Sales Manager's calendar keeps its rows as they are, field force only (owner: "Keep it as is"), so `server/team-calendar.ts` gates them on `viewer.basis === "all"` although `readsLineProductManagers` also admits him for the board. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-wave2-reports.test.ts`, `tests/phoenix-crm-rules.test.ts`.
- 🔒 **THE VISITS-PER-DAY REPORT SPLITS A MANAGER'S OWN VISITS BY NAME** (owner, 2026-10-20). `phoenix_crm_report_visits_by_day` returns `manager_hr_id`, filled ONLY where `rep_hr_id` is null (migration `20261020052000`), and `groupVisitsByRep` keys a group `seat|rep|manager`, so two managers on one territory are two rows labelled "<name> (own visits)" ("Managers' own visits" only when no id is recorded). A ride-along's manager is deliberately NOT in the key — it would split the rep's own row and change his figures. Pinned by `tests/phoenix-crm-rules.test.ts` and `tests/phoenix-crm-countable-visits.test.ts`.
