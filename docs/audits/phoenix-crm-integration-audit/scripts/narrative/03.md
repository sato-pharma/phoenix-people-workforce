# 03 — Integration Health Report

An operational health assessment of the 56 existing integrations, scored only on what was measured.

## Scoring

Six dimensions, weighted: Availability 25 · Correctness 25 · Reliability 20 · Security 15 · Performance 10 · Cost 5. Each dimension is scored 0–100 from the evidence in report 13, or left **NV (NOT VERIFIED)** when no evidence could be taken. A NOT VERIFIED dimension is removed from the score — it is never counted as a pass — and the score is re-weighted over the dimensions that were measured.

| Class | Rule |
|---|---|
| HEALTHY | Score 90 or more |
| HEALTHY WITH WARNINGS | 75–89 |
| DEGRADED | 50–74, or a measured functional failure |
| CRITICAL | Below 50 |
| NOT VERIFIED | Less than half the weighted dimensions could be measured |
| NOT APPLICABLE | Disabled, planned, deprecated or legacy — nothing to measure |

## Result

HEALTHY 14 · HEALTHY WITH WARNINGS 12 · DEGRADED 1 · CRITICAL 0 · NOT VERIFIED 24 · NOT APPLICABLE 5.

- **The one DEGRADED integration is the production scheduler (EX-03).** Three of 49 enabled jobs end their last run in a 30-second timeout and nine more run above 12 seconds (ISS-002).
- **No integration is CRITICAL or FAILED.** Production, the database, storage, the AI providers, the e-mail senders and WhatsApp all answered.
- **24 NOT VERIFIED is the honest number, not a weakness of the method.** They are integrations whose proof needs a provider console the audit could not read (Google Maps keys and bill, Sentry event flow), a billed call, a message sent, or a credential that is not on the audit machine. The constraints forbid all four.
- **Connectivity from the audit's network was uneven:** 4 of 10 connection attempts to the production and worker hosts timed out, while 10 of 10 to the database host answered (ISS-003). The same pattern was seen from both of the owner's laptops before the audit, so it is recorded as a network-path finding to investigate, not as production being down.

Latency figures are only those measured in report 13. Where nothing was measured the table says "Not measured"; no figure was estimated.
